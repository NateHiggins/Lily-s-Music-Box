# Title screen — Pull Up a Chair opens

Owner direction, 2026-11-05: **Pull Up a Chair is the title song** and the first
record of the V2 house broadcast. It is Adela Voss and Lily's own quiet song at
the Harukiya, an original composition generated by the owner with Gemini from
a prompt and lyrics written for it on 2026-11-05.

The song opens the title. When it ends, the untouched waltz follows, then its
return, then the song again; the record button offers the next record. Its
trim, -6.8 dB, levels its measured -12.0 LUFS with the waltz's -12.3 LUFS at
-6.5 dB. The waltz pair below is unchanged and keeps its own ruling.

## The cover (2026-11-06)

The title art is `assets/ui/title/pull_up_a_chair_cover_v1.png`, a 1929-style
comic cover of the night of the song: Adela at the upright piano, Lily on
string bass, Stacy behind the bar, an empty chair pulled out, under a full
moon. It replaces the waking-world hero. Owner rulings, 2026-11-06:
`title_cover_format` puts the cover whole on the left of a manila field with
the menu on the right; `title_cover_public` allows Lily's and Stacy's
likenesses in this public repository; `title_cover_stacy` approves Stacy's
drawing.

The figures were rendered alone with their own private LoRAs and composited,
in LILITH's black ink on manila with red only on Adela's hair. The lettering
was typeset, not generated. Prompts, LoRAs and working files stay outside the
repo. The shipped PNG is 1500 x 2250, SHA-256
`FD1843020D1A168265D71FA65E2A2E208FB0C54FDAF1464994BF816F5EF06710`.

Both shipped streams are 48 kHz stereo Ogg Vorbis at FFmpeg quality 8. Nothing
is excerpted, faded into a loop, pitch-corrected or layered over the other
record. The untouched original opens the title. When either complete stream
ends, the other starts from zero. `HEAR THE RETURN` and `PLAY THE ORIGINAL
MASTER` provide the same transition on demand. Per-track trims compensate for
their measured 1.53 LU loudness difference without changing the masters.

Owner correction, 2026-08-15: **the dream world is the reveal.** The title
therefore uses one record-independent waking-world hero,
`orison_grand_mundane_title_v1.png`. It makes the approved three-zone scope
grand through things the player can already encounter: the seven-storey
Orison, driving rain and roadway, a historical transit-style kiosk, the lit
Vantry Arcade, an off-hook telephone, a work order, a toolbox and a maintenance
worker crossing between them. It contains no dream geography, Tenant,
impossible architecture, supernatural figure or preview of dream play. The
fantasy is entirely composition, scale, weather and light applied to mundane
work. Selecting a record never changes the image. The menu title is the actual
project title, *Please Stay on the Line* (retitled by owner ruling 2026-10-22; Bible §VIII.5.m).

`TitleScreenTest.tscn` proves full durations, loop flags, opening selection,
single-hero invariance and alternation. `TitleScreenAudioTest.tscn` proves
actual playback and the finished-signal handoff. Production renders live in
`art/renders/title_clockwork_theme/`; the superseded first redesign remains
there as decision history.
