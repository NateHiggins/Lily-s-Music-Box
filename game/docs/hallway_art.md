# Orison Hallway Art

Eight shared-building artifacts are mounted from the basement through the
sixth floor. The collection begins as credible civic and historical décor
and becomes subtly less trustworthy with elevation.

- Basement: looping `KNOW YOUR EXIT` fire notice
- Lobby: Orison construction photograph dated 1928
- First floor: hand-colored `OUR QUEENS` neighborhood map
- Second floor: Queensboro Bridge municipal lithograph
- Third floor: rooftop residents' party photograph
- Fourth floor: abstract impossible-stair print
- Fifth floor: `LIGHTS ON` community-arts poster
- Sixth floor: `PLEASE MAKE ROOM` elevator broadside with duplicate floor 6

Each atlas quadrant is copied into an isolated runtime texture before it is
mounted. The catalog at `res://data/hallway_art.json` controls floor, room,
wall, height, and position.

The exterior main entrance also carries a shadow-casting navigation sconce
and a bronze identity plaque reading `THE ORISON / APARTMENT BUILDING /
QUEENS, N.Y. / EST. 1928`, with `REALTY MAINTENANCE` as a small diegetic
foreshadowing detail.
