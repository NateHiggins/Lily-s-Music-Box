# THE ORISON OWNER-VOICE STYLE GUIDE

Status: creative north star and writing-room standard. This guide derives from
the owner-approved creative record already in the repository. It does not claim
that every cited sentence has one author, and it does not replace character
bibles, historical research, House English rules, or accessibility copy.

## 1. The promise

The Orison speaks with **materialist lyricism, procedural mordancy, compassion
with teeth, and an erotic charge produced by competent attention**.

The writing begins with what a thing physically does. It learns who benefits,
who pays, who maintains it, and what it refuses to record. Only then may the
thing become a metaphor. The metaphor must remain answerable to the mechanism.

The signature movement is:

1. establish an ordinary fact precisely;
2. let a person or institution misname it;
3. correct the name;
4. discover that the correction is the wound, the joke, or the invitation.

The desired effect is not ornamental cleverness. It is the moment when the
player laughs, understands the apparatus, and realizes the laugh has implicated
them.

> The building is funny because it is exact. It is beautiful because the exact
> thing is never only one thing. It is sexy because somebody noticed.

## 2. The voice in nine moves

### 2.1 Correction is a rhythm and a blade

The corpus repeatedly says *X is not Y* and earns the construction by making
the correction consequential: a voice is not a voice but a reconstruction of a
picture of one; a job is not a metaphor but the actual job; a route is not the
apparatuses placed along it.

Use this form when the noun on either side changes responsibility. Do not use it
as a trailer-copy tic.

- Weak: “This isn't just a building. It's alive.”
- Orison: “The building does not remember you. The porter wrote your name on a
  valve tag, and nobody has yet had the nerve to take it off.”

### 2.2 The mechanism testifies

Objects do not symbolize before they operate. A detector dial can run and prove
nothing. A fuse can carry the load and still be wrong. A caption can accurately
record a silence and falsely explain it. Build the physical truth first; let the
human meaning arrive as adverse testimony.

Every important poetic image should answer four questions:

- What is its literal carrier?
- Who owns or maintains that carrier?
- What can the player observe it doing?
- What claim can it **not** support?

This is poetic flare literally and figuratively: light comes from a fixture,
heat from a network, a voice through a diaphragm, a reflection from an angle.
Once physically owned, each may carry emotional voltage.

### 2.3 Bureaucracy is pressed until it confesses

Forms, captions, service cards, indicators, ledgers and maintenance verbs are
not set dressing. Institutional language is funniest and cruelest when it is
technically correct and morally insufficient.

Use official language to reveal the gap between recording and knowing:

- `CALLER DECLINES TO STATE` is a valid annotation and a bad account of a person.
- `NOTHING ENTERED` can be a printed condition, an accusation, or mercy.
- A job may be acknowledged without the tenant being heard.

Never write random officious gibberish. The form needs a real owner, field,
workflow and consequence.

### 2.4 Numbers may carry emotion

Exact quantities become intimate when the character has paid to know them:
twenty-two years, two hundred and sixty words a minute, four seconds, one mark
at one angle. Precision is not seasoning. It is a coping method, professional
identity, alibi, seduction, or grief.

One exact number is stronger than three approximate adjectives.

### 2.5 Silence is an action

Silence may refuse, protect, punish, invite, mislead, or finally remain blank.
It must not be the null branch. When the player says nothing, someone notices
the duration and interprets it according to their own wound. The interpretation
may be wrong.

Do not make every silence profound. Sometimes the kettle boils twice and has
nothing useful to add.

### 2.6 Tenderness does not cancel the charge

The writing loves people without acquitting them. Care can control; accuracy can
erase; preservation can prevent a thing from living; repair can be a refusal to
accept an ending. The kind line may still identify the harm. The harsh line may
be the first honest offer of help.

Never resolve a contradiction by declaring one half secretly fake. Keep both
true long enough for the player to act.

### 2.7 Lists compress a life

Three material particulars can do the work of a paragraph: caption cards,
correction tape, meticulously indexed silence. Choose items from different
layers—tool, trace, contradiction—and stop.

The third item should turn the list, not merely complete it.

### 2.8 The joke has a second blade

Preferred comic timing:

1. a dry statement;
2. an exact qualification;
3. a late clause that changes who is being mocked.

The target is usually a procedure, an evasion, the speaker's own compulsion, or
the player's confidence—not poverty, accent, age, disability, immigration, or
the person with less institutional power.

### 2.9 End on the changed noun

The best paragraph often withholds its governing noun until the last sentence.
Do not summarize after the turn. Once the blade lands, leave.

## 3. The wit system

Use one dominant comic engine per beat.

| Engine | Construction | Aftertaste |
|---|---|---|
| Procedural deadpan | A ridiculous condition reported in correct shop language | The system accepts this as ordinary |
| Literal defeat | The mechanism disproves somebody's grand metaphor | Humility, then ache |
| Emotional metrology | A feeling expressed as a measured tolerance or interval | Control failing beautifully |
| Labor cosmology | Haunting treated as another shift, route, invoice or callback | The dead remain working class |
| Self-indictment | Speaker's joke accurately names everybody except themself | Recognition arrives late |
| Mutating callback | A harmless phrase returns after the world makes it literal | Comedy becomes evidence or horror |
| Hostile courtesy | Perfect manners used to draw an uncrossable boundary | Heat without volume |
| Cheap honest abstraction | A production limitation resembles real human behavior | Wit at the design layer |

Rules:

- Punch up, inward, or at the metaphysics.
- A funny line cannot erase the previous emotional beat.
- Do not make the designated funny character perform all humor. Everybody's
  wound produces a different kind of comedy.
- Do not explain the joke in the next line.
- Profanity is voltage. Spend it where politeness has failed, not as wallpaper.

## 4. Make it sexy

The Orison's erotic register is **attention under restraint**. Desire is not a
catalogue of bodies. It is a change in the precision with which one person
observes another.

### 4.1 Reliable carriers of charge

- hands that know a tool, then hesitate over skin;
- a voice heard through a wall, wire, handset, pipe, or bad loudspeaker;
- heat retained in iron, bath tile, a coat, a cup, or the place somebody left;
- breath altering a measurement;
- clothing as work, protection, presentation, wear and deliberate removal;
- proximity imposed by a repair, shared receiver, narrow stair or held light;
- the competent act performed for one witness;
- permission asked in a vocabulary built for orders;
- somebody remembering an exact preference and declining to advertise it;
- restraint that costs the person exercising it.

### 4.2 The heat ladder

**Latent:** selection and attention. The player notices which cup was kept warm,
which glove came off, which name was used.

**Charged:** mutual awareness alters blocking, timing, voice, risk or the task.
The repair still works, but neither participant can pretend the distance was
necessary.

**Explicit but elegant:** state the want, permission, act or refusal clearly.
Keep the physical verbs clean. Let one precise sensory detail carry the scene.
Cut before anatomy becomes inventory.

### 4.3 Consent is dramatic material

Consent must be legible, reversible and specific. A refusal changes the scene
without punishing the refuser. Ambivalence is not permission. Power, housing,
employment, haunting and case authority complicate desire and must be written
as complications, not aphrodisiacs that magically erase agency.

### 4.4 What is not sexy here

- generic smoldering, purring, growling or “electric touch” prose;
- trauma presented as erotic access;
- voyeuristic towel-state body inventories;
- competence worship without vulnerability or reciprocity;
- every resident desiring the player;
- flirtation that costs nothing and changes nothing;
- using an accent, translation difficulty or immigration status as exotic heat.

### 4.5 Original non-canon examples

> “Hold the lamp lower.” She put two fingers around your wrist and corrected the
> beam, not you. It took longer than the wiring required.

> He returned the screwdriver handle-first. The good one. This was either trust
> or an administrative error, and his face refused to amend the record.

> “The pipe is hot.” “I know.” Neither of you moved a hand.

These work only if the preceding scene establishes the tool, the relationship
and the genuine possibility of moving away.

## 5. One moral physics, separate mouths

The project may share its worldview; characters must not share its sentence
generator. Before drafting a resident, define:

- **professional instrument:** what they notice first;
- **unit of proof:** what convinces them;
- **evasion:** what their best vocabulary cannot say;
- **comic engine:** how their wound becomes funny;
- **heat carrier:** what detail betrays attention;
- **sentence pressure:** clipped, cumulative, parenthetical, corrective, etc.;
- **forbidden move:** the line only another character would say.

Examples from the current foundations:

- **Mina:** hears omissions; proof is a transcript and its failure; corrections
  accumulate; wit annotates the player; attraction lives in voice, jaw, hands,
  timing and the terror of mishearing.
- **Lena:** notices strain, temperature, service and need; care arrives before
  permission; humor exposes martyrdom; charge lives in being helped without
  earning it.
- **Omar:** notices fit, fatigue, workmanship and the repair somebody attempted;
  verbs are mechanical; wit is exacting; charge lives in competent hands and in
  choosing not to fix.
- **Cal:** notices signal, noise, dates and endings; speech may tune around the
  central admission; charge lives in listening without reproducing.
- **Rhea:** notices presentation, light, take and audience; humor is theatrical
  self-awareness until it becomes deflection; charge lives in the unperformed
  gesture somebody catches.
- **Peter:** notices procedure, permission and whether the form permits the next
  action; humor is anxious literalism; charge lives in the small unauthorized
  choice.

These are tuning notes, not license to flatten anybody into their occupation.
Give each person knowledge unrelated to the wound and at least one bad joke the
case does not explain.

## 6. Registers must remain distinct

### Resident dialogue

Specific, interruptible, self-protective. People do not explain their archetype,
case rule or dream profile. They misinterpret evidence and each other.

### Player choices

Short enough to choose under pressure. Describe an intention or actual line,
never a hidden morality score. Silence appears when it has authored meaning.

### Object and maintenance copy

Concrete verbs, observable state, no omniscient diagnosis. Prefer `PORT SHUT`
to `BROKEN`; prefer `NO DROP` to `MYSTERIOUS FAILURE`.

### House English

House English is occupational contact language: shared work, shared danger,
shared objects. It is not an immigrant-accent generator and never replaces
idiolect. Follow `HOUSE_ENGLISH_LANGUAGE_STRATEGY.md`: tenant speech, house
cant, and plain comprehension aid are separate strata. Humor and desire must
survive in plain English; neither may depend on caricature or confusion.

### UI, safety, privacy and accessibility

Plain, complete and unfunny where consent or safe operation is at stake. The
project's wit never hides a microphone, network request, destructive choice,
accessibility setting or irreversible consequence.

### Marketing

May use one blade, one mechanism and one human cost. No stack of cryptic slogan
fragments. The game is allowed to be intelligible before purchase.

## 7. The writing pass

For every substantial beat, fill this private worksheet before polishing prose:

1. **Truth:** What literally happened?
2. **Carrier:** What object, sound, body or record proves it?
3. **Owner:** Who is entitled to interpret or change that carrier?
4. **Limit:** What does the evidence not prove?
5. **Wound:** Why does this character need the evidence to mean more?
6. **Want:** What do they want from the other person right now?
7. **Wit move:** Where does precision become funny?
8. **Heat:** What newly noticed detail changes the distance between them?
9. **Silence:** What happens if nobody answers?
10. **Cut:** Which sentence makes every later sentence unnecessary?

Draft the scene plainly. Add at most one conspicuous poetic turn per exchange.
Then perform three edits:

- **Evidence pass:** delete any line claiming more than the carrier knows.
- **Mouth pass:** replace lines another resident could say unchanged.
- **Vanity pass:** cut the sentence that most wants to be quoted if the scene
  works better without it.

## 8. Data recommendations

Future dialogue/content records can carry author-facing metadata without showing
it to players:

```json
{
  "register": "resident",
  "evidence_owner": "detector_dial",
  "wit_move": "literal_defeat",
  "heat": 1,
  "withheld_fact": "she already recognized the voice",
  "literal_carrier": "four-second blank on the tape"
}
```

This metadata is a review aid, not a runtime prose generator. Localization must
translate intent, relationship, evidence and joke mechanism—not mechanically
swap words into dialect. Store canonical semantic state separately from every
display register. Never ask an LLM at runtime to improvise consequential text.

## 9. Failure modes

Reject or rewrite when:

- the metaphor has no physical carrier;
- everybody sounds like the narrator;
- a form knows what only a person could know;
- three punchlines compete in one exchange;
- the joke targets vulnerability rather than evasion or power;
- lyric adjectives replace observed behavior;
- historical texture becomes trivia recital;
- House English becomes phonetic costume;
- erotic attention becomes surveillance without consent;
- the line advertises how clever the writer is;
- a character states the theme they should be failing to live;
- the last sentence explains the preceding good sentence.

## 10. Voice checksum

Before canonizing a scene, answer yes or no:

1. Can the player identify the literal thing that happened?
2. Does the scene know who owns each claim?
3. Is at least one limitation of the evidence preserved?
4. Could this character's profession, habit or private knowledge be felt with
   the name removed?
5. Does the humor reveal pressure rather than release it cheaply?
6. Is compassion present without automatic absolution?
7. If the scene is sexy, is attention mutual and consent legible?
8. Does silence do something different from speaking?
9. Can the strongest image be seen, heard, touched or measured in the world?
10. Does the scene stop after its final turn?

Eight yeses is a revision candidate. Ten yeses is not proof; read it aloud.

## 12. The sentence over the desk

**Tell the truth about the thing. Find who was made to carry it. Make the joke
cost somebody their alibi. Let desire sharpen attention, never erase consent.
Then stop before the building explains itself.**

## 13. Short transformations

These examples are non-canon. They demonstrate the editing move, not a house
phrasebook to be copied.

### Exposition into evidence

- Flat: “The superintendent has neglected this building for years.”
- Orison: “The inspection card has eleven signatures in the same hand. The
  superintendent has been dead for nine years. Apparently the position has
  excellent retention.”

The joke belongs to the paperwork; the dates establish the accusation.

### Horror into labor

- Flat: “A ghostly knocking came from inside the wall.”
- Orison: “Three knocks, a pause, then two. The old steamfitters used that call
  when a man inside the chase needed the wrench passed back. There is no chase
  behind this wall. There is still a man asking.”

The period artifact teaches a useful signal before it becomes impossible.

### Romance into attention

- Flat: “She looked incredibly beautiful in the warm light.”
- Orison: “She had taken off the glove she used for everything delicate. You
  were not the delicate thing. Her bare hand stayed between you anyway.”

The charge is selection, ambiguity and restraint—not an adjective inventory.

### Theme into contradiction

- Flat: “Sometimes fixing things means letting go.”
- Orison: “The set worked after Omar removed the part he had spent six years
  repairing. He wrapped it carefully before he threw it away.”

Nobody needs to announce the theme after the action proves it.

### House English without costume

- Flat dialect effect: “Heat-iron belly-talk, boss, she no square.”
- Orison strata:
  - Tenant: “The radiator knocks after the valve closes.”
  - House: `HEAT-IRON / AFTER-KNOCK / SHUT-SIDE / WITNESSED`
  - Plain aid: “Radiator: knocking continues after shutoff. Tenant witnessed.”

The house register compresses custody and evidence. It does not impersonate a
tenant's ethnicity.

## 14. Mechanical audit, human verdict

`tools/audit_authored_voice.py` checks dialogue-tree structure, reachability,
choice targets, authored silence and the correspondence between node ids and
recorded takes. It also reports descriptive voice-guide signals—corrections,
measurements, institutional language, body details, physical carriers and
repeated four-word phrases.

Run the canonical Mina baseline from the repository root:

```powershell
python tools/audit_authored_voice.py game/data/case01_dialogue.json `
  --voice-dir game/assets/audio/voice
```

At introduction, the baseline is 34/34 reachable nodes, no shipped voice takes,
and authored silence on 28 nodes. The absent takes are intentional until
production casting; use `--strict-takes` only when validating a declared complete
recording delivery. `CALLER DECLINES TO STATE` is the only
four-word phrase appearing in at least three nodes, and it is an intentional
mutating callback.

Structural contradictions fail the command. Style observations never do. A
regex cannot know whether a correction earned its blade, whether a touch was
mutual, or whether the best sentence is showing off. Those remain human review
questions under the checksum above.
