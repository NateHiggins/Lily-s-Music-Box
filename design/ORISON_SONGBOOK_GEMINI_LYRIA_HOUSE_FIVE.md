## THE REJECTION CHECKLIST — one list, all five tracks

Judged on the full performance; any FAIL rejects the candidate — do
not rationalize:

1. Recognizable source melody against the attached score.
2. Correct meter and approximately the specified BPM.
3. Fully instrumental — no singing, humming or voiced artifacts.
4. Guide instrument present and subordinate.
5. Open vocal phrases — an untrained singer fits without fighting.
6. Period instrumentation only; no substitutions that break the
   ensemble.
7. No accidental modern production (synths, e-drums, sub, loudness).
8. No fake old-recording effect; no imitation of any historical
   record.
9. The native-speed performance works by itself across the whole
   form, including the ending.
10. The complete take (with a real scratch vocal — a synthetic
    singer proves nothing) rips at the track's return ratio: the
    2007 test.

## THE RETURN — true-varispeed templates (verified)

Record a disposable scratch vocal over the entire base, mix once
into one complete take, convert to 48 kHz, then:

- ×1.335 (Dreamland, Aloha ʻOe, Ain't We Got Fun):
  `ffmpeg -i take48.wav -af "asetrate=48000*1.335,aresample=48000" return_1335.wav`
- ×1.414 (Charleston, Nobody):
  `ffmpeg -i take48.wav -af "asetrate=48000*1.414,aresample=48000" return_1414.wav`

never use formant correction, never use `atempo`. Loudness-match
native and returned versions by plain gain only (measure with
`ffmpeg -i file.wav -af ebur128 -f null -`, apply `volume=XdB`).

## AUDITION LOG — 2026-08-14, and one open decision

- **Two Gemini candidates processed** (manifests beside each file in
  `art/audio/`, uncommitted with the audio): *Ribbons on the
  Banister* (old 30-second hook prompt; retired) and *Moonlight on

- **The plain base-only return underwhelmed the owner** ("just

  transformation. Two consequences follow:
  1. **The scratch-vocal test is still the real audition** — no

     distortion and a heavy bass beat on the return. Prototypes

     misreading, not a remix" — no post-speedup additions), so it
     needs an **owner ruling**: keep pure varispeed, or amend to
     varispeed **+ one authored house-rig chain** (proposed diegetic
