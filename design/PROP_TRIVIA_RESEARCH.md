# PROP TRIVIA RESEARCH — THE SERVICE WIRE COPY LEDGER

**Production research ledger — 2026-08-15.** This ledger is the source side of
`game/data/prop_service_wire.json`. It records the claim boundary behind each
field slip; it is not player-facing prose. The corresponding coverage census is
`design/PROP_SET_INTERACTION_MATRIX.md`.

## 1. Editorial law

The card is a tiny maintenance observation, not a museum caption. It must:

1. name the object the player actually touched;
2. spend one short sentence on a useful fact demonstrably available by the end
   of 1928;
3. report present condition only from the object's authoritative owner;
4. use original wording, with `STOP` as house punctuation rather than quotation;
5. omit prices, priority folklore, superlatives and exact dates unless the date
   is both useful and supported by the cited source;
6. reveal no private contents, case solution, resident diagnosis or dream rule.

The data book deliberately contains tokens such as `{leaf_state}`. I4/I5 may
replace them only from public read-only state after the physical interaction.
An unresolved token is invalid and must produce no slip. The service-wire
presenter never opens, closes, buys, repairs, saves or advances anything.

`case_object` is the one deliberately non-presentable routing template. A case
or resident owner must replace its title, period fact, condition and source ids
as one dictionary. This is how the owner ruling “resident variants come from
resident/case authority” is enforced without a generic card leaking a clue or
stereotype. It is not a fallback and the book marks it `presentable: false`.

## 2. Rights and cultural boundary

The source pages below are evidence, not copy reservoirs. No archival sentence,
illustration, trademark treatment or catalogue layout is reproduced. Patent and
museum descriptions are paraphrased down to mechanical facts. That produces a
worldwide-original text layer, but it is a design-risk record, not legal advice.

All fixed cards describe material or mechanical behavior. None assigns a
technology, literacy, taste or domestic habit to a resident because of identity.
Harukiya's culturally specific displays remain authored by Harukiya's existing
fiction owner; R041 supports only the glass-display mechanism. Any future card
that names Japanese goods, language, family history or practice requires the
same consultation gate already recorded in the project bible.

## 4. Copy audit

The JSON book contains **81 entries**: 80 presentable researched cards and one
non-presentable case-owner template. Its matrix registry contains **105 unique
non-ambient rows**: 50 OPERATE, 38 INSPECT and 17 RESIST-REFUSE. Every static
card cites at least one ledger id. Every present-condition line contains at
least one owner token; a static “everything is fine” sentence is forbidden.

Several rows intentionally share a card because they are the same object at
two provenance layers: for example the live signal-parlour cabinets and
generator `arcade_cab` records, or the live wall switches and generator
`switch` records. That is reconciliation, not duplicate coverage.

The case-bearing domestic mechanisms are the corresponding dynamic exception:
their `PossessedDomesticProp` owner supplies the complete title, fact,
condition and source list from a closed kind table. The generic `case_object`
record remains non-presentable. The returned condition may say only whether
the visible mechanism is at rest or visibly altered; ids, tells, residents and
causes are outside the copy boundary.

## 5. Deferred implementation proof

I2 writes and sources the book only. I4/I5 still owe owner binding, physical
responses and reachability. I6 must fail on:

- a registered row with neither a presentable static card nor a complete
  case/resident owner result;
- any source id missing from this ledger;
- any unresolved `{token}` at presentation time;
- a dynamic owner result with no honest title, period fact, condition or
  source id;
- a condition copied into the presenter rather than read from its owner;
- a generic fallback being used for a registered matrix row.
