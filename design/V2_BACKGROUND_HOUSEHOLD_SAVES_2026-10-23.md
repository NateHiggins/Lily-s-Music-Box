The FIFO retains every submitted snapshot and previous backup generation. At eight
pending jobs, backpressure drains synchronously to bound memory. Thread-start
failure falls back to the same synchronous transaction; that resource-exhaustion
branch has not been fault-injected. A protection failure discards jobs that have
not started. An older successful worker cannot clear a newer live-domain refusal.

settle old work first. Background notification means the live world changed; it
does not assert durability. Pending status is explicit. Abrupt termination before
settlement can lose the latest pending progress; existing crash recovery remains,
and no native atomic-replace/fsync or power-loss guarantee is added. Serialization,
owner validation/capture, ordinary non-household commits and barriers can still
stall. Queue saturation deliberately prioritizes preserving snapshots over latency.
