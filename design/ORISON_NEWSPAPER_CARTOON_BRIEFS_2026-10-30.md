# The Speaker's weekly cartoon — briefs for 52 Sundays

One cartoon for each Sunday from 1928-11-11 to 1929-11-03. The calendar gives
**52** Sundays. Each sits on the Speaker's back page, on the corner-flipped sheet
with the optional columns, six boxed ads and the foot.

All captions are **PROPOSED**. Each gag riffs on a headline from `origin/main`'s
`game/data/newspapers/`, in that Sunday's issue.

**The drawings.** The local generator drew them from these briefs. The prompts and chosen seeds are in
`art/data/newspaper_cartoon_book.json`, and `art/tools/build_cartoon_book.py` inks them. The generator keeps the
scene better than the gag, so a drawing may lose a small detail the brief names. The caption still carries the joke.

**Rules held throughout.**

- **The drawing carries no lettering at all** (RUL-008). No signs, tickets, tape,
  placards or labels with marks on them: anything that would be a word is blank.
  Any words live in the caption.
- No resident is named or drawn, and the building never appears. No dream material,
  no hum, no WORS 1610.
- Humour aims at institutions and pompous men, never at women and never at Adela.
  Deaths in the news are not gags; a week with one uses another headline.
- The telephone pun is the Speaker's habit, not the cartoonist's. It is avoided.

**The mystery's weeks are ordinary gags.** The treatment plants no clue in a
cartoon, so none is used, and **no entry carries a clue**. The weeks nearest a
chain beat are marked "chain week": B05, B06, B03, B04, B07, B02, the murder, B16,
B17, B18, B19, B20, B21, B22, B23 and B24 all fall beside one. On 1929-07-21, the day
of the wrong notice, the drawing has no roof, storm, fall or push, and the gags
elsewhere keep clear of clocks, gloves, keys, plates and envelopes.
