## Period references and editing choices

The [Science Museum Group praxinoscope record](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co8233478/le-praxinoscope)
describes Reynaud's1877 optical toy and its repeating sequence of pictures.
It informs short cycles with clearly held silhouettes, rather than continuous
motion that the mechanical squares cannot complete.
The [Library of Congress silent-animation catalogue](https://wwws.loc.gov/rr/mopic/findaid/animate.html)
identifies changing drawn faces in **The Enchanted Drawing**1900 and
**Humorous Phases of Funny Faces**1906. Those references support exaggerated
brows, a deliberate wink and a broad yawn.
[Fleischer Studios' own account](https://fleischerstudios.com/inkwell)
describes the New York studio founded in1921 and its earlier Bray work.
The urban, mechanically mischievous pantomime informs the bowler/rain and
turning gear-eyes. These are original motifs, not copied characters or frames.
The optical toy supplies an animation precedent, not a claim that the fictional
Vantry machine or bistable matrix was a normal commercial1928 object.
