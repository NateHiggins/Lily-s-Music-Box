# F01 arrival-wall object retirement audit

Decision: remove both crowded objects from production before introducing any
replacement. The house telephone, wainscot and circulation space are the
control image. A future novelty must earn its footprint against that control.

## Retired video cabinet

- Authored record: `lobby_cab`, assembly `arcade_cab`, formerly at
  `[-4.60, -9.05]`, yaw 180.
- Runtime name: `Arcade_lobby_cab`.
- It was one member of the broader signal-parlour cabinet family, not a unique
  lobby mechanism.
- Reason for retirement: the electronic/video silhouette reads later than
  1928, duplicates a family already distributed elsewhere, crowds the opening
  telephone wall and spends period credibility before it teaches anything.
- Replacement rule: never restore the row. A11 may eventually place one
  compact, source-grounded mechanical answering novelty after a clear-wall
  A/A image and route-clearance proof.

The cabinet has been removed from the source generator, both generated layout
copies and the rebuilt F01 baked geometry. Tests forbid its authored id and
production node from returning.

## Retired sales broadside

- Runtime node: `OrisonOriginalSalesBoard`, implemented by
  `lobby_orison_ad_board.gd` and formerly instantiated directly by
  `building_root.gd`.
- Source: `game/assets/ui/title/orison_original_advert_lobby_v1.png`, a
  1672 × 940 obsolete AI title tableau.
- Display treatment: crop `(438, 126, 486, 650)` mapped onto an approximately
  1.08 × 1.45 m sheet inside an approximately 1.42 × 1.89 m frame.
- What the image is: a fictional early sales advertisement containing a
  differently realized Orison, embedded in a superseded title composition. It
  is neither resident evidence nor current diegetic advertising.
- Why it failed: the crop magnified incidental raster art into a dominant wall
  object, inherited obsolete building design, and competed with the telephone
  and real notice board. Scaling it again cannot repair those semantic faults.

Production construction is retired. The source asset and prop class remain
temporarily as concept provenance so removal is reversible and references can
be audited. Archive or delete them only in a later exact-reference migration;
do not silently repurpose the bitmap. Current marketing art is a separate
artifact and must not be substituted into the lobby without a diegetic brief.

## Acceptance evidence

Before A11 production placement:

1. capture the same west-wall camera with both objects absent;
2. prove the telephone remains reachable and the route adds no body;
3. compare negative space and hierarchy against PHONE-B;
4. retain the cleared wall as the A/A control for every novelty proposal.

After A11 production placement:

1. the machine must be one object, not a restored arcade family;
2. its period mechanism must read without a caption;
3. its ordinary operation must alter only its own visible state;
4. the clear wall must remain the quantitative and aesthetic control;
5. if the wall is better empty, the machine stays unplaced and the storyline
   moves to another measured location.
