# Dream temporal biology

Owner ruling, 2026-08-24: the Dream being has perceptual access to time as a
fourth-dimensional extent and to exotic higher-dimensional space. In trying to
understand human reality, she observes living and made systems across eras and
scales, with a strong bias toward microscopic biology, then grows sensory life
from those observations.

This is the controlled source of Dream anachronism. It is not permission for
arbitrary future props, exposition, time travel or a collapse of the waking
1927 setting.

## What she can perceive, and what she cannot understand

She can encounter before, during and after as neighbouring structure. A nerve
ending, a radio diaphragm, a printed circuit, a fungal front and a sensor not
yet invented may all be equally available specimens. Higher-dimensional access
also lets her observe interiors, cross-sections and relationships that a human
camera could never share in one view.

Access is not comprehension. Human beings build meaning from sequence,
attention, embodiment, memory and consent. She initially reads correlation as
anatomy and recurrence as need. She may see the shape of an outcome without
understanding why a person chose it, whether it was wanted, or which moment
made it emotionally true. This is why her attempts to communicate can be
tender, intelligent and catastrophically inappropriate.

Therefore she is neither omniscient nor prophetic in play:

- she cannot state an unresolved case truth for the player;
- she cannot reveal a future choice as settled fact;
- she cannot repair history, rewind a case or mutate committed save facts;
- she cannot use later technology as a correct solution to an earlier human
  problem;
- she can foreshadow shapes, rhythms and relationships whose meaning remains
  unavailable until the player earns it in ordinary sequence.

The dramatic model is **complete access, incomplete interpretation**.

## The specimen-to-organ rule

Dream design follows three steps:

1. **Observation.** Name a real sensory, regulatory or communication mechanism
   from life or technology at some scale and time.
2. **Misreading.** Name what the being gets wrong about its human purpose,
   chronology or boundary.
3. **Embodiment.** Grow one organ, tissue behavior or spatial rule that tests
   that interpretation through contact.

The result is never a museum replica. A printed circuit does not appear as a
motherboard in a 1927 room. It is re-grown as vascular routing under translucent
flesh. A phased array becomes a field of cilia that turns in delayed groups. A
tomographic scan becomes a room that exposes mutually impossible sections of
one body. A microphone becomes a pressure membrane that grows mouths because
the being mistakes receiving for answering.

Strong source families include:

| Observed specimen | Dream interpretation |
|---|---|
| dendrite seeking a synapse | a palp approaches, pauses, samples and secretes |
| cilia and stereocilia | fixed sensory anatomy folds, sorts gradients and answers |
| immune synapse | apparent pursuit becomes recognition, rejection or tolerance |
| vesicle transport / exocytosis | secretion crosses a narrow cleft |
| quorum sensing | local organs change likelihood without becoming one animation |
| mycelium / slime mould | architecture routes pressure and remembers passage as stain |
| axon and myelin | long insulated signal paths with exposed relay nodes |
| cochlea / tympanum | rooms separate frequency, pressure and delayed response |
| radiolaria / diatoms | gold and crystal become load-bearing biomineral skeletons |
| circuits / antenna arrays / fibre optics | organic vascular, ciliary and photic routing |
| microscopy / tomography | incompatible scales and sections coexist |

Microscopic reference supplies function and strangeness, not clinical drabness.
The ruled cool/sexy visual doctrine still applies: organs are generous enough
to read, sensuous in motion, richly incarnated and compositionally beautiful.
No specimen is made literal at unreadable real scale, and no grey laboratory
aesthetic replaces the wine, flesh, gold, crystal and case-substance language.

## Where anachronism is allowed

### Waking Orison

The historical baseline remains strict. An ordinary object is 1927,
second-hand and repaired. The Rule of Signal still controls the building's
sanctioned communications divergence. A future appliance, interface, brand,
screen or clean science-fiction prop may not appear merely because the being
has observed one.

Encroachment may leave bounded evidence of mistranslation on an existing
historical object: impossible vascular routing beneath Bakelite, a service
contact behaving like a ciliary bed, or one mechanism briefly presenting an
inside/outside relation it cannot physically possess. The historical object
and its normal maintenance story must remain legible beneath the event.

### Dream and release prints

The Dream may combine observations from any era, but every anachronistic form
must pass the specimen-to-organ rule. No contemporary UI, readable future
label, brand silhouette or generic cyberpunk dressing. The player sees an
organism trying to grow an understanding, not a prop department mixing eras.

Release prints may place incompatible temporal states beside one another, but
they cannot rewrite the resolved case. They are the being's interpretation of
what was integrated, not a preview of the next resident.

## Relevance to the existing six-case grammar

- **Mina:** captions and print systems can become cellular labelling,
  transcription and membranes that over-identify what they touch. They cannot
  reveal an unearned human meaning.
- **Peter:** ledgers, stamps and routing can become replication, proofreading
  and checkpoint tissue. Future databases may inform the anatomy, never appear
  as screens or improve the truth into certainty.
- **Juno:** her approved delayed feedback partitions are the clearest first
  temporal specimen. Tympanic membranes, cochlear delay, radio feedback and
  later signal arrays can coexist as one listening organ that mistakes every
  received channel for a request to answer. Her truth remains **connection
  requires an open channel**.
- **Mae:** simultaneous object histories can appear as growth rings, repaired
  fractures and incompatible material ages, without choosing one history for
  her or changing committed facts.
- **Cal:** held time can borrow biological oscillators, delay lines and later
  recording systems, but may not become a literal time loop.
- **Omar:** repair tissue may compare an object's many possible repaired states
  and keep trying all of them. The pathos is failed interpretation, not a
  futuristic tool that solves the unrepairable.

These are surface, anatomy, behavior and spatial-language extensions. They do
not reopen the approved case order, dream profiles, maze ownership, pursuit,
hazards, saves or waking case loops.

## Gameplay consequence

The player's relationship to Dream life should mature through three readings:

1. **Observe / avoid.** An organ's premature response looks predatory or
   supernatural because its purpose is unknown.
2. **Recognize.** Maintenance and conversation teach the player to identify
   gradients, contacts, delays, inhibition and repair attempts.
3. **Communicate.** The player deliberately supplies a legible stimulus and
   receives a bounded anatomical answer, eventually understanding that the
   ecosystem is trying to sense and converse.

Waking maintenance supports this without becoming a lore lecture. Historical
mechanisms teach pressure, continuity, timing, regulation, contact and flow;
Dream organs later reuse those verbs at biological and impossible scales. A
successful maintenance action never proves a supernatural theory, and a Dream
response never replaces the human conversation required to resolve a case.

## Production gates

Every proposed anachronistic Dream element must record:

1. the real specimen and approximate scale/era observed;
2. the organelle function borrowed;
3. the being's specific misinterpretation;
4. the existing owner that expresses it;
5. the visible anatomical or spatial response;
6. why it does not reveal an unresolved case truth;
7. why it adds no save, time-travel or waking-technology owner.

Visual claims require production render proof and a frozen A/A control. A
future-looking silhouette without this account is rejected as arbitrary
anachronism. A sound, shader or animation that merely decorates the scene
without changing how the player reads an organ's sensing is not sufficient.
