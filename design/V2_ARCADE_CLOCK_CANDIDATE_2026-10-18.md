# Arcade pendant clock native candidate — 2026-10-18

Evidence class: **INERT**. Offline, uninstalled candidate. This document and
its Blender views grant no spatial, runtime, human or photoreal acceptance.

The current source clock is a solid black case with two intersecting blank
face blocks and two rectangular brass bands. The candidate keeps the same
five source envelopes and the original suspension rod and lower finial axes.
It replaces the blank form with a hollow four-face case, pierced side sheets,
recessed enamel dials, turned brass rims, closed minute strokes, slotted screw
heads, separate hands and actual thin glazing stock. The fitted sheets now
close the cap seams; an earlier candidate left a visible opening there.

All eight hands have independent native nodes and spindle origins. The GLB
exports them at their twelve-o'clock reference; the Blender review poses them
at 10:10. This is preparation for a shared campaign-time presenter, not a
claim that the installed game clock moves or can be serviced already.
Numerals remain a future Label3D layer; no lettering is baked into any texture
or exported font mesh.

The reviewed kit is **art/reviews/v2_arcade_clock_20261018/native05/**. Its
editable native file, generated GLB, deterministic plan and source/map hashes
are retained. The model has 13 material draws and 37,424 triangles. Stock
checks require closed outward solids; actual mesh rays inspect the suspension
bearings, empty interior, recessed dial backs and closed upper/lower seams.
Exported normal frames, metre UVs and tangent derivatives pass. Four tools
tests refuse changed source faces, caps, materials or supports and check the
four outward frames and eight independent hand clearances.

The first brass stock looked like wood grain and the first enamel was too
coarse. Both were replaced with existing registered, calibrated service brass
and appliance enamel for this controlled native comparison. Ring topology
also biased the reflected highlight; analytic lathe normals now remain
outward on every actual triangle and remove that azimuth bias. Rejected views
and original Blender logs are preserved under **diagnostics/**.

All six current views were visually reviewed. The before images are the exact
source geometry under the same controlled mapped finishes, not current Godot
screenshots. New native UV/PBR/mipmap and composed appearance qualification
remain pending. The mapped clear-water stock is explicitly a glazing preview
placeholder; purpose-made glazing and actual optical calibration are still
required. The case finish also needs its composed-light review.

The candidate was prepared while the unrelated trim-qualification batch was
running. It writes no game assets, game scripts, runtime JSON, catalogue or
production Blender family. Frozen game/surface/physical input hashes remain
the qualification boundary; installing this kit will require its own reviewed
batch with the other pending arcade finish work.
