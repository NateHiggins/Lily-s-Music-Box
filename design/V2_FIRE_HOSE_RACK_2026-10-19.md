## Change

The original two primitive pins and rectangular bar did not follow the ten
actual hose folds. Their visual payloads are retired on their original nodes.
The Blender-built successor has two screwed mounting brackets, a common steel
shaft, retained end nut and ten separate curved brass carrier fingers with
rounded hose bearings. Each finger follows its actual retained crown position,
including both stored fold arrangements. The brackets bear on the actual case
back. All eight production stations receive the successor through the existing
V2 native installer; original maintenance and save owners remain authoritative.

The form is informed by the common-rod, individual-arm construction in the
[1915 Stillwaggon hose-rack patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1150075A/en).
This is an authored adaptation to the existing cabinet and folds, not an exact
replica or a certification of historical hose dimensions. No reference image
is baked into the model. The five mesh templates use authored UVs and existing
registered iron/brass PBR finishes, including active normals and loaded mips.

## Diagnostics retained

Default mesh compression collapsed small UV derivatives and stretched some
charts by up to 72.8 percent. The measured rack exception disables compression
for this asset through the existing narrow policy; it does not change mipmaps
or global mesh defaults. Imported chart stretch is then below 0.14 percent.

Even after that correction, Godot's convenience TriangleMesh conversion rounded
the bearing's 19.94996 mm top to 19.9 mm. Original **rack_probe1** records the
actual rounded vertices. The successor now builds its concave collision shapes
directly from imported vertex/index arrays, and the rack contact harness reads
those same actual imported surfaces. Native geometry and the contact tolerance
were not changed. Original failed runs are preserved. **rack_stock3** contains
a test-only unnamed-child lookup error and was explicitly stopped as this
task's own failed run; the corrected original **rack_stock4** completed normally.
