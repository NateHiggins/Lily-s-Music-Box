# Dream biology reference dossier - 2026-09-19

Evidence class: **INERT - REFERENCE DOSSIER (not canon; promotes nothing)**

Image sources and artistic interpretations of alien and microscopic life,
compiled for the Dream's organs. It is a reading list for whoever designs,
models or textures Dream anatomy, organised by the organs the project has
already ruled. It proposes nothing: every organ, specimen and style law cited
here is already canon in the documents named below, and anything this dossier
suggests is marked as a recommendation.

## Where the Dream's biology is ruled

- `design/DREAM_TEMPORAL_BIOLOGY.md`: the specimen-to-organ rule. The being
  observes a real mechanism at some scale and era, misreads its human purpose,
  and grows one organ that tests the misreading. Microscopic reference
  supplies function and strangeness, never clinical drabness.
- `design/DREAM_TEMPORAL_SPECIMEN_LEDGER.md`: which real specimen each landed
  organ is grown from, at what scale and era. This dossier uses its organ list.
- `design/DREAM_ECOLOGY_ARCHITECTURE.md`: three resolutions of one biology
  (hero limb, margin palps, critters).
- `design/DREAM_FAUNA_BRIEF.md` V3-V14: silhouette vocabulary, cloisonné
  shader doctrine, colour tokens (aubergine-black ground, wine dark-life,
  lamp-woken gold, emerald/carnelian/lapis stones) and the V14 material
  boundary (procedure is the skeleton, AI-derived plates supply substance).
- `design/SIX_INCARNATIONS.md`: the six case readings of the one body.

## How to use these references (binding rules, not suggestions)

1. **Form only.** A reference informs form, proportion, part layout, motion
   and palette. It is never traced, projected, photobashed, baked into a
   texture, used as a displacement or normal source, or fed to an image
   generator as an input or style image; its bytes are never committed or
   shipped (RUL-007). Cite it by URL.
2. **Licence tells you how freely you may look, not whether you may copy.**
   Public-domain and CC0 plates may be downloaded, studied side by side and
   pasted into internal review sheets; CC BY and CC BY-SA need attribution on
   any sheet that is shared; everything marked COPYRIGHT_REFERENCE_ONLY is for
   looking at in its own page, never saved into the repository or a review
   sheet. Nothing here licenses copying into a shipped asset, whatever its
   licence.
3. **AI plates (V14).** AI-derived plates supply substance only (membrane,
   tissue, enamel, alloy, lacquer) inside procedural regions, and no image
   from this dossier is ever handed to a generator (rule 1). *Recommendation,
   not a ruling:* describe substance in words ("translucent wine membrane
   over gold filigree") and do not name a living artist or studio in a
   prompt, so no plate carries anyone's signature look.
4. **Era discipline.** A specimen's era is when the being observed it, not
   when the Orison may show it. The Dream may draw on any era; the waking
   world keeps the strict 1927 era discipline the ledger names. The
   "period-safe" column marks works made or described in 1927 or earlier,
   which could also appear in the waking world as books, plates or
   instruments, subject to the Rule of Signal.
5. **The look is the project's, not the source's.** Every organ must still
   read as cloisonné jewellery made alive under the three-light test. A grey
   SEM image supplies structure; the wine, gold and enamel come from the
   style laws. The speculative-design canon below is studied for body logic
   and motion, and several entries carry a "do not imitate" caution because
   their look is famous and owned.

## How this was compiled

Six research lanes searched the open web in parallel: historical scientific
illustration, modern open-licensed imagery, scientific and microscopy art,
speculative and alien design, fine art and ornament, and real organisms that
read as alien. A separate checker then re-opened every URL and confirmed or
corrected its licence against the page's own rights statement. A
completeness critic compared the result with the organ list, and a further
researcher filled the gaps it named, with its own checker. Status in the
tables says which sources were fully confirmed, which were corrected, and
which could not have their licence confirmed. Sources the checker could not
reach are listed apart at the end, not cited.

## Gaps no verified source covers yet

- hero limb: the only footage of vesicle transport or exocytosis is two reference-only animations ([73], [63]); there is no free-licence footage.
- hero limb and branches: no footage of a dendrite or growth cone growing and searching. The Physarum time-lapse [42] is only a stand-in.
- margin palps: no vibrissa (mammal whisker) specimen. The whisker's stereocilia half rests only on cochlea images ([27], [4]).
- margin palps: no specimen of a tactile ribbon organ. The nearest are the polyclad frilled margin [33] and Klimt [80].
- seam grazer: no motion reference of a flatworm gliding or rippling its margin.
- crystal listener: no statocyst specimen, only a fish otolith [47]. Nothing period-safe shows an otolith or statocyst, and there is no motion reference.
- fold crab: no footage of an arthropod walking or folding, and no free-licence photograph of a real walking limb. Only Haeckel's plate [48] covers it.
- encrusting flora: the only lichen is the Haeckel plate [53], with no free-licence photograph, and there is no colony-growth time-lapse.
- grazer armour: no footage of a radula rasping or chiton plates flexing.
- wine anemone: no footage of a tentacle crown swaying, feeding or retracting.
- ribbon display: no photograph or footage of a living nemertean, only McIntosh's plate [46].
- ocellus lens: no insect or spider ocellus (simple eye) specimen, and no motion reference for eyes.
- incarnations: no tree-ring specimen. The otolith growth bands [47] are the only annual-ring stand-in.
- incarnations: no source dedicated to biological oscillators. The chromatophore flicker [34] is a stand-in.
- incarnations: DNA proofreading has no free-licence or period-safe image, only the reference-only animation [65].
- incarnations: no tympanum (eardrum) specimen. Only the cochlea is covered.
- hazards: nothing shows immune rejection itself (graft or transplant rejection). The T-cell attack in [24] stands in.
- cellular surface: no image or footage of a microorganism swarm, such as bacterial swarming.
- living gold: no period-safe diatom or coccolith image, and no footage of biominerals growing.
- living architecture: no period-safe mycelium image.
