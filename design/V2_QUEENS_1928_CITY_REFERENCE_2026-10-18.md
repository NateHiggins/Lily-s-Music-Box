Evidence class: **INERT**. Historical research and implementation direction;
neither a runtime receipt nor human appearance acceptance.

The Bible dates the opening to November 10, 1928 and places the Orison in
Queens without fixing a surveyed neighborhood. Use western Queens as a visual

  pages 1-3 and 6: the bank opened in May 1927; the report records nearby
  five-story apartments, factories, elevated transit and a second office

  **2015** photograph. Its surviving upper form is useful; later storefronts,
  air conditioners and altered details must not be copied into 1928.
- [NYC LPC, Hunters Point, LP-0450](https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/lpc/lp/0450.pdf),
  pages 3-6: earlier brick houses and apartments provide narrow vertical
  windows, cornices, stoops and a mixture of two-, three- and four-story
  frontage. This is a domestic reference, not a template for every factory.

  catalogue date and power-station caption verified. The full image request

  dates that building to 1928-30. A finished Chrysler crown is unsuitable.

  construction to 1930-31. Neither completed silhouette belongs in this scene.
  [NYC housing historic documentation](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/hpd/downloads/pdfs/services/nycha-section-106-programmatic-agreement.pdf)
  reproduces a 1939 Queensbridge opening notice: its superblock form is later.

### Direction for subsequent city polish

- Preserve the established low and middle roofline with occasional commercial
  accents. Give ordinary buildings coherent vertical walls, roof parapets and
  purpose-built cornices; repeated shrinking floor plates are not a default.
- Keep domestic narrow sash bays distinct from broad industrial glazing.
  Express material changes at real construction joints, lintels, sills and
  party walls rather than with arbitrary bands across a stepped mass.
- Compose open sky and industrial gaps deliberately. Rail-yard openness is a
  reference for appropriate views, not permission to leave exposed scenery
  backs, unsupported ground or holes in a playable street edge.
- Treat a clock tower as an occasional landmark. The bank's documented tan
  masonry and vertical window grouping offer a specific vocabulary; avoid
  distributing landmark crowns over every background building.
- Use atmospheric distance to separate local Queens fabric from any Manhattan
  backdrop. A real landmark requires a plausible bearing and a November 1928
  construction-state check before inclusion.

These directions are consistent with the installed horizon's varied flat-roof
stocks and separate cornices. The source owner remains
**art/data/city_horizon/source_plan.json** and its Blender builder
**art/blender/scripts/build_city_horizon.py**. Further geometry changes require
native review and composed views under the normal batch workflow. References
remain external form research; no archive photograph is a game texture.

### Proposed background pipeline, to measure rather than assume

Reuse the installed horizon source plan, builder, stock and catalogue first.
The brief's OSM/GIS → modular facade generation → atlas/trim allocation →
block-based glTF/culling chain is an authoring option, not a requirement for new
add-ons or a replacement world generator. Preserve editable footprints and
deterministic seeds. Array/instance facade bays and scatter supported water
tanks, chimneys, fire escapes and aerials with varied, intentional distributions;
do not let a Poisson scatter put heavy machinery on openings or unsupported roofs.
The proposed **3.0 × 3.5 m** bay is a trial module, not a floor-height override.

Consider full near-field stock, simpler middle-distance blocks and distant
clusters/impostors with weather-aware transitions. The suggested **0–50 / 50–200 /
200+ m** bands and **300+ m** skyline card are starting points; rooftop views
and the 0.1-degree telescope can invalidate an ordinary FPS distance-only LOD.
Preserve depth/parallax, silhouette and landmark detail through that viewing path.
Any horizon card is an original Blender render, never a reference-photo bake.

Treat one **2048-square** brick/stone/metal/window atlas and **1,500 draws** as
unmeasured example targets, not guarantees. Allocate texture space by projected
detail and actual physical scale; retain distinct registered substance recipes,
correct PBR channels, UVs, normal mapping and loaded mipmaps. Measure draw calls,
transparent overlap, shader cost, memory, shadow work and frame-time tails.
Interior mapping/POM is optional where it reads convincingly and costs less
than existing geometry; it is not free. Avoid a costly per-pixel treatment on
every distant window by default.

Evaluate block-sized exports, measured HLOD/culling and shadow simplification
without arbitrary global cutoffs. Lightmaps or baked illumination must retain
the campaign's changing sun, night, storms and weather response; do not bake a
permanent noon into a year-long atmosphere. Use the existing coal-smog/weather
owner to blend distance, not fog to conceal broken playable geometry or disable
visibility arbitrarily. Review near/far overlap, dusk/night, storm/clear, rooftop
and telescope views, then compare repeated occupied approach routes before
accepting a replacement. Existing visual/spatial/save authorities remain intact.

### Coordinate and height-data contract

Use a **1,000 × 1,000 m study footprint** centered on the authored Orison site;
the supplied 256 × 256 grid is a blockout starting point, not required final
topology. Establish one scene-to-ASL offset. For example, choosing street ASL
**+6 m** while retaining the playable street at **scene Y=0** gives water near
**scene Y=-6 m**. Record the chosen datum in the existing city source, with the
installed compass/coordinate conversion, rather than move the building, anchors,
traffic, doors or campaign owners to fit an illustrative graph. Confirm east/west
from the installed rig; do not assume a Godot Z sign is geographic north.

The proposed 0/85/140/180 gray swatches are conceptual class labels, not a linear
metre encoding: 0→0 m, 85→3 m, 140→6 m and 180→12 m cannot all share one scale.
Author elevations in metres; if a height image is useful, encode normalized
height in a genuine **16-bit** channel with explicit minimum/maximum elevations,
linear data sampling and documented datum. Do not bake an 8-bit quantization
and then merely save it in a 16-bit container. Bridges, elevated roads and tracks
cannot be represented as a single-valued terrain height field without filling
their underpasses; export them as separate native assemblies.
