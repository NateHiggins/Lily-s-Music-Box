# Suspect chains and the papers' plot: writers' room, 31 October

Two writers pitched six suspect chains and 67 newspaper edits. A third attacked both. This is what
survived. 54 of the edits are in the papers. The owner ruled that the player may wrongly suspect
residents, and that amendment is in the treatment. The Prices, Peter and the three kept details are
**built as first-draft threads**. The four men are not, because their chain needs section 4.2.

## 1. Three chains, and what they hold

The rules for all of them:

- The game never says "suspect". The player does the suspecting, and nothing blocks on it.
- No clearing uses 21:01 to 03:02. A clearing is a dated paper from outside the gap, and it clears
  only what it clears.
- No existing thread gains a requirement. Each chain opens on any one of several flags, so it can
  start at any point and be skipped.
- A chain never rests on anyone's sex, race, class, immigration or illness. The two chains that
  couldn't meet this are in section 2.

## 2. Cut, and why, so nobody pitches them again

- **Rhea: the wax voice.** Her failing voice becomes a secret fraud, on the one thread canon says
  must never read as guilt, and "a surface on her" beside a trans lead is a bad echo. The clearing is
  a letter sent the morning after, which is Mae's own trick. It also breaks **rs_p3**, and wax
  can't be spliced.
- **Cam and Lena: every hinge.** It makes the conclusive carrier a fake-out, on the one working family
  whose whole hinge is suspect. It ties Lena's silk to the ribbon work the Lilith design says is never
  named or explained.
- **Nadia and Omar: the dark was made.** Held, not dead. Omar knows the part is a 1912 fake and works
  on it Fridays, and the 19th is a Friday, which makes his "Six hours is bad work" a lie or negligence
  in Adela's death. The clearing also fails: the back order ships after the 19th, so it is cover. Bring
  it back only with an answer to both.

## 3. The papers

54 edits are applied: 34 Record, 20 Speaker. They replace filler in place, so the front pages, N1 to
N16 and the treatment's quoted items are untouched. The threads a careful reader can compare:

- **The drawer.** Lamps set in one afternoon (24, 26 Nov). Rusk moves to make photographers give up
  negatives, then withdraws unopposed (16, 24 Jan). The Borden Avenue crossing gates are refused twice,
  then pass 16 to 0 in six minutes (15 Feb, 14 and 29 Mar). Orr fits fire stairs early (22 May). Then,
  after her death: Stroud resigns (26 Jul), Boxes 61 and 74 hunt prints at a rising reward, the gates

- **Her life.** The Speaker's feud with her window (12 Nov, 2 Feb), Holmes Airport and her one plate

## 4. Decisions for the owner

1. **Does the player get to suspect residents?** Ruled yes, 31 October. The treatment's section 3 now says
   the player may wrongly suspect a resident, the game never does, and each is cleared only for what its
   evidence clears.
2. **A thread for the four men.** The 31 October ruling says they have "no access and no thread". This
   chain needs "one outside thread, which clears them". Its papers read fine either way.
3. **New names now in print, as canon unless cut:** Alderman Tobias Rusk, Alderman Gerald Mott, Mr. Silas
   Orr, Mr. Warren Stroud, Box 61 and Box 74, Block 418 Lot 22 (the deed's schedule should cite it), Inspector
   Farrow with "The Fast Clock" and "The Second Key", Captain Doran, Magistrate Lerner, the crossing gates.
4. **Her window cards.** The 12 Nov and 2 Feb items quote proposed window cards: THE HAT CAN MANAGE and
   SUPPER WAS NOT THE POINT. Change them together if the cards change.

## 5. Cost if built

What was built, and what the chains still lean on:

- **Built.** A thread can open on any one of several clues (`requires_any`). The newspaper panel grants
  **m_c_gloved_lady**, and the table in `companion_witness.gd` takes more papers. The route book's entries are
  lines from Cam. Sacha's sighting replaces a timed placement of Noel, because residents talk only through a
  knock. Five typed companion lines enter a theory or answer a wrong distrust, and suspect nobody.
- **Still unbuilt.** Most entries on **carriers.json** have no runtime, including **m_notebook_spine** and
  **m_glove_scrap**. So Jonah's page and the glove clearing reach the player only through the F9 playtest panel.
  Sacha's sighting, Noel's reports, Evelyn, Peter and Cam all open in ordinary play.
- **Checked.** The mystery checker walks reachability over the real data. Hours and dates are held by the wiring
  test's gates, and the chain threads all open from the 20th, after the gap. The pitch's 20,000-order run was on
  the six-chain draft and wasn't repeated.

The red-team's checks are limited too: Stroud's post, a Register's "term run, no instrument" and Block and
Lot usage are plausible, not verified. They are alt-universe facts in a world that has already diverged.
