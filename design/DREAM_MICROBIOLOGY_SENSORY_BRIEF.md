# Dream microbiology — light, vibration and scaled-up cellular response

Owner-directed research and integration brief, 2026-08-25.

## Purpose

The Dream organism should look less like a collection of fantasy animals and
more like real microscopic phenomena made room-sized, sensuous and legible.
The fiction remains higher-dimensional: these are organs of one being whose
model of life draws from many scales and times at once. The *behavioral
grammar*, however, should be recognizably cellular. Light becomes ion flux,
adaptation and motor asymmetry. Sound becomes strain, shear, pressure and
membrane tension. Communication becomes finite-speed electrochemical waves,
vesicle transfer and locally synchronized cilia.

This brief adds no claim that the Dream has completed a waking case loop. It
does not reopen approved irradiance, lamp, organelle ownership, fauna batching,
lifecycle, pursuit, hazard, or save decisions.

## Research ruling

### What is well supported

1. **Microbial light response is direct sensory–motor chemistry.** Sensory
   rhodopsins and channelrhodopsins convert photons into ion currents; those
   currents alter flagellar or ciliary beating. In *Chlamydomonas*, light can
   change the two flagella unequally, steering the cell, while a sufficiently
   abrupt or strong stimulus produces a photophobic reversal rather than a
   graceful turn. The response adapts over time.

2. **Direction can be discovered by scanning, not by having an eye.** A
   pigment-shaded receptor or the cell body acting as a lens converts rotation
   or side-to-side casting into a periodic brightness signal. A one-pixel
   receptor becomes directional because the whole body moves.

3. **Cilia coordinate locally.** Adjacent cilia can synchronize through
   hydrodynamic coupling and form metachronal travelling waves. Increased load
   or viscosity changes beat frequency. The carpet should therefore organize
   from neighbour coupling, not receive one perfectly simultaneous animation.

4. **Mechanical disturbance can become electrical and chemical response.**
   Membrane tension opens mechanosensitive channels. Yeast vacuoles can release
   calcium after osmotic/mechanical shock. Mechanically stimulated
   dinoflagellates convert surface strain into a rapid proton-mediated blue
   flash, with a threshold, millisecond latency, saturation and depletion.

5. **Cell communities carry waves.** *Bacillus subtilis* biofilms propagate
   potassium-mediated membrane-potential waves that coordinate metabolism and
   can attract motile cells of more than one species. This is excellent real
   precedent for the landed shared signal bed and delayed neighbour response.

6. **Biofilm anatomy is functional architecture.** Growth stress buckles
   biofilms into wrinkles; channels beneath those wrinkles transport liquid.
   Localized death can help initiate buckling. Membrane blebs and extracellular
   vesicles carry material away from cells. Stains, folds and secretions should
   therefore read as plumbing and messaging, not decorative slime.

### What must remain qualified

Some laboratory studies report that acoustic vibration changes bacterial
metabolism, biofilm patterning, fungal radial growth or sporulation. The effect
depends strongly on organism, substrate, amplitude, duration and experimental
geometry; several proposed mechanisms remain unresolved. Most such changes
occur over hours or days, not on every audible beat.

Therefore the Dream does **not hear like an animal** and does not dance to the
soundtrack. Its canonical input is registered physical disturbance—surface
motion, compression, fluid shear, pressure impulse and sustained vibration.
Frequency-specific colony patterning is a slow accumulated effect. This keeps
the fiction fascinating without presenting uncertain microbial acoustics as a
settled universal sense.

## The sensory grammar

Every response follows the same causal order:

`physical stimulus → local membrane event → finite-speed chemical/electrical
relay → motor or metabolic response → adaptation/refractory recovery`

Never jump directly from “the player made a sound” to “every creature turns.”
The visible delay is the biological detail.

### Four time bands

| Band | Biological inspiration | Dream-scale read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–120 ms | channel opening, photoshock, scintillon flash | a local membrane pinch; one cold flash or cilium reversal |
| 0.12–2 s | calcium propagation, flagellar beat asymmetry | a travelling band followed by a local turn, arrest or opening |
| 2–15 s | adaptation, receptor recovery, neighbour recruitment | response decays; nearby tissue answers at different times |
| 15–150 s | biofilm metabolism, growth bias, lifecycle consequence | ethermoss redistributes, budding axis changes, secretion or senescence becomes more likely |

The first two bands make an interaction feel alive. The latter two stop it
feeling like a particle effect.

## Light integration

### L1 — distinguish level, direction and change

The existing irradiance scalar remains authoritative, but receptors interpret
three different facts:

- **level:** current local irradiance;
- **direction:** lamp vector relative to a shaded receptor, cilium field or
  body axis;
- **step:** current irradiance minus a short adapted memory.

A slow brightening invites positive scanning or metabolic opening. A sudden
step above threshold produces photoshock: asymmetric ciliary arrest/reversal,
a brief membrane depolarization band and withdrawal. Sustained illumination
adapts instead of holding the organ in permanent alarm. Excessive light may
reverse the sign from approach to avoidance.

### L2 — make directional sensing require motion

Eyespots, receptor plaques and crystal listeners do not solve the lamp vector
instantaneously. They yaw, roll, cast or pulse once, sampling light as their
pigment shield alternately exposes and shades the receptor. The measured
oscillation selects the next orientation. This should be especially visible in
Ribbonettes, crystal listeners, margin cilia and the modelled hero's ocular
assembly.

### L3 — use the approved three-state irradiance language

- **darkness:** quiet membrane potential—thin bounded contour activity and
  occasional internal ion flicker, not frozen black tissue;
- **oblique light:** translucent receptor layers, pigment shields, wet
  membranes and travelling cytoplasmic bands;
- **full exposure:** structural density, compartment boundaries and mineral
  plumbing; no generic bloom wash.

The response may recolour an existing emissive/dark-glow budget, but creates no
`Light3D`. A mechanoluminescent flash is a bounded surface event and must obey
the established dark-glow ordering. It does not illuminate the room or become
a gameplay flashlight.

### L4 — adaptation and refractory rules

Unreliable-lamp flicker must not machine-gun the organism. Each receptor keeps
one bounded adapted-light value and one refractory timer in its existing
owner. Repeated pulses inside the refractory window produce diminishing
responses. Darkness restores sensitivity gradually. Case profile hue and
surface language may alter the appearance, never the threshold logic.

## Vibration and sound integration

### V1 — semantic mechanics, never audio feedback

Do not analyze the Master bus, microphone waveform or `AudioStreamPlayer`
output to drive rules. Playback is presentation; authoritative gameplay events
are physics. Doors, footsteps, switches, machinery, impacts, speech at close
range and maintenance actions may publish a bounded **mechanical impulse** at
the real contact position. The floor or wall is the source that transduces a
footfall, so player footsteps do not require a new biological source class.

The existing `DreamEcologyDirector` signal ring is the preferred bounded seam.
Add a mechanical family and a compact carrier class—impulse, scrape, sustained
hum—without creating a second director. Mechanical packets never seize global
attention and never change pursuit hearing. Receptors decide whether substrate,
distance, morphology and refractory state make the packet meaningful.

### V2 — local response shapes

- **impulse / foot plant:** a compression dimple, rapid calcium-colour band,
  then cilia lean away or brace;
- **scrape / door / tool:** a directional wave that walks along the contacted
  surface and recruits adjacent palps in order;
- **steady machinery:** cilia phase-lock locally while individual beat
  frequency drops under load; ethermoss growth bias accumulates at standing
  nodes over tens of seconds;
- **voice nearby:** broad weak pressure modulation may synchronize exposed
  cilia, but semantic words are not understood through this seam;
- **violent strain:** one thresholded scintillon-like flash, saturation and a
  refractory dark interval—not a continuous equalizer.

### V3 — sound output is sonification of cellular work

The player hears the scaled organism because the Dream makes microscopic work
room-sized. This is an artistic sonification, not the claim that isolated
bacteria make these airborne sounds.

Use one pooled positional response layer per existing room/encroachment owner,
never a player per organelle:

| Cellular event | Sound language |
| --- | --- |
| channel opening | sparse wet electrical tick with no tonal tail |
| calcium/potassium relay | travelling granular hiss, moving through space |
| ciliary metachrony | soft combed friction whose phase moves laterally |
| vesicle secretion | close membrane tack/release, small and moist |
| photoshock | inhaled reversal transient; no animal cry |
| mechanoluminescent threshold | very short cold glass/water snap, followed by refractory silence |
| sustained ethermoss work | filtered porous breathing tied to flux, not a lung cadence |

Author new recorded/processed sources into `PropAudio` deliberately. Missing
keys remain silence plus warning. Do not generate placeholder oscillators in
production, and do not let sound playback feed the mechanical input seam.

## Rendering and FX integration

### R1 — cellular detail hierarchy

At normal play distance, each family needs three readable scales:

1. **silhouette:** cilium carpet, vesicle cluster, wrinkle, vacuole or receptor
   shield;
2. **mesoscale flow:** one or two slow cytoplasmic/perfusion bands crossing the
   anatomy;
3. **microdetail:** puncta, granules, membrane pores and mineral channels that
   appear under oblique/full light.

Do not cover everything in uniform noise. Real microscopy is compelling
because compartments differ: membrane edge, cytoplasm, vacuole, granule,
channel and extracellular matrix each carry a different motion and optical
response.

### R2 — finite-speed fields on existing surfaces

Electrical/calcium waves should travel from a real packet origin across
existing world-space coordinates. The leading edge is narrow; tissue behind it
briefly changes membrane tension, perfusion or ciliary phase and then recovers.
Neighbour owners answer only when the front reaches them. This visually
reinforces the already-proved secretion → adoption → recognition → fauna →
neighbour causal order.

### R3 — vesicles without particle soup

Secretion forms as one or a few membrane blebs: bulge, neck, release, drift to
the addressed surface, collapse into it. Implement with existing batched
instance or shader capacity where available. No free-floating GPU particle
swarm, collision body or collectible is implied. At distance, a moving punctum
inside the surface band is sufficient.

### R4 — biofilm wrinkles are circulation

Ethermoss and death stains gain branching raised wrinkles with darker channels
beneath. The network follows food/ether transport and accumulated strain;
localized death may seed a buckle. A stain should slowly become more organized
after death—an empty vascular map—rather than merely dry or fade. This extends
the approved beautiful/disturbing memory doctrine.

### R5 — no universal response

Morphology determines sensing:

- ciliated tissue changes beat phase and direction;
- crystal/receptor tissue scans and refracts;
- ethermoss changes flux and long-term growth axis;
- vesicular/secretion tissue blebs and transfers;
- architecture carries slow potassium-like waves;
- the hero combines receptor scanning, asymmetric motor response and
  dendrite-like seeking;
- pursuers/hazards interpret the same physics through their existing fairness
  and danger owners, never through a new global command.

uniforms for shared adaptation and derive per-instance variation from existing
phase, morphology and flags.

## Production sequence

### MBIO-1 — light step and adaptation proof

Start with one landed margin cilium carpet and one fauna receptor family.
Implement directional scanning, asymmetric beat response, photoshock threshold,
adaptation and refractory recovery using the current irradiance owner. This is
the highest-value first slice because the lamp already supplies a production
input and the visual result is immediately judgeable.

Required proof: production root; lamp-off, slow beam entry, abrupt switch-on,
sustained beam and repeated-flicker sequence; fixed camera; A/A control;
receptor response must diminish during the refractory sequence; zero new draw,
light, collision and save fact.

### MBIO-2 — bounded mechanical packet seam

Extend the existing signal ring with mechanical family/carrier data. Publish
only from authoritative physical events. Prove impulse locality, finite-speed
relay, recipient selectivity, refractory behavior, deterministic eviction and
no global attention/pursuit/case/save mutation.

### MBIO-3 — metachronal and calcium animation

Let nearby cilia self-organize from phase offsets; make load slow the beat and
make a mechanical or light step reverse/arrest locally before recovery. Carry
one finite-speed calcium/potassium band through existing fauna, palp and
architecture materials. No simultaneous whole-room pulse.

### MBIO-4 — vesicle, wrinkle and long response

Add bounded membrane blebs for addressed secretion and make ethermoss/death
memory organize into transport wrinkles. Sustained vibration may bias growth
or sporulation only after accumulated exposure; it must not twitch to music.

### MBIO-5 — pooled cellular sonification

Curate a tiny recorded source palette, implement one pooled positional response
layer per active room, and map accepted cellular events to it. Prove voice
count, attenuation, no audio-feedback loop, no per-agent players and silence on
unknown asset keys.

### MBIO-6 — integrated production encounter

One production-root sequence: enter in darkness, scan with the lamp, cause one
photoshock, let adaptation settle, cross the room with footfall impulses, hold
near a working cilium field, witness a finite-speed reply and later see a
wrinkle/vesicle consequence. The sequence must remain observation and
communication, not a new combat rule or completed waking case loop.

## Proof and performance gates

- Only one Godot instance, through `tools/run_godot_serial.ps1`, 60-second cap.
- Forward+ production-root renders for every visual claim, with local A/A
  controls because irradiance and architecture have a live noise floor.
- Measure input → membrane event → relay → response timestamps; no same-frame
  remote answer.
- Record node, draw, material, light, collision, audio-voice and signal-ring
  ceilings before and after.
- Compatibility on-wire decode for any packed-channel change.
- Lamp flicker/repeated vibration must show adaptation or refractory decline.
- Audio-off and headless runs must preserve identical biological state.
- No `RealityState`, case record, pursuit rule or hazard fairness mutation.
