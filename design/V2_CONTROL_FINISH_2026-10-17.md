## Problem and result

still resolves through an old marbled library plate, while weighted corner
normals tilt nominally flat brass and resin fronts by up to 13.48 degrees.
Mapped, normal-off and constant-finish renders isolate material variation
from geometry shading. A different texture cannot straighten a bent shading
normal, and a global strength increase would obscure that distinction.

The native exporter now accepts explicit planar axes for manufactured stock.
Eligible cap/bevel fans anchor to their actual flat face; turned skins retain
their existing interpolation. The two annunciator assemblies and the rebuilt wall switch opt in.
Twelve of nineteen exported partitions change normals. Expanded physical
triangles, UV derivatives and all spring morph positions remain byte-identical.
Native front bias falls below 0.02 degrees; the adjoining bevel tangent stays
below 0.028 degrees. Rebuilding the unanchored normals on the same meshes
reproduces the 13-degree failure as a negative control.

recipe with quiet filler/flow colour, independent roughness and authored
submicron relief. Its three primary maps are 512 pixels across a 40 mm tile,
replacing the 1024-pixel library plate and its stale 350 mm visual-lock scale.
Other aliases still load legacy maps; no aggregate VRAM saving is claimed.
Normal maps derive from the metre height field; this is authored calibration,
not a measured historical sample. Physical cap travel and geometry are not
textured into the maps. No photographic pixels, marks or lighting are baked.

The worked-brass recipe keeps its physical microrelief while reducing cloudy
pigment and roughness contrast. Native flat stock and cap bevels retain actual
geometry, rather than receiving painted highlights or generic dirt masks.
The keys use the calibrated resin normal and roughness values without the
old arbitrary attenuation/multiplication. Other 82 runtime material records
remain identical. One visual lock is retired; fourteen others remain.

the established roughness/alpha tuple layout. It stays out of the existing
authoring-only JSON mirror instead of adding unread runtime fields. Actual
renderer tests compare all 83 materials' metallic, roughness, alpha and normal
