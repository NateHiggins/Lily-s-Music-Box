## Historical basis and character interpretation

The [1920 Hoosier manufacturer pamphlet record](https://indianamemory.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/collection/p16066coll3/id/418)
and [1924 cabinet/flour-bin patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1496121A/en)
support cabinet/worktable preparation rather than a modern continuous fitted
kitchen. The pamphlet's cataloguing description was read; its illustrations
were not inspected. [The 1920 detachable drain-board patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1327039A/en)
and [1910 sink/washtub-cover patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US964857A/en)
establish period use of removable or convertible sink-side surfaces. These
are national historical references, not proof that a particular Queens
household owned the exact new design. The new stock is an adaptation.

[The Tenement Museum's household breakfast account](https://www.tenement.org/blog/waking-up-at-97-orchard-street/)
informs modest, varied household equipment. Its examples span several dates;
later examples are not treated as specific 1928 evidence. Reference photographs
were not downloaded, projected, baked or committed. The Bible's kitchen-era
and material rules govern: wood, enamel and repairable iron; no stainless or
aluminium. Habits below are design interpretations of established character

## Surface and contact method

The owning Blender builder reuses the established closed-stock and metre-chart
pipeline, exports corrected tangent handedness and checks every final bevelled
solid for positive volume and manifold edges. Wood tops have separate planks
and cross-grain breadboard ends; painted/enamel designs retain their timber
substrate, rolled edges and turned legs. Repair straps and pegs are geometry.
The four existing registered PBR substances are **oak_work_surface**,
**door_paint**, **enamel_appliance** and **iron_blackened**. No new material key
or legacy exemption was introduced. Imported derivatives and active authored
UVs are measured; loaded albedo, roughness and normal maps require complete
mip chains. The enamel bowl has a real cavity and separate wall collision
segments rather than a single solid convex cap.

Each table is fitted to four actual finished-floor bearings. The six sink
boards use the native rolled lip; its sloping normals are expected and are
checked separately from flat-floor normals at the unchanged 40-micrometre
position tolerance. Actual support rays distinguish a resting pin from an
unrelated nearby tool. A board carries its supported pin as one swept group.
Released tools fall under gravity to sampled native bearing faces. Cached
stationary supports avoid repeated ray work; moving or deleted supports
invalidate that cache. Saved airborne poses resume falling; held state,
velocities and support references are never serialized.
