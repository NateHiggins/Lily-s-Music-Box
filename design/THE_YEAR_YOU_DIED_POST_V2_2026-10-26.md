# Please Stay on the Line — The Year You Died

Evidence class: **INERT**. Owner-directed post-V2 creative goal and sequencing
brief, 2026-10-26. Not an implementation receipt, current feature claim, or
permission to substitute new scope for finishing V2.

**Game type:** Nonlinear Temporal Forensic Mystery.
**Tagline:** You have 365 days to solve your own murder. You may investigate them in any order.

The owner describes the tone as CSI meets Doctor Who, by way of cosmic horror
and a detective disadvantaged by already being dead. Queens, 1928 remains the
setting. Death grants access to any moment in the year surrounding the player's
murder, without a helpful instruction manual. Time itself becomes the
investigation map. The decisive question is **when should I look?**

## The investigation

Discoveries can supply **where, when and why**. The player may find an object
in December, trace its manufacture in March and theft in August, and determine
how it reached November's murder scene. Physical evidence, testimony, weather,
newspapers, construction, architecture, schedules and seasonal vegetation can
establish or destroy alibis. Statements must be comparable across a suspect's
past and future, as well as against other witnesses.

No prescribed mission sequence or mandatory revelation order: a player may
learn an answer before discovering its question. Permit visits before crimes,
after disappearances and to later publications that explain earlier events.
A body present before its stated death, a formerly empty room, an earlier tenant
who denies living there, and later records omitting the apartment are observations
to reconcile, not instructions to force a predetermined solution. Reality may
be an unreliable witness. One room may be occupied, disturbed, sealed by an
infection or geometrically impossible at different times; reuse the compact
building instead of expanding the geographical world.

The larger arc begins as an apparently conventional murder, connects incidents
across the year, and reveals that some happened because the player investigated.
The death is an essential component of the conspiracy's machinery. The eventual
question is what preventing it would do, rather than simply naming a killer.
Preserve that arc without imposing an order on discovery.

## Calendar, evidence and companion

Treat the evidence graph and calendar as two views of the same investigation.
A clue exposes relevant moments in its history; a date exposes theories that
can be tested there. Preserve provenance and distinguish **fact, inference,
contradiction and unsupported accusation**. Record the observed timestamp,
claimed event timestamp, source identity and corrections rather than flattening
contradictory accounts into a single invented truth.

The existing free-floating mechanical companion carries the interface: date
readout, printed evidence reports, and its folding keyboard for questions,
cross-references and chronological reconstructions. Continue its literal,
bureaucratic, technically confident and caring voice. Answers must remain useful
and subordinate to witnessed evidence and validated world state. Do not replace
its physical interface with a conventional dialogue wheel or omniscient chatbot.

## Temporal engineering constraint

**Never simulate all 8,760 hours in sequence.** Use deterministic, event-driven
temporal states, continuous date/time selection and authored causal dependencies.
Materialize a location from validated events and its requested timestamp. Run
local physical mechanisms while present; do not replay a year's physics to visit
a room. Require repeatable forward, backward and out-of-order reconstruction,
bounded caches and save/load equivalence. Choose the exact causal/branching model
when authoring the first chain; the pitch does not settle paradox mechanics.

Keep the weather/calendar, resident, reading, inventory, relationship, work-order,
case and persistence owners authoritative. A newspaper is available only once
published at the visited time; forecasts keep their issue times and historical
editions/corrections retain their provenance. Reading a future edition during a
legitimate future visit can create a remembered observation, not omniscience for
past NPCs. Keep player knowledge distinct from the world's state at the selected
time; possession and object identity require explicit temporal rules, not copies
of inventory items or assertions made by generated dialogue.

## First milestone after the V2 prerequisite

Build one authored room/evidence chain across several dates, including a clue
found out of order, a contradictory witness, a weather or publication alibi,
companion cross-reference, and save/load after returning to an earlier date.
Qualify traversal orders and knowledge boundaries before scaling to the whole
year. This is the first verification slice of the big goal, not a reduction of
the owner's nonlinear creative vision. Current technical checks never substitute
for the owner's judgment of mystery, humor, atmosphere and feel.
