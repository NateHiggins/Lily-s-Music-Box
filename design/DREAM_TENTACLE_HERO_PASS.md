# DREAM TENTACLE — CANONICAL HERO PASS (owner, 2026-08-22)

*Executed against three references held together: the in-engine screenshot
(spatial canon), the concept/animation sheet (visual and motion canon), and
the implementation report (technical-state canon). The concept sheet is a
TARGET, not inspiration.*

## The problem
The engineering underneath the creature is now more sophisticated than its
visible screen read. In the current gameplay screenshot the immediate read
is still approximately **giant glossy pink-purple tube + glowing gold
eye/object + two smooth gold stripes**. That is far below the canonical
target. This pass is therefore NOT feature accumulation — it is a
readability, animation, lighting and composition pass designed to EXPOSE
the systems already built. Every expensive anatomical feature must earn
pixels on screen.

## The scale hierarchy (features must not vanish where they matter)
- **5 m** — impossible silhouette
- **2–3 m** — eye + gold skeleton + tactile distal anatomy
- **1 m** — lids + cilia + veins + gold roots + suckers
- **macro** — pores + wet film + crystal interiors + micro-mineralization

At gameplay distance, with nothing explained, the player should perceive:
dense living purple flesh rather than gel; substantial anatomical mass and
changing cross-section; an eye physically buried in the organism; three
visibly alien lid systems; active sensory cilia; irregular gold that
appears grown through the flesh; crystalline intermediary organs; subtle
mechanical articulation; suckers and tactile specialization toward the
distal end; internal vascular pressure; contact deformation; dimensional
instability; curiosity rather than aggression.

## 1. Macro silhouette first
Regional identities along the 1.6 m class, in real geometry, not normals:
broad muscular **root** embedded in the membrane; a genuine anatomical
**eye station** swelling (not the same tube with an inset eye — increase
shoulder/brow/socket mass until the silhouette visibly changes); a
compressed/flattened **transitional section**; a **ribbed/mineralized**
region where gold growth and flesh tension alter the cross-section; a more
dexterous **distal third**; a visibly specialized, sucker-rich **sensory
club**. The eye should follow root → sensory/ocular mass → articulated
shaft → tactile tip.

## 2. Remove the clean gold-stripe read
The smooth circumferential gold lines read as painted decoration. Break
them into broken skeletal crescents, asymmetrical plates, partial rings,
dendritic roots, crystal-filled joints, branching gold veins, articulated
mineral knuckles, and structures that disappear beneath flesh and emerge
elsewhere. A band should sometimes thicken into a load-bearing plate,
split into branches, disappear under skin, emerge as crystal, terminate
asymmetrically, or mechanically articulate. At every gold/flesh boundary
show flesh displacement, a raised tissue lip, compression wrinkles, local
vascular response, tiny mineral roots, moisture accumulation and contact
shadow. No major gold feature may look painted on.

## 3. Make gold mechanics visible during normal motion
Small enough to stay plausible, large enough to perceive: adjacent plates
shift against each other; a strut telescopes 1–3 mm; a crystal joint
rotates fractionally; a plate settles as flesh contracts; orbit supports
tense before globe motion; distal gold aligns during investigation. Tied
to the shared biological state — no random wiggle. *There is a living
mechanical skeleton underneath this thing.*

## 4. The eye must read as embedded from gameplay distance
Do not enlarge the globe. Increase the visual authority of the whole
ocular station to **2–3× the apparent visual area of the naked globe**
using folds, gold, cilia, socket depth and surrounding mass. From normal
distance: purple brow mass overlapping the globe, lower cushioning fold,
asymmetric lateral socket, true darkness inside the orbit, gold supports
emerging from tissue, cilia silhouette, and some lid anatomy while open.

## 5. The three lids must be visible before they blink
At full open: the dorsal lid leaves a visible thick violet overhang over a
small portion of the globe; the ventral/lateral lid remains an asymmetric
muscular fold wrapping partway round the lower/side orbit; the nictitating
membrane is mostly withdrawn but keeps a tiny pearlescent/gold-filament
edge in its recess. A still must contain evidence that three different
closure systems exist. The blink runs: cilia sense → orbital gold
pre-tenses → membrane begins its diagonal sweep → dorsal lid follows →
ventral/lateral rotates up and around → three layers overlap briefly →
flesh lids reopen asymmetrically → membrane lingers → membrane retracts →
pupil reacquires → gold relaxes. Never synchronized, never human.

## 6. Cilia as a hero silhouette feature
No urchin spines. Eighteen differentiated, elegantly directional cilia in
the three classes, making an irregular unmistakable sensory silhouette:
swept curves, clustered directions, varied lengths, asymmetric gaps,
graceful arcs, several longer hero cilia, smaller supporting elements.
Strange eyelashes and whiskers, not sea urchin. The chain **stimulus →
cilia orient → orbital gold tenses → lids anticipate → eye rotates** must
be consciously perceptible at least once in the review animation.

## 7. Flesh: deeper and heavier
Keep the pitted detail; differentiate wet surface film / epidermal flesh /
deep vascular tissue / compressed anatomy. Convex masses bruised
rose-violet; medium flesh deep magenta-violet; hollows aubergine
approaching black; thin stretched regions warm crimson-magenta transport
under the flashlight; compressed gold sockets locally darker congestion.
Effectively no transparency: light entering a few millimetres of dense
living tissue, never translucent plastic.

## 8. Split wetness from flesh
Concentrate the film at eyelid margins, sucker fields, deep creases, gold
sockets, contact regions, the underside and the membrane root; leave
broader dorsal flesh and muscular high spots satin. At grazing angles tiny
wet highlights skate independently across otherwise dense matte flesh.
This surface/depth separation is critical.

## 9. Make the vascular pulse physical
Synchronize crimson brightening, 1–3 mm local tissue expansion, increased
vein relief, a subtle SSS rise, wet-highlight deformation, tiny
neighbouring gold displacement and crystal-core propagation. The pulse
visibly travels root → distal; one pulse must be easy to follow in the
review asset.

## 10. Crystal organs read without transparency
At least one growth large enough to read in the hero footage: real faceted
geometry, dark occluded interior, local-space fracture planes, inclusions,
an internal pulse carrier, a rare precise glint, partial gold growth
around and through it. The glint is a reward for camera + flashlight +
facet alignment, not a constant pulse. Crystals are punctuation.

## 11. The distal tip is the tactile star
Highest sucker density, strongest small-scale articulation, tactile
pad/club specialization, independent fine motion, delicate curl, subtle
papilla change, limited mineralization that supports rather than dominates
touch. Movement hierarchy, obvious in motion: root massive and delayed;
eye station stable and observant; mid-limb muscular follow-through; distal
third dexterous; sensory club fine-motor.

## 12. The canonical six-beat animation
**1 BULGE** (~1–1.5 s) the wall does not open; the encroachment tightens,
perfuses, grows glossy under tension, shows internal gold light, bulges.
**2 EMERGENCE** (2–3 s) not linear extrusion: the club searches BEHIND the
membrane, presses at one place, withdraws, presses elsewhere, finds a
dimensional soft spot; the membrane stretches over the tip, gold and
crystal glimpsed through thin tissue, the tip emerges, the membrane clings
and releases progressively rather than popping; the limb follows with
weight and delayed elasticity. *Something learning how to enter our
dimensional slice.* **3 SEEK** the tip lifts, 5–7 Hz tremor at the sensory
tip only, cilia sample, the eye begins opening, the gold settles, one
vascular pulse moves forward — it studies the room, not the player. **Owner
ruling, 2026-08-24: this seeking is an attempt to communicate, with dendritic
synapse formation as its movement inspiration.** The distal club makes local
micro-protrusions toward several candidate sites, dwells, secretes/samples,
then retracts and reconsiders before choosing contact; the massive proximal
body follows late. It is not a predator lunge, a pathfinding sweep or generic
search AI. At a chosen site it hovers across a narrow temporary "synaptic
cleft," stabilizes sequentially with cilia and suckers, sends an
electrochemical pulse and transfers a secretion. The ordinary surface cannot
answer in the creature's language, so the existing living-field conversion is
the material consequence of a sincere but dimensionally and biologically
wrong communication attempt; it remains owned by the shared LivingField and
creates no new gameplay or save owner.
**4 CARESS** fast then slowing inside ~10 cm, a hover, first sucker
compresses, neighbours engage sequentially, distal tissue flattens, the tip
traces a slow figure-eight, the last third curls an edge, gold adapts, the
eye watches the contact; conversion follows but stays secondary to the
physical caress. **5 FLINCH** cilia detect first, the eye snaps attention
without cartoon speed, the gold orbit locks, the distal limb contracts, the
mid-limb follows, the root barely moves, suckers release in a wave, a
defensive curl holds — surprise, not terror; then curiosity wins. **6
WITHDRAW** materially different from emergence: the gold contracts in
sequence, distal suckers release last, the limb slides through, the
membrane grips anatomy, large segments disappear impossibly fast once
embedded, the eye keeps looking as long as geometry allows, lids close only
near the end, the longest cilia are among the last visible features, the
membrane seals without a conventional hole — leaving transformed material
behind.

## 13. Two functional hyperdimensional events in this animation
**A — PHASE SLICE during SEEK**: during a controlled curl one narrow
section shows a slightly different VALID pose for several frames; the
surrounding limb stays stable, the alternate pose is smooth not noisy, gold
and crystal stay coherent inside the displaced slice, and it recombines
seamlessly. **B — OCCLUSION / REFLECTION DISAGREEMENT during CARESS**: a
reflection containing a subtly different version (extra gold anatomy,
different curvature, more ocular complexity, greater continuation) — or, if
impractical in this room, a restrained impossible occlusion: a gold/crystal
echo faintly perceptible through a surface that should fully occlude it.
Brief. No glitch noise, no RGB splitting, no digital aesthetic.

## 14. Halos stay off
`TENTACLE_HALOS=0` for this pass. Do not use the old halos to compensate
for weak eye readability. The body must earn the angelic read first.

## 22. Acceptance rule: camera truth overrides implementation pride
If a feature exists in code but cannot be perceived in the canonical
screenshots or video, **it does not yet count.** Not "the socket is
technically recessed" — show the globe buried in flesh. Not "the gold
articulates" — show one piece shifting against another under the
flashlight. Not "there are three lids" — give a blink no terrestrial
animal could perform. Not "the cilia respond" — make us see them notice
the player before the eye does. Not "the flesh has SSS" — make light
disappear into dense purple meat and bloom crimson through a thin fold.
Not "it is hyperdimensional" — make one visual event occur that
conventional three-dimensional anatomy cannot explain.

## Final canon target
A massive violet limb extruded through living architecture; dense
pressurized flesh flexing over internal anatomy; irregular gold skeletal
growth physically embedded through the tissue; crystalline organs carrying
pulses between flesh and metal; a deeply buried intelligent eye supported
by an asymmetric living orbit; three incompatible eyelids; sensory lashes
that notice us before the eye does; a distal tactile organ delicately
tasting ordinary matter; and occasional spatial events proving the visible
body is only a three-dimensional cross-section of something much larger.
Sensual because it is tactile, attentive, graceful, luxuriant, responsive
and physically convincing. Horrifying because every convincing biological
detail makes the impossible details harder to dismiss. Not a tentacle with
interesting shaders — **an alien body whose anatomy has laws.**
