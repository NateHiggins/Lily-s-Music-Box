# Dream organelle communication

Owner ruling, 2026-08-24: every Dream entity is an organelle or tissue
expression of one larger hyperdimensional being. Its parts seek contact because
they are trying to communicate. The model is dendritic: probing, recognition,
electrical excitation or inhibition, secretion across a cleft, transport,
repair and resource allocation. Local agency is local regulation, not a
separate creature, faction or predator.

## Owner-order rulings

1. Existing fauna trophic behaviour is preserved but predator/consumed naming
   must be reframed as allocation, reclamation and transport. No numbers or art
   change follows from the semantic correction alone.
2. The first executable signal slice uses the already-wired Blender hero,
   margin and critter owners. The shelved procedural limb does not return by
   implication.
3. Pursuers and hazards are organelles in canon, but do not enter this runtime
   seam until maze/encroachment ecologies actually coexist. Canon is not a
   license for speculative cross-owner plumbing.
4. Rare whole-body attention remains the response to a meaningful player
   world modification behind its 22-second gate. Ordinary organelle packets
   can never seize it.

## Transient packet contract

`DreamEcologyDirector` owns a fixed ring of 32 reused dictionaries. A packet
has exactly these fields:

`live, src_id, src_class, function, at, radius, strength, family, sign, born,
life, affinity`

Source classes cover hero and procedural limbs, palp, branch, cilia, fauna,
architecture, incarnation and hazard. Functions cover probe, recognize,
pulse, secrete, repair, transport, allocate, reject and inhibit. Chemical
families are electric, secretion, mineral, vascular and alarm. `sign` carries
excitation/inhibition.

The director only stores, expires, spatially queries and counts packets. It
does not match classes, route meaning or command a response. Receptors own
affinity and interpretation. The ring prefers expired storage and otherwise
overwrites the ring head, which is the oldest live slot. It has its own
monotonic clock, no `RealityState` key and no save representation.

## DO-2 first slice

1. A hero-limb contact emits a positive `SECRETE / SECRETION` packet.
2. At the margin's existing 0.18-second social cadence, the nearest idle palp
   inside it adopts the contact point and probes.
3. Only the palp's real contact value crossing 0.5 emits one positive
   `RECOGNIZE / ELECTRIC` packet. Held contact cannot repeat it.
4. Fauna with sociability above 0.45 interpret recognition by turning toward
   its origin and presenting sensory structures. Lower-social fauna ignore it.
5. The recognizing palp conducts the answer to neighbouring tissue after
   0.45 seconds. This local turn excludes the source and never invokes global
   attention.

The order is secretion < target adoption < contact/recognition < fauna
presentation < delayed neighbour orientation. The focused production-owner
test proves the packet's exact shape, ordering, one-shot edge, bounded
eviction, low-social non-response, no attention seizure and no persistence
keys. The staged Forward+ sequence is at
`art/renders/dream_organelle_signal/README.md`.
