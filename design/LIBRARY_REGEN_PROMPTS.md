# LIBRARY REGENERATION PROMPTS (task #45)

The last non-photographic material family. Fifteen sets under
`art/textures/library/`, script-generated by `tools/surface_library.py`.
Audited 2026-08-06 by albedo standard deviation plus eyeball.

**Keep, do not regenerate:** `book_brown`, `book_burgundy`, `book_green`,
`book_navy`, `book_ochre`, `book_teal` (real period covers with gilt
rules and label panels), `wallpaper_old` (proper acanthus, correctly
aged). These are good and a regeneration would lose ground.

**Regenerate:** the eight below.

## Flow

1. Generate; save under the exact stem given, in
   `art/textures/ai_sources/`.
2. Add a `SLOTS` entry in `art/tools/ingest_material_sources.py` with
   the coverage stated here — it writes into `ai_materials/` and
   repoints `catalog_mapping.json` automatically.
3. Run the ingest, rebuild, retire the old `library/` directory.

**Coverage is not negotiable.** Each figure below is the existing
`meters_per_tile` from that set's `asset.json`. Change it and the scale
shifts under everything already placed.

## 8. `art_canvas_sheet.png` — a SHEET, not a texture

**Read this before generating.** `art` is unit-mapped: every quad shows
the *whole* texture, so all 67 framed pictures in the building are
currently the same painting. Regenerating it as one image gives a nicer
painting sixty-seven times.

It needs a **3×3 sheet**, sampled a cell per frame by UV window — the
same mechanism as the stair treads and the mail cards. This is a
COMPOSITION: it must **not** be seamless, and it must skip the ingest's
square-crop and tiling blend (add it to `COMPOSITION` in
`ingest_material_sources.py`).

```

prewar Queens apartment house, arranged in a 3x3 grid, each cell one
complete picture filling its cell edge to edge with no frame, no mount
and no border. The nine are, in order: a dim landscape in oils gone
brown with varnish; a faded botanical print of a single fern; a
black-and-white family photograph, faces small and indistinct; a cheap
seascape print with visible halftone dots; an amateur still life of
fruit, slightly wrong in perspective; a foxed map of a coastline; an
abstract in muted ochre and grey; a portrait photograph of a woman from
the shoulders up, formal and stiff; a religious print in soft blues.
Every cell muted, desaturated, dulled by decades of low light and dust,
nothing bright or contemporary. No text, no legible writing, no
signature, no watermark, no logo. Flat even diffuse lighting, straight-on
orthographic view, no shadows, no glare, no reflections on glass, base
color map only. High resolution, production-ready. NOT seamless - each
cell is a discrete picture.
```
