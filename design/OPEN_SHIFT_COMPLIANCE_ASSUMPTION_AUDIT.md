## First bounded correction

The 2B service round is the first open-shift slice. `WorkOrders`, radiator,
boiler, inventory, residents, case, dream, and house clock retain authority.
An `OpenShiftSituation` record observes concrete events and house-clock time,
coordinates a porter compensation path, and stores consequence/residue. It
must never mark the radiator repaired, close a job, resolve a case, or request
a dream on the player's behalf.

Objective-shaped presentation outside this slice remains audited debt. Removing
it globally would exceed this milestone and risk unrelated authored systems.
