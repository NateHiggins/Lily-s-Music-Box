# Full-length hose and taller rack study

Evidence class: **INERT**. Uninstalled native study, not runtime, surface or
completion evidence. No game asset, mechanism or maintenance fact is replaced.

The cabinet's stated 50 feet requires 15.24 m of actual continuous stock.
The earlier two-bank study reached that length but crowded the cabinet with
a high crossover and a second set of short folds. The new single-bank study
uses thirteen taller crowns, twenty-six legs, the original nozzle inlet and
the original upper hose port. Its solved long-leg length is about 540 mm.
The source polyline measures 15.24 m, including both transitions and returns.
This is a construction measurement, not a deployed-length qualification.

The final resting study moves the bank 4 mm right to leave room for a properly
retained shaft head. The shaft's plain middle is lengthened while its original
head, shoulder and thread pitch remain. Thirteen original native carriers
support the crowns at 680 mm local height. All 65 bearing rays pass the
existing 100-micrometre tolerance; the maximum gap is 53.525 micrometres.
All 65 deliberately lowered-carrier checks detect the missing support.
Eight rear-bracket bearing rays meet the original steel backplate. Native
shaft/carrier and shaft/nut tests find no crossing pairs; the shaft head
clears the left coating by 1.05 mm. Support strength is not certified.

The resting hose mesh is closed with positive volume and no nonadjacent
self-crossing polygons. Static native checks find no crossings with the
retained cabinet, valve wheel, closed door, four new coatings, or the current
tin and spare gasket. An initial probe used the superseded source-plan tin
and gasket locations and reported crossings. Those original diagnostics are
retained; the corrected probe explicitly reads the installed owner's offsets.
Moving joints, nozzle poses, tag swing, deployment and the complete assembled
mechanism are outside this static scope.

Longitudinal metre UVs and the existing custom linen PBR material are being
reviewed. Exported tangent derivatives pass, but tangent correctness alone
does not accept the surface. Centreline-based mapping stretched the inside
of the tight bends by up to 6.34 times in area. Measuring each cloth strand's
actual travel substantially improves the distribution, but remaining local
distortion in the transitions is still open. A missing-image preview caused
by saving to a different directory was diagnosed separately; the final
candidate establishes its save directory before assigning catalogue images.
No loaded Godot mip-chain or final surface acceptance is claimed for this
candidate. The complete mesh is also still 271,360 triangles; optimize and
repeat native contact checks before installing eight copies.

Sources, original diagnostic logs, native previews and construction summaries
are preserved in **art/reviews/v2_fire_hose_capacity_20261019**. Editable study
files remain under **tmp/v2-fire-hand-20261019/hose_capacity_native5**. The
study is not published to production. Finish fitted supports in all physical
poses, texture density, deployment/refolding, coupled hose movement and saved
pose reconstruction before the next combined Godot qualification. Human
appearance acceptance remains **OPEN**.
