## Thesis

The ecosystem is the dream body's microbiome. Creatures emerge from the landed
lineage body's birth-frames, feed on the exposure field awakened by the service
lamp, reproduce statistically and are reabsorbed into wall tissue. The lamp is
the sun; it remains the only player verb. Forgotten rooms forget their fauna and
derive the same biome again from Atlas lineage, decay, case-folded seed and
golden salts. Nothing is saved.

## Production laws

1. **Pure derivation, no campaign storage.** A room biome is a pure function of
   lineage genome, decay, night and fixed salts. Live dynamics are chase-frame
   state only.
2. **Simulate densities, render individuals.** A fixed ~3 Hz tick advances four
   bounded values—nutrient, grazer, predator and detritus—while deterministic
   slots realize visible births, feeding, courtship, death and reabsorption.
3. **Batch families.** One MultiMesh per family across the pocket; shader gait;
   no particles, rigs, per-creature nodes, lights, shadows or collision.
4. **Presentation-only ownership.** The proposed `DreamFaunaDirector` may read
   live rooms, lineage, decay, exposure, lamp and Tenant position. It may not
   write topology, plan, hazards, exposure, cases or saves.
5. **The Tenant outranks all life.** Fauna hush, freeze and submerge near her,
   and close/freeze during the existing embrace. They never use her
   shadows-only language, scale or role. Nothing strobes; luminance stays sub-Hz.

## Proposed architecture

One `DreamFaunaDirector` child of `DreamMazeRoot` owns the fixed tick, ephemeral
per-room density records, deterministic realization and family MultiMeshes. It
diffs `DreamRoomBuilder.live_rooms()` rather than adding builder callbacks.
`DreamLineageBody` would publish birth-frame/bud transforms as metadata; fauna
reads them. Movement remains within a room in v1, so Juno's partitions and all
route owners remain orthogonal. Fauna may appear through the depth-zero R6 view
only if the owner approves that visibility.

Suggested total ceiling: 96 visible instances, 10–13 draws, zero lights,
shadows and collision, and no more than +0.5 ms at the existing worst-pocket
station. These are proposed acceptance values, not rulings.

## Genome and succession

Lineage curl biases slitherers, girth biases round grazers, mutations bias
disconnected forms, and decay increases strangeness. Adjacent rooms therefore
share a biological character while long walks drift. Instance hashes vary hue,
pattern, scale, gait phase and optional mesh sections without adding draws.
Early passages remain sparse; deep-decay rooms may support late families.

## Interaction and safety

One table maps beam splash, player proximity, Tenant proximity, exposure, decay
and cross-family density to gather, retreat, graze, hunt, flee, freeze or
submerge. V1 is proposed as entirely harmless: fauna cannot end a run, damage,

through `DreamHazard`, with a ruled tell and warning margin. V1 has no audio; a
later audio pass must remain below hazard/Tenant cues and never occupy the

## Visual addendum — silhouette, texture and style system

**OWNER-DIRECTED DESIGN PASS INGESTED 2026-08-20 — NOT YET LANDED.** This is
the visual doctrine for dream flora/fauna. It does not undo FA1/FA2's functional
or ownership proofs. Migration must preserve their density behavior, caps,
harmlessness and cost contracts. Three creative calls remain explicit at the
end of this addendum, and the current shader/material/debug seams require the
compatibility audit recorded in TASKS before visual implementation.

Everything here obeys the landed dream-surface laws: gold is metallic rather
than emissive; canopy stars are the sanctioned bright emission; dark-live wine
is the one dark exception; motion is safe and slow where it affects luminance;
substance flows while structure does not.

### V1. The three-light test

The dream is black outside the lamp pool. Every family must have a distinct read
in three production frames:

- **Dark:** a wine/dark-glow silhouette; shape carries identity.
- **Beam edge:** a gilt rim catches the cone; contour carries identity.
- **Full beam:** gold ornament and molten response wake; pattern carries
  identity.

Silhouette comes before ornament. The outline is the creature. Every species
requires dark, beam-edge and full-beam proof with the normal A/A control.

### V2. Inverse-signature law

The Tenant is a shadow with no body. Fauna are bodies with no shadow. Elevate
`cast_shadow = OFF` from performance law to aesthetic law: she alone darkens a
wall; they gleam without casting. This protects the one-Tenant distinction at
the perceptual level.

### V3. Silhouette vocabulary

- **Calligraphic profiles:** swept Blender curves with taper and entasis;
  drawn-line contours filled by pattern. Spend triangles, not submissions.
- **Negative-space bodies:** near-black unlit forms seen as holes in gold,
  stars or wine fields. Deep Koi and selected clouds may be moving absence.
- **Aperture bodies:** clean keyhole, vesica or crescent voids through torsos so
  the room remains visible through the creature.
- **Flatness as dimension:** near-2D ribbon bodies alternate between banner and
  gold stroke while they undulate; use swept two-sided ribbons.
- **Gestalt fragments:** disconnected vesica shards share one spline phase so
  the eye constructs the Parliament that geometry never draws.
- **Kinetic camouflage:** Bezel Beetles match floor tesserae while still and
  become legible only in motion.
- **Motion smear:** the gait shader stretches vertices along velocity, creating
  a calligraphic gesture without trails or extra instances.
- **Quarter-turn morphs:** selected disc-front/blade-side profiles transform
  continuously as the player moves around them, without touching the camera.

### V4. Shader doctrine — cloisonné, no bitmap skins

**One-line style guide:** the ecosystem is cloisonné jewelry—wine enamel cells
partitioned by gold wire. Fauna inherit the lineage shader's vessel grammar so
they read as ornament made alive by the same goldsmith as the walls.

- No fauna texture files. Procedural pattern lives in a shared fauna shader
  include beside `dream_lineage_gold.gdshader`, selected from packed per-instance
  and vertex data. Do not create material/draw proliferation.
- **Habitat motif law:** floor species wear MOSAIC, wall species SPIRAL,
  ceiling species CANOPY/STAR, threshold species CHEVRON. The decorative eye
  motif stays reserved for danger; harmless animals may have real eyes as
  organs, but not eye-pattern skin.
- Vertex color carries region id, curvature/AO for niello recesses and two or
  three procedural joint weights. No UV unwrap.
- Joint weights form a bone-lite rig: shader rotations produce swim/walk from
  `TIME` plus instance phase, without skeletons or `AnimationPlayer`s.
- Anisotropic tangent flow runs along the body axis for brushed silk/fur gold.
- Reuse tissue transmission and wet-specular terms for mucosal surfaces and
  backlit membrane wings.
- Courtship may use spatial moiré from two pattern scales. It changes pattern,
  never brightness, and must not become temporal flashing.
- One species only may use the R4 recession-band trick as an impossible gem
  window. It uses no viewport or camera.

### V5. Systematic style guide

#### Color tokens

- **GROUND:** aubergine-black, the `1d1a20` family.
- **WINE:** dark-live plum.
- **GOLD:** antique, metallic and lamp-woken.
- **EMERALD:** `(0.180, 0.404, 0.360)`.
- **CARNELIAN:** `(0.451, 0.098, 0.106)`.
- **LAPIS:** `(0.145, 0.216, 0.463)`.

These are the three authored frieze stones; invent no fourth. Each creature is
approximately 70% ground-dark mass, 20% wine, 8% gold response and 2% jewel.
Each owns one jewel organ—iris, lure or abdomen stone—except Jewelfruit, whose
whole body is the sanctioned stone exception.

Hue expresses allegiance: warm gold approaches the lamp; lapis retreats;
carnelian is reserved for danger-adjacent life. Nothing exceeds woken gold
except sanctioned stars; there is no white; saturation remains precious.

#### Shape tiers

- **Cute / Fulfilment:** domes and circles, short symmetric axes, large round
  irises, bounce/overshoot and warm gold. Buttons and Tessellates.
- **Sexy / Tree:** long lines and curves, 10:1+ aspect ratios, asymmetric drape,
  half-lidded eyes, slow sinusoidal ease, wine bodies, gold selvage and wet
  film. Ribbonettes, Wine Anemones and Chandelettes.
- **Cool-alien / Expectation:** facets, vesicas, crescents and disconnected
  geometry, linear-mechanical ease, lapis and the slowest phases. Parliaments,
  Deep Koi, Bezel Beetles and the Loupe.

Organic tiers use coiled-wire curves without straight edges. Non-organic life
uses pure geometry without curves. That contrast is the organic/inorganic axis.

#### Scale and motion

- **PEARL:** about 3 cm.
- **HAND:** about 10 cm.
- **CAT:** about 30 cm.
- **KOI:** about 1.2 m implied below a surface.

No species sits between classes. Cute gait is 1.5–2.5 Hz, sexy gait 0.3–0.8 Hz,
and alien motion 0.065–0.105 Hz. Those are geometry bands; luminance change
remains sub-Hz. Courtship doubles pattern density, not brightness.

#### Eye grammar

Inherit the R3 lid, long-blink and fixed-gaze family. At most one shader-driven
camera tracker may exist per batch. Flora have no eyes. The Loupe is all eye.
Iris ratio and lid height are the chief cute/sexy controls.

### V6. Per-species assignments

- **Gilder's Buttons:** PEARL, cute, dome-lathe profiles, mosaic caps, no eyes,
  gold-warm.
- **Wine Anemones:** HAND, sexy, swept taper curls, maximum wet film, shy lapis
  tips, no eyes.
- **Jewelfruit:** PEARL, non-organic faceted stone in a gold bezel; the whole
  body is its one jewel; no movement beyond ripening scale.
- **Tessellates:** HAND, cute, sphere-dominant boolean-softened body, mosaic
  plate, round-lid eyes, squash/stretch bounce and overshoot.
- **Spiralings:** HAND, cute/cool bridge, true spiral shell, spiral filigree and
  an aperture-carved keel showing the wall through its void.
- **Ribbonettes:** CAT-length but ribbon-flat, sexy, chevron selvage,
  anisotropic groom, courtship moiré and half-lidded eyes.
- **Chandelettes:** HAND, sexy, pendant-lathe abdomen with one stone, backlit
  membrane wings and ceiling-habitat star marks.
- **Bezel Beetles:** PEARL, non-organic brilliant-cut gem with prong legs,
  kinetic camouflage and the single impossible gem-window treatment.
- **Deep Koi:** KOI, cool, negative-space traveling relief bulge with no body
  mesh; a gold fin breach is its only positive geometry.
- **Parliaments:** CAT-implied, cool, gestalt vesica fragments with lapis edges
  and linear ease.
- **The Loupe:** CAT, cool, one giant landed-grammar eye, lash curves, the
  batch's single tracker and blink-away motion smear. Its proposed carnelian and
  decorative eye treatment remain an explicit harmless/danger-adjacent ruling.
