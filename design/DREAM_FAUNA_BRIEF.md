# DREAM FAUNA — HYPERDIMENSIONAL FLORA/FAUNA ECOSYSTEM

**RULED FOR F1 PRODUCTION — 2026-08-20.**

This brief governs the owner-directed request to populate the dream with an
efficient procedural ecosystem: visually distinct organic and non-organic life,
multiple locomotion families and bounded variation, with the dream entity's own
body as the source and destination of life. It does not authorize implementation.
The ruled dream, ownership and case-profile contracts continue to prevail.

**Owner ruling:** all five decisions below are approved as written:
`DreamFaunaDirector` is a presentation-only owner; the named bestiary/register
is accepted; v1 is harmless; fauna remain visible through the R6 view; and F1
is Gilder's Buttons plus Tessellates under the 96-instance, +13-draw and +0.5 ms
ceilings. This licenses F1 only, not FA2–FA4 or dangerous fauna.

## Thesis

The ecosystem is the dream body's microbiome. Creatures emerge from the landed
lineage body's birth-frames, feed on the exposure field awakened by the service
lamp, reproduce statistically and are reabsorbed into wall tissue. The lamp is
the sun; it remains the only player verb. Forgotten rooms forget their fauna and
derive the same biome again from Atlas lineage, decay, case-folded seed and
golden salts. Nothing is saved.

## Production laws

1. **Pure derivation, no campaign storage.** A room biome is a pure function of
   lineage genome, decay, night and fixed salts. Live dynamics are chase-frame
   state only.
2. **Simulate densities, render individuals.** A fixed ~3 Hz tick advances four
   bounded values—nutrient, grazer, predator and detritus—while deterministic
   slots realize visible births, feeding, courtship, death and reabsorption.
3. **Batch families.** One MultiMesh per family across the pocket; shader gait;
   no particles, rigs, per-creature nodes, lights, shadows or collision.
4. **Presentation-only ownership.** The proposed `DreamFaunaDirector` may read
   live rooms, lineage, decay, exposure, lamp and Tenant position. It may not
   write topology, plan, hazards, exposure, cases or saves.
5. **The Tenant outranks all life.** Fauna hush, freeze and submerge near her,
   and close/freeze during the existing embrace. They never use her
   shadows-only language, scale or role. Nothing strobes; luminance stays sub-Hz.

## Proposed architecture

One `DreamFaunaDirector` child of `DreamMazeRoot` owns the fixed tick, ephemeral
per-room density records, deterministic realization and family MultiMeshes. It
diffs `DreamRoomBuilder.live_rooms()` rather than adding builder callbacks.
`DreamLineageBody` would publish birth-frame/bud transforms as metadata; fauna
reads them. Movement remains within a room in v1, so Juno's partitions and all
route owners remain orthogonal. Fauna may appear through the depth-zero R6 view
only if the owner approves that visibility.

Suggested total ceiling: 96 visible instances, 10–13 draws, zero lights,
shadows and collision, and no more than +0.5 ms at the existing worst-pocket
station. These are proposed acceptance values, not rulings.

## Genome and succession

Lineage curl biases slitherers, girth biases round grazers, mutations bias
disconnected forms, and decay increases strangeness. Adjacent rooms therefore
share a biological character while long walks drift. Instance hashes vary hue,
pattern, scale, gait phase and optional mesh sections without adding draws.
Early passages remain sparse; deep-decay rooms may support late families.

## Proposed bestiary

Flora: **Gilder's Buttons** (exposure crop), **Jewelfruit** (canopy mineral
fruit), and **Wine Anemones** (joint-bound dark-live tufts).

Fauna: **Tessellates** (walking/bouncing grazers), **Spiralings**
(wall-crawlers), **Ribbonettes** (slithering double-helix courtship),
**Chandelettes** (slow flying lamp-orbiters), **Bezel Beetles** (rolling mineral
life), **Deep Koi** (sub-surface relief swimmers), **Parliaments**
(hyperdimensional synchronized shards), and **the Loupe** (animal-scale,
harmless predator). Names, register and inclusion all require owner approval.
The unused harpy/oni assets are not candidates.

## Interaction and safety

One table maps beam splash, player proximity, Tenant proximity, exposure, decay
and cross-family density to gather, retreat, graze, hunt, flee, freeze or
submerge. V1 is proposed as entirely harmless: fauna cannot end a run, damage,
stagger, obstruct or gate a route. Any dangerous species must later enter
through `DreamHazard`, with a ruled tell and warning margin. V1 has no audio; a
later audio pass must remain below hazard/Tenant cues and never occupy the
motif's missing fifth position.

## Minimum provable rollout

- **F1:** Gilder's Buttons plus Tessellates in one production room: feeding,
  birth from a real birth-frame, death/reabsorption, Tenant hush and measured
  cost. Stop if the slice is not delightful.
- **F2:** three or four families plus the harmless Loupe; complete trophic loop.
- **F3:** succession, courtship, Deep Koi, Parliaments and surface swimming.
- **F4:** separately ruled audio. Dangerous life remains outside this brief.

## Proof contract

`DreamFaunaTest` must prove deterministic census, caps, room forgetting and
identical re-derivation, hush/recovery, embrace freeze, zero collision/light/
shadow/per-instance nodes, and byte-stable doors, plan, hazards, exposure and
save state. `DreamFaunaShot` must use the production root and an equal-interval
A/A control before lamp gathering, hush and birth-frame A/B frames. `PERF_DREAM`
must report before/after draws and frame cost.
