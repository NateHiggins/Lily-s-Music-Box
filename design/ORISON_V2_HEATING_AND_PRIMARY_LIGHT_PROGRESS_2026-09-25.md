checks the actual radiance textures, material ownership and resource teardown.

## Remaining scope and limitations

The optical volume is beam-local instantaneous lighting with native shadowed
surface lighting. It is not whole-building voxel global illumination. Air/dust
remain bounded to the accepted local volume even though the native throw is
longer. Darkness outside the beam, normal thermal transients and the physical
hand-held aim remain visible. Room fixtures retain their ordinary switches.

Basement electrical/shop/coal/storage architecture, the lower service-stair
continuation, modeled ventilation branches and roof lift machinery still need
completion. New resident stories and repair jobs are not invented. Existing
H23 seams and the historical M11C1 receipt-hash debt retain their own status;
this work does not claim an entirely green historical test catalogue.
