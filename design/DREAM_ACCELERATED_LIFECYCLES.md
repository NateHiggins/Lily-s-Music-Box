# DREAM ACCELERATED LIFECYCLES

Owner ruling, 2026-08-24. Every visible Dream organelle passes through an
accelerated life, reproductive and death cycle. Death leaves a beautiful,
disturbing environmental stain. The ecology is sustained by an alien
moss/plant/fungus tissue and a closed ether-vapour cycle.

This extends the one-body canon in `DREAM_ECOLOGY_ARCHITECTURE.md` and
`DREAM_ORGANELLE_COMMUNICATION.md`. It does not turn local organs into separate
species, restore predator/prey language or create a second ecology director.

## What lives and dies

The larger hyperdimensional being does not die every few minutes. Its local
incursions do. A fauna body, fungal button, palp, cilium, temporary limb,
architectural organ, incarnation, pursuer or hazard is tissue pushed through a
narrow human-visible section of the whole. Its birth and death are recruitment
and shedding at that section. Large hero and case organs may withdraw or leave
an empty sheath instead of becoming a small corpse, but they still traverse the
same readable stages.

The shared stage vocabulary is:

1. **spore / folded** — complete anatomy held in reserve;
2. **bud** — attachment and first perfusion;
3. **juvenile** — feeding, sampling and calibration;
4. **mature** — full local function;
5. **exchange** — reproduction and signal transfer;
6. **senescent** — slowing, mineral bloom and membrane thinning;
7. **shed** — local death and collapse;
8. **stain / reclaim** — persistent evidence consumed only slowly by the bed.

Small organelles should complete a visible life in roughly 45–150 seconds,
with staggered cohorts so the room never pulses in lockstep. Large organs may
hold stages longer for encounter fairness. Birth, sex and death cannot alter
collision, navigation, hazard tells, case truth or maze topology.

## Reproduction is environmental

All three modes belong to one body. They exchange pattern and function rather
than establish animal parentage.

- **Asexual budding** dominates when food is plentiful, ether is low and a
  useful tissue is locally sparse. It is fast, clonal repair.
- **Sexual recombination** dominates when ether is rich and two mature,
  complementary local organs can maintain a stable cleft. It trades packets
  and secretion before one or both bud.
- **Pansexual / pan-compatible recombination** dominates in heterogeneous or
  partner-poor tissue. Any mature organelle class may exchange a bounded
  pattern contribution with any other class, irrespective of morphology or
  apparent sex. The offspring keeps the receiving owner's gameplay function;
  only anatomy, surface pattern, affinity and behaviour bias may recombine.

This is deliberately sensual: approach, appraisal, mutual opening, ciliary
contact, secretion, electrical climax, temporary fusion and separation should
be elegant and legible. It is never coercive shorthand. Inhibitory or rejecting
signals visibly stop the exchange. The being's incomplete understanding of
human consent remains a story problem, not permission for unreadable contact.

## The primary food — ethermoss

**Ethermoss** is the common name for one alien composite bed that reads at once
as moss, lichen, slime mould, fungal mycelium and microscopic villi. Gilder's
Buttons are its fruiting bodies, not a separate crop. Wine Anemones are its
reclamation mouths. The mat creeps along seams, radiator-like ribs, skirtings
and dead stains; lamp exposure reveals jewel vesicles and gold conducting
hyphae without making it ordinary green vegetation.

Ethermoss consumes mineral salts, transformed architecture and reclaimed dead
tissue. Organelles crop its villous surface or receive vesicles from it. It is
the ecology's primary food and the visual ground that makes an occupied room
feel cultivated rather than randomly populated.

## The ether breath

“Ether” is tenant vocabulary for a cold, faintly luminous aerosol/plasma phase
of the being's transport medium. It is not supernatural fuel and never enters
waking inventory. Ethermoss exhales it; active organelles draw it across cilia,
spiracles, wet membranes and pressure pits; their depleted exhalate, secretions
and shed tissue return to the mat through reclamation.

Each live room keeps one bounded, conservative presentation ledger:

`ethermoss → ether vapour → living tissue → death stain → ethermoss`

Transfers may change rate with light, pressure, crowding and Dream attention,
but the sum cannot create matter. Ether should pool low in cold rooms, stream
toward breathing anatomy and briefly ionize around electrical communication.
No particle swarm, damaging gas volume, new light owner or gameplay oxygen bar
is implied.

## Death stains

A shed organ leaves a flattened anatomical memory: wine-dark membrane,
iridescent oil edge, branching capillary negative space, gold mineral sutures
and pale vesicles where a body used to breathe. It must be attractive enough to
approach and wrong enough to regret touching. Stains inherit the dead organ's
position, family motif and a small genome trace; they are not generic decals.

Stains persist for the entire current Dream visit and survive room streaming.
They coalesce into a bounded per-room record rather than one node per corpse.
They are cleared at waking and never enter `RealityState`. Reclamation may
thin or overgrow them but cannot erase a newly witnessed death immediately.
This narrow visit-memory supersedes the earlier identical-rederivation rule
for the stain overlay only; live fauna still re-derive from the room seed.

## Ownership and rollout

`DreamFaunaDirector` owns the first complete slice because it already owns the
fixed density tick, deterministic room realization and the five harmless
batches. It gains ethermoss/ether/living/stain compartments, environmental
reproduction choice, accelerated stages and a bounded visit-stain memory.
Gilder's Buttons become fruiting bodies of the mat and their existing batch may
also carry flattened stain impressions, avoiding another draw.

Other organ owners later consume the shared stage vocabulary but retain their
own clocks and safety gates. `DreamEcologyDirector` continues to store only
transient signal packets; it does not breed or kill anything. A pure lifecycle
helper may classify stages and environmental modes, but owns no nodes, routes,
facts or persistence.

Production order:

1. Pure lifecycle/reproduction/environment contract with conservation tests.
2. Fauna-room lifecycle integration and visit-persistent stain memory.
3. Ethermoss/stain anatomy in the existing Gilder's Button batch.
4. Visible ether breath using bounded existing material/field owners.
5. Palp, cilia and hero stages; then architecture/incarnation/hazard stages.
6. Production A/A, full lifecycle sequence and performance proof.

Acceptance requires all three reproductive modes under named environmental
constraints; every stage without zero-scale anatomy; a witnessed death whose
stain survives streaming; closed-cycle conservation; no save, collision,
topology, hazard or case mutation; and rendered proof that moss, breath and
stain remain readable under the unreliable lamp.
