# Vantry service set: hero-object concept iteration

Evidence class: **INERT**. Native design study and short baseline diagnostic;
not installed gameplay, runtime qualification, or human acceptance.

## Direction and boundaries

The owner now wants the instrument visible most of the time, a showcase with
readable output and restrained screen occupation. This newer instruction
supersedes the earlier normally-line-only presentation preference. The no-screen
radiophone, physical paper, existing radio/lamp controls, one WorkOrders jewel,
case/inventory authorities and unrestricted player movement remain intact.
The world lamp remains the existing volumetric emitter; no lens flare is added.

Three Blender concepts were compared: A, a tall field ledger; B, a broad folio;
C, a compact service set. C was developed further because the near-square paper
window can retain the right-third reading width without a full-height frame.
The retained native views show the final front, open service door and rear.
Earlier renders, including the misplaced paper and naming failure, remain in
the review packet. Earlier A/B renders are shape studies, not finished products.

The final candidate is 214 x 193 x 55mm before projecting controls, with a
164 x 116mm paper bed. A rolled-paper bonnet, platen bearings, spring paper
guides, tear bar, ribbon hubs, carriage rails and supported return spring explain
the paper path. The hammer belongs to the moving carriage. The lower door has
hinge knuckles, a pin and captive latches; it exposes retained dry cells and
their clips instead of a solid decorative shell. A rear hand strap, carbon
mouthpiece, side receiver grille, lamp saddle and telescoping aerial complete
the heavy radio identity. Review lettering is separate unexported geometry;
production lettering remains Label3D.

## Measured baseline and source budget

The already-running windowed baseline finished before any new candidate input
was installed. It built the actual V2 world, used the same standing view, sent
E to the carrier and small actual mouse turns, then retained 48 wall-time samples
in each presentation state with VSync disabled and no frame cap at 1280 x 720.
The source harness and original suite receipt are preserved in
**art/reviews/v2_handheld_20261023/**. The receipt says completed, exit 0 and
HANDHELD REVIEW PASS. It is a wrapper receipt, not a runtime contract.

| Baseline | p50 ms | p95 ms | p99 ms | max ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Collapsed | 22.762 | 28.292 | 30.102 | 30.660 |
| Deployed | 21.950 | 26.969 | 29.225 | 29.262 |

These short distributions overlap; they do not establish the handheld's GPU
cost or an improvement. Process/physics monitor values are retained but repeat
between engine monitor updates. Rendering, shader compilation, streaming and
loading were not separately attributed by this harness. Broader world stutter
remains open.

The old assembled instrument has 384 mesh nodes, including a procedural radio
behind the native printer. Its deployed alpha mask covers 178014 pixels, 19.32%
of the viewport, inside a 426 x 720 held pass. Body glyph em is 13.25 pixels.
The new candidate has 35 material/assembly partitions and 11920 triangles.
These counts are source measurements, not a 384-to-35 installed optimization.
Seven existing catalogue finishes are reused; no new bitmap texture or shader
is introduced. Jewels need their own authoritative state overrides at runtime.
Source GLB UV audit passes all 35 surfaces; exported tangent agreement is at
least 0.99999978. The 1379 currently referenced runtime texture import policies
pass unchanged. Imported precision, loaded maps/mips and runtime appearance of
the new candidate remain untested.

## Next playable iteration, requiring owner permission

No new Godot process was started after the owner requested a pause before Godot.
Another contributor held the shared import lane during native review; nothing
was stopped. The candidate is deliberately unreferenced by production scripts.

Next: bind catalogue finishes and the existing state owners to the native
instrument, remove the duplicate procedural assembly, fit a compact persistent
corner pose and larger paper-reading pose, and verify actual grab/motion/release
for accessible physical controls. Paper pagination must preserve every supplied
fact and historical report; no truncation to fit this study's sample copy.
The focus wheel, switches, cover, platen and ribbon must move from their real
owner values, with meaningful stops and no competing state machine.

Use an event-driven held pass: bounded animation during printing/operation,
cached idle output after motion settles, no perpetual decorative physics,
shadow maps, new world lights, continuous duplicate target scans or extra
post-processing. Initial native budget is at most 36 mesh partitions and 35000
triangles. Screen occupation and actual glyph size must be measured at 720p,
1080p and different aspect ratios before accepting the carrier framing.

After owner approval, use only shared-lane wrappers for the candidate import,
loaded UV/PBR/mipmap checks, actual-input mechanisms and the same occupied-world
before/after view. Add first-print and steady-print distributions, draw/primitive
counts and compilation counters; collect enough frames to separate cold events
from idle costs. Expand the world-route attribution separately; this static
object study does not qualify V2 performance or completion.
