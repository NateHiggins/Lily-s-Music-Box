# THE ENCROACHMENT — owner ruling 2026-08-21 and the programme it opens

*Sequencing authority for the dream's look, the dream's reach into the waking
building, and the critters, from this date. It sits under the Bible and
`THE_TENANT.md`; it supersedes the look sections of
`DREAM_SURFACE_REDESIGN_BRIEF.md` where they conflict, and leaves that
brief's exposure field, hazard ownership and safety rules intact.*

## THE RULING, VERBATIM

> 1 yes, 2 + 3 apply cool yes unless otherwise directed. I want the materials
> we generated for the dream world included in the real world especially
> customizing each cases apartment to make them feel the encroachment from
> the dream world. in the dream world we need distinct layers... the golden
> shader is cool but dated, we need a solid, physically grounded base with
> ethereal layers on top, not a wavy golden shower curtain. make a realistic
> idealized fractalized apartment being encroached from the 5th dimension,
> consuming, melting folding reality with a purplish flesh golden skin and
> impossible molten golden hyperdimensional shifting interplanar dimensional
> welds that work like portals. also a variety of hyperdimensional crittters
> we have sketched in need detailed textures, build them from the ones we
> made.

Read as five decisions:

1. **M-COVER adopted.** Cell-snapped hex for terrazzo (and lattice sets),
   per-board-row offsets for oak, into production
   (`art/renders/material_coverage_m/README.md`).
2. **Rule of cool is standing licence** for the punchlist taste rows and for
   the items that were gated on an owner ruling (FA3–FA4 breadth, FA-V5
   style breadth, RECURSION's *look*, the embrace's later forms) — "yes
   unless otherwise directed". Safety rules (no photosensitive flashing, no
   forced roll/fisheye/chromatic assault) are not taste and stay.
3. **The dream reaches into the waking building.** The six cases' generated
   plates (`game/assets/dream/incarnations/<case>/`, four substances and one
   reflected-world plate each) appear in each case's real apartment so the
   flat feels the encroachment before the player ever sleeps.
4. **The dream is re-layered.** A physically grounded base — the real,
   idealised Orison apartment, PBR, photographable as itself — with ethereal
   layers above it: purplish flesh under a golden skin; the building being
   consumed, melted and folded from a fifth direction; and molten golden
   interplanar *welds* — the seams where folded reality has been fused —
   that shift and behave as portals. The gold is a skin over flesh, not a
   curtain over plaster.
5. **The critters get real textures**, built from the plates already made
   (no new generation is available while the image lane is out), for the
   landed five and the sketched FA3 families.

## WHAT IS ALREADY TRUE THAT THIS BUILDS ON

- The Klimt shader already declares itself a *filter over the real material
  library* ("the dream is the Orison being eaten") and R1 landed a
  recognisable Orison substrate; the complaint is the look of the layers
  above it, not the premise. `dream_corruption_layers.gdshaderinc` already
  holds a tissue / tendon-fold / wet-film / warped-gold vocabulary, and the
  hazard growth is already wine-purple flesh with lamp-woken gold veins.
- R4–R6 already built one torn wall with 32 m of false depth, a transition
  controller (Orison → rupture → living gold), and a bounded shared-world
  camera showing another live Atlas room through it, at recursion depth
  zero. That *is* a weld-as-portal, singular and hazard-provenant. The
  ruling generalises it into a vocabulary.
- Six incarnation bundles are shipped and residency-proven (17 maps,
  96 MiB active); `dream_incarnation.gdshaderinc` anti-tiles them; the fauna
  shader already wears the active case's plates as skin.
- The waking apartments already carry one authored anomaly prop per case
  (`domestic_anomaly_props.json`: Mina 2A intercom, Juno 2C speaker, Omar 3B
  outlet, Peter 4A thermostat, Cal 5B table radio, Mae 6C key bowl) and
  per-wall compiled finishes with survival masks — the encroachment has a
  place to land and a pipeline to land through.
- `DreamAtlas` owns topology, `DreamHazard` owns danger, the exposure field
  owns what has been uncovered. None of that changes here.

## THE LAYER MODEL (decision 4)

Five layers, bottom to top, each photographable alone with the ones beneath:

| # | layer | owner | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Base** | real material library, M-COVER classes | the idealised 1928 apartment, PBR, lit by the service lamp; must read as the Orison with every upper layer off |
| 1 | **Fold** | geometry-side, bounded | reality folded from a fifth direction: vertex-side warps that stay inside the aperture rules (R5's 0.085 UV warp is the precedent), mirrored/repeated architecture across a fold line, floors that continue as ceilings — *fractal* in the Atlas sense, never a camera effect |
| 2 | **Flesh** | `dream_corruption_layers` tissue/tendon/wet-film | purplish (wine/plum/aubergine) living tissue growing from exposure, physically shaded: subsurface-ish wrap, wet specular, slow sub-Hz breath |
| 3 | **Skin** | living gold as a *metallic skin over flesh* | antique gold with real roughness and real reflections, draped over layer 2 where exposure is high — it has thickness, edges and tears; it is never a full-screen pattern and never emissive |
| 4 | **Welds** | generalised R4–R6 | molten golden seams where two folded pieces of reality were fused: slow flowing metal with heat-gradient colour (no flicker, no strobe), along fold lines and around the breach; at high exposure some welds *open* into portals — the R6 bounded camera, now placed by the weld vocabulary rather than one per hazard, still depth zero, still non-enterable unless a case names the verb |

The test for layer 0 is a frame under the service lamp with layers 1–4 at
zero that a stranger would call a real flat. The test for the stack is the
same frame at high exposure that the owner calls the thing in the ruling.
"Wavy golden shower curtain" names the failure mode: a single screen-space
pattern draped over everything. Every layer above 0 must be *local* — it
grows from a place, has an edge, and stops.

## THE WAKING ENCROACHMENT (decision 3)

Per case apartment, in the resident's own material language (the six
surface languages of `SIX_INCARNATIONS.md` already name them):

- **Surfaces:** the case's four substance plates enter the apartment's
  material set through the existing per-wall finish pipeline and the
  runtime `MatLib` route — as a *creep* with a mask, not a repaint: a
  gilt-edged seam along a skirting, ink-fibre bleeding through one wall's
  plaster, carbon-copy ghosting on a desk top. Strength is tied to the
  case's progress (`RealityState` case stage), so the flat gets stranger as
  the case deepens and settles when it resolves.
- **Objects:** the anomaly prop the case already owns is the beachhead; it
  gets the first plate.
- **Reflection:** the case's reflected-world plate is what its mirror or
  glass shows at the late stage, through the existing late-reflection tell.
- Nothing in the waking loop's logic changes: no new verbs, no new case
  state, no new save keys beyond reading stage.

## THE CRITTER TEXTURES (decision 5)

The fauna already sample the active case's plates as skin through the
cloisonné shader. "Detailed textures" means per-family plates: a packed
skin atlas per family built from the thirty dream plates (crops, recolours,
fibre/wet/metal families mapped to body regions the part kit already
authors — region 2 gold wire, 3/5 jewel, 4 sclera, 6 lid). Built by a
script, versioned, reviewed in the three-light harness. FA3 families
(Jewelfruit, Spiralings, Chandelettes, Bezel Beetles, Deep Koi,
Parliaments) are licensed by decision 2 and get the same treatment after
their part kits exist.

## BUILD ORDER

1. **MC-P — M-COVER into production.** `FloorCoveragePass` swaps terrazzo
   and oak floor materials for the proven shader at build; frames, perf
   station A/B, WalkTest, LightingAudit. Small and ruled.

   switched on one at a time, plus the high-exposure composite; costs per
   layer. No production change until the owner has seen the stack.
3. **EN-2 — welds.** The weld vocabulary along fold lines, the portal
   opening rule, bounded cameras placed by welds; perf against the dream
   station.
4. **EN-3 — folds.** Bounded geometry-side folding inside the Atlas's
   non-Euclidean promise; fairness clamps re-run.
5. **WK-1 — Mina's flat first.** 2A carries her four plates as creep tied
   to case stage; then the other five in owner order (Peter, Juno, Mae, Cal,
   Omar).
6. **CT-1 — family skin atlases** for the landed five; **CT-2** for FA3 as
   each part kit lands.
7. The punchlist taste rows, by rule of cool, in the gaps.

Every slice ships with frames from a fixed camera and a measured cost, and
none of them touches topology, danger, exposure ownership or the save
boundary.
