# Directing the historical visits

Evidence class: **INERT**

These are proposed performances for the 13 callable historical visits. The time and conditions come from [the anchor table](../game/data/temporal/anchors.json); the cast's phase changes come from [the performance table](../game/data/temporal/performances.json). The player keeps the camera and can leave, look elsewhere or talk. Shot directions describe coverage for a film take and sightlines to protect in gameplay. The player manipulates each discovery. Returning to a mark or hearing a line gives no extra evidence. On a pickup, restore the actual visit phase, object state and knowledge before placing a camera. The following times are civil start times. Minute offsets in the blocking run from admission to that visit.

## The radiator in 6C · 13 June, 16:20 · tea

Begin over the investigator's tool hand with the radiator and tea tray visible in one turn of the head. Mrs. Kessler clears a place, notices she has put something back where it began, and straightens it a second time. Give that second movement time to be funny. Nadia's first ten minutes belong elsewhere. Let her arrive through the doorway and stop on its hinge-free side. When Mrs. Kessler steps away for cold tea at minute 35, keep the same tray edge in frame. Lifting it exposes the order slip and two distinct hands on the paper. A conversation with Nadia holds her at the threshold; resume the current phase when it ends. Frame the handwriting closely enough to read before asking for a reaction.

## Friday's errand · 19 July, 18:20 · friday

Open on a vacant patch of 4C where a returning courier would throw his jersey. Hold the emptiness on an early arrival. At 18:40 Cam has finished his round. Let his shoulders settle before he starts putting his things in order; the homecoming should be recognizable with sound off. The player lifts the jersey to uncover his route leaf. Cam's kitchen break at minute 40 gives the player room to read. Keep the pencil mark and the instruction to hold for Saturday in the same insert. If the player leaves before Cam comes home, the visit clock and errand continue.

## The stairs before light · 20 July, 03:02 · stairs

From 1D's threshold keep Teresa's flask and fob watch at different depths. Begin with the flask in the way. Use a quiet breath between the two lifts. When the watch comes up, let its chain fall clear of the folded note and hold on the written time. Teresa's short work, rest and work beats continue at minutes 3, 15 and 28 with her existing whereabouts. She need not appear beside the investigator. Ground the conclusion in her nurse's entry; the stair sound and her exhaustion cannot identify who caused the injury.

## The writer upstairs · 6 August, 20:00 · notebook

Give Jonah the physical habit of beginning a sentence with his forefinger and rubbing it away with his thumb. Keep the dated spines, desk and basket identifiable from one standing master. Let the player draw the July 19 notebook, lift the draft, then uncover the discarded page. Repeat the framing of the first account for the struck-through one. Jonah shifts from work to his hobby at minute 20 and goes to the kitchen at 45. If questioned during the search, give him the existing line and leave the papers where the player put them. The contradiction belongs to the two texts.

## The mending basket · 20 July, 05:00 · button

Block Lena on the working side of her bench with an empty landing spot for the parcel. Her fingers measure a small span, then stop to let the investigator reach. Play each reveal at hand scale. First the finished parcel, then the tape, then the notions box; keep a clean visual route back to the original pile. Lena rests in the kitchen at minute 45 and resumes work at 65. On interruption, her hand leaves the work surface. The button book's dated entry carries the information. A glance at a glove must never identify a customer.

## The yard drain · 7 August, 09:00 · drain

This scene plays as a duet between the player's hand and the drain. Listen to the water before showing the clean-out. Give the plug's first movement a change of sound and leave a moment for the player to notice what has caught. After 09:10 the existing work order and plug interaction admit the discovery. Frame the scrap and pearl together where the recovery system places them. Preserve the grip and progress if the player looks away as allowed by the interaction owner. Keep the boiler room empty of invented witnesses.

## The twelfth record · 24 August, 15:00 · drama

Seat the camera where Cal and Juno can both be read across the listening space. Cal makes room by the shelf; Juno listens a fraction longer before answering. A useful reverse angle includes the listener's ear and the box being handled. Headphones come off first, followed by the boxes dated the twelfth and nineteenth, then the plain recording. Leave gaps between playbacks so room tone cannot be mistaken for recorded dialogue. Cal's kitchen break starts at minute 55; Juno has her own rest and hobby phases. Dialogue releases the shelf hand and returns control to the search. Keep the recorded comparison audible without using expressions to supply its answer.

## Glass from the ash · 23 September, 12:00 · bench

Hold the dusty outline of a tray as the organizing image. Omar is still on his prior work until 12:20. When he reaches his bench he sorts by size, leaving the player's near edge free. The parts crate, first tray and second tray come off in that order. Let the recovered object catch the existing light as the last tray clears it. During his kitchen interval at minute 55 use its uninterrupted silhouette for the insert. Keep the inspector's and Omar's hands distinct; his sorting cannot complete the player's interaction.

## The sleeve check · 18 October, 19:30 · duplicate

Start wide enough to read the archive boxes as a managed collection. At 19:45 Sacha takes up the check. Follow the player's actual order C, A, B, then the loupe. Hold one steady angle across the three box landings so a repeat visitor can recognize the routine. The sleeve inscription and date need their own reading time. Sacha leaves for the kitchen at minute 45 and returns at 65. If the player engages her, let her look up from the work without exposing the protected sleeve early.

## Plate thirteen · 29 October, 19:00 · plate

Reuse the archive-room axis so returning players recognize Sacha's bench. Change the light source to the light box and make room for the chemistry cans. The bath takes until 19:15. The player's sequence is plate box, cans, light-box lid, loupe and drying print. Let each removal change what the player can physically see. Hold the damaged edge as long as the surviving glove. Preserve the missing half in every insert and every pickup. Sacha's later move to the kitchen opens space around the plate; her expression cannot reconstruct the missing image.

## Mrs. Kessler's box · 9 November, 15:00 · box

Recall June's 6C master from the same side of the cabinet. This time the room has just closed for sales. After 15:05 the glove tray, hairpin tin, index card, object under appraisal and ledger box each yield a little counter space. Let the player see where the last item can safely land. Mrs. Kessler goes to the kitchen at minute 25 and resumes work at 45. Nadia arrives at 48, after much of the search may already have happened. Give her the doorway and a look toward the person who speaks. She acquires no knowledge retroactively. End on the papers together, with their dates legible, before returning to the room.

## Mrs. Owen in the window · 10 November 1928, 09:00 · owen_window

Start on the pavement. Find Adela by following the small adjustments she makes while dressing the Photo Supplies window. Keep her hands and the display in the same plane, then find a side angle where the glass lets the player see her face. Give the exchange room to finish before cutting to the object of her interest. The window log opens the existing witness route. Only hearing the authored exchange can advance that lead. If the player walks past, let the window keep its ordinary appearance.

## Adela on the roof · 11 July, 18:00 · july_roof

Give the player a view of Adela already occupied on the roof. Frame work, parapet and approach together; approach from the open side so a turn toward the caller has a reason. Hold on her listening before she answers. Use the existing July date and window lead for entry and the authored conversation for the key-loan witness. Do not stage the later storm or death here. End on her returning to the work the investigator interrupted. Silence, early departure and replay preserve the existing conversation and knowledge rules.

## Incidental encounters that can run alongside a visit

The [ordinary encounter table](../game/data/temporal/ordinary_performances.json) owns eligibility, exclusions, speakers and words. Stage these only when its participants are present. On a cutaway, preserve who heard the last line. If a participant departs, yield to the encounter owner. Each exchange has its own piece of business.

| Encounter | Blocking and delivery |
| --- | --- |
| OY01 · The margin | Evelyn traces the space a corrected word would need. Peter measures the remaining space with his eyes. Give his offer of more room a beat to sound perfectly practical; finish on Evelyn, who has already finished the word. Keep the lobby or mail-bank passage clear. |
| OY02 · One minute | In the corridor Teresa clocks Cam's loaded shoulder before speaking. He eases that shoulder on her instruction. After his question about her own hours, she holds his eye through “You first.” Let them resume their separate routes. |
| OY03 · The temporary repair | Omar stands still long enough for Lena to judge her work. Cut to the difference between her quick assessment and his expectation of a long repair. Where the actual wardrobe has no repairable part, play it in their eyes and stance; keep the inventory with its owner. |
| OY04 · Keep listening | Juno catches the beginning of Cal's reach. Hold the hand suspended while he says he was considering. Let the chair noise interrupt the shared silence. Keep the apparent sound source off the listening axis so both have a reason to turn. |
| OY05 · Before the picture | On the roof, frame Sacha and the leaf in the same plane. Allow the first adjustment to look like a lighting correction. When Malcolm names the leaf, repeat the original composition with the leaf clear. |
| OY07 · A clear corner | Frame the cup, draft and available landing space together when the room supplies them. Noel sees the danger before Jonah notices the sentence. In a hallway play the encounter with a glance toward what each actually carries. No pantomime against an invisible desk. |
| OY10 · A place for the gloves | Mrs. Kessler indicates a small deliberate rectangle of empty space. Let her hand return to rest before the reply about leaving it clear. End on the same empty rectangle. The scene needs neither an invented visitor nor a coat appearing in the room. |

## Rehearse the cuts and the interruptions

For each visit, seek to just before and just after a listed transition, enter on foot and interrupt a resident during their ordinary work. Compare a linear take with a pickup after the prop or actor changed state. The working hand, physical object position, listener, speaker and next eligible interaction should agree. For the two witness visits, compare hearing the exchange to passing by. For the drain, exercise the route without adding an actor. Track these as separate checks from a director's judgement of framing, timing or clarity.
