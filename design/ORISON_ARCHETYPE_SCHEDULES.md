# THE ORISON ARCHETYPE SCHEDULES

Daily, weekly and annual schedules for the eighteen residents, each one
derived from a Jungian archetype-and-shadow reading of the canonical
wound. The archetype does not generate the character — the bible already
did that. It generates the *timetable*: what hour this person wakes,
what they check, what they avoid, and what the 3 a.m. world catches them
doing.

Companion to `ORISON_BIBLE.md` §IV (identity, face, wound),
`ORISON_WARDROBE_BIBLE.md` (the three states referenced throughout) and
`ORISON_RELATIONSHIP_WEB.md` (the strata and knots the schedules keep
faith with). Where this document adds it *extends* those; it does not
contradict them.

Two disciplines govern everything below:

- **Archetypes are design lenses only.** Per bible law 7, no archetype
  label ever surfaces diegetically — no resident names one, no case file
  prints one, and no label resolves the both-true ambiguity. The
  schedules express the archetypes as behaviour and nothing else.
- **Dates marked *(proposed)* are new day-of-year keys, not yet canon.**
  They await ratification under the bible's evidence discipline. Only
  Jul 13 (the real 1977 blackout) and Mar 25 (the real Triangle fire
  date) are canon-derived.

## I. THE JUNGIAN REFERENCE

### The machinery

**PERSONA** — the adaptive mask built to meet social expectation.
Necessary equipment; pathological only when the ego identifies with it
and the person becomes their role. Stripping the persona is the first
movement of individuation. The wardrobe bible's three states are
already a persona gradient: STREET is the persona fully presented,
LATE NIGHT is the persona shed but not replaced, TOWEL is the removal
of every authored signal — and the rule about which signature item
survives into TOWEL is precisely the question *what of the persona is
actually Self*.

**SHADOW** — everything the ego disowns to keep the persona viable. It
does not disappear; it becomes an autonomous counter-personality, and
the less conscious it is, the blacker and denser it operates (Jung,
*Psychology and Religion*, CW 11). It is met in daily life through:
(a) **projection** — disproportionate, affect-loaded irritation or
fascination with the specific other person carrying the disowned trait;
(b) **compulsion** — repetitive behaviour whose energy the ego cannot
account for (checking, archiving, mending, motion);
(c) **avoidance** — of the place, hour, or act that would force contact
with the repressed material;
(d) **breakthrough at low-ego hours** — the 3 a.m. phenomena: insomnia
rumination, secret comfort behaviours, the ritual performed when nobody
is watching.

**ANIMA/ANIMUS** — the contrasexual soul-image, the bridge from ego to
the deeper unconscious. Projected, it produces fascination; possessing,
it produces moods (anima) or rigid opinionation (animus). Least directly
needed for schedules; most relevant to the Omar–Iris marriage — two
people who stopped carrying each other's projections and never took
them back — and to Malcolm's century-long carried image of Auggie.

**SELF** — the archetype of wholeness: the totality and centre of the
psyche, conscious plus unconscious. Not achieved, approached.
Individuation aims at completeness, not perfection.

**INDIVIDUATION** — the lifelong process of becoming the undivided
person one actually is: dissolve identification with the persona,
integrate the shadow, relate consciously to the anima/animus, orient
toward the Self. Behaviourally, shadow integration looks *small*:
proportionate reactions where there was compulsion; humour about
oneself; the tiny formerly-impossible act done voluntarily — sewing
one's own hem, keeping a flawed take, letting a tape end. This maps
one-to-one onto the case rules: every resident's italicised rule in the
bible is a shadow-integration sentence, and the smallest
formerly-impossible act is what a closing case looks like on a
schedule.

### The twelve archetypes (Mark & Pearson's mapping of Jung)

Format: drive / fear / shadow / routine tells (unintegrated →
integrated).

1. **INNOCENT** (Jung's Child; puer aeternus when arrested) — Drive:
   safety, happiness, doing things right. Fear: doing something wrong;
   punishment. Shadow: denial, risk-aversion, deferral to rules and
   permission, the provisional life. Routine: rituals of correctness,
   decisions converted into procedures, waiting; 3 a.m. is re-checking
   and re-reading, never deciding. Integrated: acts before certain;
   keeps one harmless wrongness on display.
2. **EVERYMAN / ORPHAN** — Drive: belong, blend, connect. Fear:
   standing out, being left out or exploited. Shadow: self-erasure,
   victimhood, cynicism, facelessness. Routine: mirrors whoever is
   present, owns nothing distinctive, keeps bags half-packed socially;
   3 a.m. is the fear of being nobody, medicated by anonymous places —
   an all-night bodega is this archetype's chapel. Integrated: claims
   one unmistakable personal mark.
3. **HERO / WARRIOR** — Drive: prove worth through courageous,
   competent action. Fear: weakness, vulnerability, standing down.
   Shadow: compulsive crusading, needing a battle, contempt for rest.
   Routine: cannot be off-duty, adopts others' emergencies,
   over-trains, under-sleeps; 3 a.m. is patrol — checking on things
   that are not theirs. Integrated: chooses battles; sleeps through an
   alarm that is genuinely someone else's.
4. **CAREGIVER** — Drive: protect, nurture, be needed. Fear:
   selfishness, ingratitude. Shadow: martyrdom, guilt-leverage,
   smothering, care-as-control — help that must first grade its object.
   Routine: everyone else's errands scheduled, own needs perpetually
   deferred; 3 a.m. is doing someone else's mending. Integrated:
   receives help; lets one imperfect thing stand uncorrected.
5. **EXPLORER / SEEKER** — Drive: freedom, the road, self found through
   movement. Fear: entrapment, conformity, and the inner emptiness
   stillness would reveal. Shadow: aimless flight, inability to commit
   or arrive, restlessness as anesthesia. Routine: never fully unpacks,
   eats standing, leaves early; 3 a.m. is walking, riding, circling the
   block. Integrated: sits down; stays; lets a place hold weight.
6. **REBEL / OUTLAW** — Drive: revolution or revenge; overturn what
   wronged them. Fear: powerlessness, being nullified. Shadow: becoming
   the perpetrator — doing to others what was done to them — masked
   sabotage, rage turned inward. Routine: operates covertly, hoards
   leverage, breaks small rules chronically; 3 a.m. is the anonymous
   act — the unsigned memo, the unlicensed sample. Integrated: revolts
   openly, in their own name.
7. **LOVER** — Drive: intimacy, devotion, union. Fear: loss, being
   unloved, the beloved's ending. Shadow: possession, jealousy,
   embalming — preserving the beloved instead of letting them live and
   change; losing self in the other. Routine: tends relics, keeps
   vigils, maintains shrines disguised as habits; 3 a.m. is watering,
   rereading, touching the kept thing. Integrated: lets love change
   form — gives away, propagates, buries.
8. **CREATOR** — Drive: give enduring form to vision. Fear: mediocrity;
   the flawed or inauthentic work. Shadow: perfectionism (destroying
   honest work), endless drafts, performing the artist instead of
   making, life dramatised rather than lived. Routine: makes and
   unmakes, curates the workspace instead of working; 3 a.m. is take
   19, or arranging the studio for an audience that isn't there.
   Integrated: releases work that shows the hand.
9. **JESTER** — Drive: live in the present moment, joy for its own
   sake. Fear: boredom; being boring. Shadow: frivolity,
   deflection-by-joke, cruelty, a wasted life. Routine: turns every
   hard moment into a bit; 3 a.m. is the laugh that avoids the sentence
   underneath. Integrated: play that includes rather than deflects.
   (The Orison contains no Jester — see below.)
10. **SAGE** — Drive: truth; understanding; the complete record. Fear:
    being deceived; ignorance; the unknowable gap. Shadow: dogmatism,
    ivory-tower detachment, analysis paralysis, observing life instead
    of living it — the record substituting for reality. Routine:
    annotates, verifies, files; experiences events primarily as future
    documentation; 3 a.m. is transcription and indexing. Integrated:
    tolerates an unannotated silence; is present without proof.
11. **MAGICIAN** — Drive: master the hidden laws; transform. Fear:
    unintended consequences; transformation escaping control. Shadow:
    manipulation, omnipotence (denial that anything is beyond
    transformation) — or the inverted form, using mastery to *prevent*
    transformation, freezing moments against their ending. Routine:
    tinkering as liturgy, systems maintained past their purpose;
    3 a.m. is the workbench and the dial. Integrated: consents to
    endings; lets one thing stay broken or die naturally.
12. **RULER** — Drive: order, control, legacy, a prosperous realm.
    Fear: chaos; being deposed; the record being wrong. Shadow:
    tyranny, rigidity, a kingdom where nothing may be touched or
    questioned, certainty enforced over memory. Routine: alignment
    rituals, inventories, white-glove inspection of a frozen domain;
    3 a.m. is straightening objects nobody uses. Integrated: lets the
    realm be used, changed, and contradicted.

### How the mapping sits on the building

- **Mirrored pairs.** The cast mapping produces shadow-pairs that track
  the relationship web exactly: Evelyn/Lena (Caregiver: control vs
  martyrdom), Mina/Sacha (Sage: record vs proof), Rhea/Iris (Creator:
  perfection vs performance), Omar/Cal (Magician: cannot-stop-fixing vs
  cannot-let-end — their shadows intersect on the one dead-air night of
  1977), Peter/Jonah (Innocent: cannot file vs cannot finish,
  bracketing the Price verdict), Juno/Nadia (Outlaw: covert theft vs
  covert war, both masked), Noel/Mae (Ruler: the frozen flat vs the
  authenticated ledger). The unpaired four — Teresa (Hero), Malcolm
  (Lover), Cam (Explorer), the Guests (Everyman) — each find their
  complement outside the archetype: Teresa's battle is Mina's silence,
  Malcolm's beloved is Sacha's ancestor, Cam's stillness is Lena's
  century, the Guests pair with the building itself.
- **The absent Jester.** No resident carries the Jester: purgatory
  contains nobody who can live in the present moment for its own sake —
  present-moment joy is precisely what a closed case would restore.
  This rhymes with the motif's empty fifth slot. Jester energy exists
  only off-premises: the Harukiya karaoke basement is where residents
  go to borrow it, and the 3 a.m. bodega is the Everyman's chapel;
  routines that send a resident to either are shadow-contact scenes.
- **3 a.m. is shadow time.** The hour the game lives in is Jung's
  low-ego hour; the wardrobe bible's LATE NIGHT state is, formally, the
  persona half-shed. Every resident's night behaviour below is their
  archetype's shadow routine; their case rule is the integration
  behaviour.
- **The player.** The bible's planned third presence is "the player's
  shadow", learning a vocabulary from resolved cases — this mapping
  supplies the eighteen shadow-vocabularies it learns from.
