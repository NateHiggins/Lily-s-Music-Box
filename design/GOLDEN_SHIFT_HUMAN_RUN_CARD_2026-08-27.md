# Golden shift — human run card

**Print this. Keep it beside the machine. Fill it in as you go.**

---

## SCOPE — read this first

**Fresh campaign. Curb to waking in your own room. One sitting.**

- **No console, no debug panel, no noclip.**
- **No source knowledge, no walkthrough, no map, no notes from anyone.**
- **No coaching.** Nobody explains anything to you, before or during.
- **Stop at the first transition that is missing or genuinely unclear.**

You are not testing whether the game is good. You are finding **the first place
a person cannot tell what to do next.** Getting stuck is the result. If you
finish smoothly that is also a result, and a rarer one.

**If you are being watched, the watcher stays silent.** That is not rudeness. It
is the entire experiment.

## THE TWO JOBS — they are not the same run

**K2 — ONE UNINTERRUPTED WALK.**
Fresh save, curb to wake, no saving and reloading, no restarting. **It ends
either at the wake or at the first blocked transition, whichever comes first.**

**K3 — ELEVEN SEPARATE SAVE/RELOAD CHECKS.**
At each beat below, save and quit at the stated moment, relaunch, and check the
three things listed. **Eleven independent checks, not one walk.**

**Do K2 first, completely, then K3.** Doing them together ruins both: saving
mid-walk breaks K2's continuity, and walking past a boundary breaks K3's
isolation.

**Automated suites may support K3. They cannot replace it.** A green test is not
one of the eleven checks.

---

## STOP RULES

1. **No hint after confusion begins.** The moment you are unsure, that is the
   data. Anyone who helps you has ended the run.
2. **A failure is a failure.** Do not write "passed, but…" or "passed once I
   realised…". If you had to realise something, it did not pass.
3. **During K2, stop collecting at the first blocked transition.** Write it up
   and stop. Do not push on to gather more defects — later observations from a
   confused player are not evidence.
4. **A K3 reconstruction failure must name which of the three broke:** the
   immediate practical intention, the owner of the job or conversation, or the
   physical state of the world.
5. **Stop immediately** if the game crashes, if a developer overlay or floating
   debug text appears, or if you want to stop for any reason at all. A stopped
   run is not a failed run.

## NOTES FOR WHOEVER SETS THIS UP

**Current, and corrected against older paperwork:**

- **Frame time on the primary machine is now proved.** All eleven playable
  stations pass the 1440p budget. **The tester does not measure performance.**
  Report stutter, hitching or a slideshow as an observation; do not chase
  numbers.
- **Two detached composition cameras remain over budget. They are not gameplay
  and are not failures.** Nothing the tester can walk to is one of them.
- **A second machine is still unproved.** That is a separate gate, and the
  carriageway is its first retest. **This card does not close it.**
- **Only the gradual sleep onset exists on this route.** The sudden form is not
  enabled and **must never appear as a tester instruction.** If a tester asks,
  the answer is that this route has one form.
- **The save/reload matrix is manual and open.** Automated suites may support
  it; they do not close it.
- **Run K2 at default settings** — that is what a player gets. Toggling the
  gradual-warning setting on and off is a different, separate check and does not
  belong in this run.

**Routing a failure:** one observed failure becomes **one bounded task**, named
after the transition that was missing. Not a redesign, not a list.
