# SIX INCARNATIONS

*Owner-approved design plan, 2026-08-20. The owner approved the plan, Cal fifth,
Omar sixth, and the exclusion of identifiable people and the faceless figure
from reflected-world plates. This
document expands the owner-approved V14 contract in
`design/DREAM_FAUNA_BRIEF.md`. It designs presentation only. The
case profiles, spatial verbs, topology, danger, pursuit, save facts and waking
case loop remain owned by their existing systems.*

## 1. The invariant body

The six release prints are one intelligence making six intimate translations.
Every incarnation therefore keeps the same near-black aubergine ground, wine
dark-life, lamp-woken gold, emerald/carnelian/lapis stones, three continuous
irradiance bands and inherited genome. What changes is how those common tokens
behave when she tries to say *I see you* in the resident's own material
language.

The procedural layer remains semantic and authoritative. It draws the dark
anatomy, oblique veins, cloisonne partitions, danger eyes, motif rhythm,
gutter response, exposure order and every survival-readable edge. Static
AI-derived plates provide substance inside those procedural regions. They may
be warped, recolored and blended; they may never decide what a mark means.

There are no new chromatic accents in this plan. Peter's oxblood is an already
landed case cue and remains a dark red decision material, not a fourth jewel.
Cal's amber is warm gold through dial glass, not orange. Omar's steel is a
desaturated substance. This deliberately leaves no color ruling open.

### Reading order shared by all six

1. Darkness owns most of the frame. Dark-neon anatomy is the lowest readable
   layer and never exceeds the reserved danger eye.
2. Oblique light reveals depth under the line: fibers, weave, lacquer,
   membrane, glass or metal fatigue become locally translucent without making
   the whole room glow.
3. A sustained full beam earns molten gold. The surface event grows from the
   same exposure field and returns continuously when the gutter sags.
4. Retained exposure remembers attention; it never changes collision,
   topology, a case verb or a pursuit fact.
5. All autonomous motion is deterministic, smooth, below 1 Hz and free of
   forced camera movement.

## 2. Mina Vale — THE MERCY OF THE UNMARKED

**Love letter.** She learns Mina's exacting hand well enough to leave one
beautiful thing unnamed.

### a. Surface language

- **Pattern species — editorial palimpsest.** Procedural baselines, crop marks,
  nested brackets and erased caption boxes organize the common tesserae. No
  generated plate contains actual lettering.
- **Dark-neon topology.** Wine handwriting gestures run toward visible object
  boundaries, stop just short, and close into empty leader lines. Lapis is the
  sparse stone, used only at terminal dots.
- **Oblique tissue.** Light enters black ink bleed and capillary paper fibers;
  annotations appear to sit one membrane below the plaster. The subsurface
  edge feathers like a correction wicked through damp stock.
- **Molten behavior.** Gold boils outward from the ink rather than over it.
  Marked cells clarify, loosen, and evacuate into broad dark blankness while
  their gold rims remain as evidence that something was deliberately spared.

### b. Token rotation

Wine cools toward dried blue-black ink; gold is pale leaf over warm paper;
lapis leads, emerald is rare, carnelian is nearly absent. No new accent.

### c. Gutter and exposure

A lamp sag makes leader lines retract toward their anchors without erasing
them. Recovery does not immediately rewrite the empty area: retained exposure
increases the radius of calm blankness over the run. The motion is a slow
inhale/exhale, never crawling text and never coupled to Mina's annotation verb.

### d. The five fauna

- **Gilder's Buttons:** small sealing-wax dots whose empty centers refuse a
  label.
- **Tessellates:** paper-fiber grazers plated in cropped, illegible letterform
  fragments.
- **Wine Anemones:** ink blooms whose tendrils end in clean proofreader loops.
- **Ribbonettes:** paired strips resembling loosened correction tape, with no
  readable marks.
- **Loupe:** a black editorial lens with one carnelian anatomical iris; its
  rim reveals fibers, never words.

### e. Signature impossible moment

In a fully exposed room, every correction line lifts a few millimeters as
vertex-only gold wire, curls inward, and sinks through the existing surface.
The result is a large, perfectly bounded patch of unmarked black. Geometry,
collision and the annotation system do not change.

### f. Tenderness

The blank patch keeps the faint pressure dent of a palm that chose not to
write. It catches oblique gold only when the player stops moving.

## 8. Shared implementation architecture

### 8.1 Profile-to-presentation seam

`game/data/dream_profiles.json` gains one optional `presentation` dictionary.
The runtime validates it when profiles load and reduces it to a bounded,
presentation-only value object:

```text
presentation
  incarnation_id          mina | peter | juno | mae | cal | omar
  production_enabled      bool
  palette                 token indices and bounded temperature/value offsets
  pattern                 species, scale, line weight, branch, interference
  irradiance              dark/oblique/molten gains and blend-shape controls
  motion                  sub-Hz rates, phase offsets, gutter response
  substance               four approved runtime plate-set keys
  reflected_world         one approved equirectangular plate key
  signature               bounded shader event id and thresholds
  fauna                    five bounded costume parameter records
```

Absent `presentation` means the exact current look. Invalid fields reject the
profile; they never fall back to a different case. `production_enabled` is a
hard data gate in addition to campaign order: Cal and Omar remain false until
the owner rules. Presentation data cannot activate a case or create a dream.

`DreamMazeRoot` already owns the active profile. During build it passes one
immutable presentation record to the existing room/material collector and the
existing `DreamFaunaDirector`. Those owners set uniforms on their current
materials. There is no case switch in process code, no case-specific node and
no new signal. The save continues to store only the existing profile id.

### 8.2 One maintained shader path

Architecture, lineage and fauna keep their current shader files and common
irradiance include. Add one shared incarnation include containing bounded
functions selected by a numeric incarnation id. Each function returns only
pattern, palette, substance-blend and vertex-offset values. The compiler can
retain all six small branches, but exactly one uniform id is constant for a
run; no material or shader file is forked.

The signature events are visual thresholds evaluated from retained exposure,
gutter energy, genome and a deterministic run clock. They may offset existing
vertices or shade existing fragments. They return no event to GDScript and
cannot mutate the Atlas, room graph, hazards, pursuit, channels, annotations,
provenances or saves.

### 8.3 Analytic UV and anti-repetition stack

Each existing surface supplies body coordinates already available to its
shader: architecture uses stable world/room coordinates; tubes use
`param/theta`; fauna use their vertex channels plus instance genome. Sampling
then follows one fixed stack:

1. normalize coordinates by the plate's authored meters-per-tile;
2. add a genome-derived, static per-room/per-instance offset and quarter-turn;
3. apply low-frequency domain warp capped below 8% of one tile;
4. blend primary and secondary frequencies at an irrational ratio;
5. composite only inside procedural region masks;
6. apply state-specific recolor, normal/height influence and molten flow.

Derivatives use `textureGrad` where the warp would otherwise break mip choice.
No temporal UV noise is allowed. A long uninterrupted sample is a failed mask,
not a reason to increase noise.

### 8.4 Loading lifecycle

1. Dream build validates the active profile and resolves its five runtime sets.
2. A shared presentation resource cache loads those sets before the first dream
   frame and publishes one immutable bundle to existing collectors.
3. Only the active incarnation is referenced. Other case textures have no live
   Resource references and are not preloaded by script or scene.
4. On accepted wake, the root clears material texture uniforms, releases the
   bundle and lets the ordinary deferred resource lifetime finish before the
   waking scene resumes.
5. Re-entry of the same seeded run resolves the same keys and parameters.

Loading failure is fail-closed: the current procedural look remains usable,
the missing set is logged once, and no other incarnation is substituted.

### 8.5 Ingest and custody checklist

- Generated sources live only in ignored `art/textures/ai_sources/`.
- Accepted source names map explicitly to
  `T_ai_dream_<case-or-family>_<substance>`; no unknown source is guessed.
- Shipped derivatives and a `SOURCE.md` per set are committed under the dream
  runtime asset namespace. The note records prompt, generator, date, crop,
  scale, derivation recipe and prohibited-content review.
- Ingest produces albedo, height, normal and roughness; it never bakes semantic
  lines, eyes, text or rhythm.
- The runtime-import sidecar fixer covers every derivative. Its `--check` mode
  must pass after a fresh Godot import; mipmaps and anisotropic filtering are
  mandatory.
- Every set passes dimensions, color-space, channel, naming, seam, visible
  tiling and content-prohibition checks.
- Lossless source derivatives remain the default. Compression requires a
  measured desktop memory or bandwidth problem and a new A/B artifact review.

## 9. Proof plan

### Machine contract

- schema rejects unknown ids, out-of-range rates, missing set keys and any
  production-enabled Cal/Omar record;
- profiles with no presentation block reproduce the current parameter bundle;
- each active profile reaches architecture, lineage and all five fauna through
  the shared collectors, with no case-specific owner;
- fixed seed plus fixed clock yields bit-identical parameters and stable image
  deltas within the renderer's established A/A floor;
- darkness < oblique < molten luma, while fauna remain below danger;
- all autonomous rates are below 1 Hz and transitions have smooth bounded
  slopes;
- presentation changes no room plan, collision, hazard, pursuit, attention,
  annotation, provenance, channel or save fact;
- only one case bundle is resident; wake releases it; substitutions never occur;
- submitted object/draw counts are unchanged and dream frame time remains under
  16.6 ms at the established production stations.

### Rendered production proof per licensed case

1. Equal-interval A/A control with the same fixed camera, seed, lamp phase and
   exposure.
2. Dark, oblique and sustained-full-beam contact sheet from the production
   dream root.
3. Five equal exposure blends proving continuity and ordered luma.
4. Steady/gutter pair plus long-shadow read.
5. Signature moment before/peak/after without a geometry or collision delta.
6. All five fauna together, showing family continuity and case costume.
