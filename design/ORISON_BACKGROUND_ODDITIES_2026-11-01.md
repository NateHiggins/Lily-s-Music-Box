# Four background oddities for the year

Evidence class: **INERT**. Writers' room flavor, 2026-11-01, chosen by Lily from a list of ten. First draft for her read.
All of it is invented fiction. The places marked real are real places only. Not in the Bible.

These are the world's small unexplained things, seen at the edge of the papers and in gossip. They never connect to each
other, the case, the Hum, WORS 1610, the infection or the Tenant, and nothing ever explains them.

| Oddity | What it is | Where it runs |
|---|---|---|
| **The Unlisted Stop** | A lit, green-tiled platform with a bench and a clock, seen for half a second in the Steinway Tunnel (real) on the Corona line. The Interborough denies it, and later paints a stretch of tunnel grey | Papers; **co_stop** |
| **The Sixty-First Pigeon** | The Maspeth Homing Club flies sixty and clocks in sixty-one. The hen's band reads "Loft 9, Q." There is no Loft 9. Raced as the Extra, she wins nine races | Papers; **mr_bird** |
| **The Society for the Prevention** | Has met monthly since 1871 to prevent something it won't name. Every 6 November it prints one word: "Prevented." | Classifieds in both papers; **pw_society** |
| **The Ash Man of Corona** | A tall party of cinders who sorts the Corona ash dumps (real) at dusk. The Department finds the labour satisfactory | Papers; **ir_ash** |

**What's built.** Eight new back pages, 32 columns, in **game/data/newspapers/**, and four first-draft threads. Nothing else
reads them, and no existing item or ear is edited.

**Rules that keep them flavor.**

- No clue, case flag, carrier or O2 line. The threads grant no flags.
- Nothing prints from 14 to 21 July or on the 20 August and 16 September memorial days.
- The pigeon's band is a name, not figures, so it can't read as WORS. Nobody decodes it.
- The Society's near thing on 14 March is never explained and touches no resident.
- **The grey paint is the tunnel's, not a coat.** The man in the grey coat stays the Arcade's.
- Nobody here sees or sorts anything from the Orison's ash pit, so plate 13 is untouched.
