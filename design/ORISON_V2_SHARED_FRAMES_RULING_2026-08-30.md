# Orison v2 shared frames ruling — 2026-08-30

This document records the machine-readable rulings lifted into
**orison_v2_shared_frames.json**. It does not claim spatial proof.

- The threshold of the Orison front door is the neighbourhood origin. Forward
  into the building is positive Z. **building_layout.json** remains the sole
  metric authority until a separately authorized cutover.
- Commercial identities use **SHOP_**. The hero bodega is
  **SHOP_BODEGA**; legacy labels are aliases, not parallel authorities.
- Campaign creation samples the real local date and time exactly once. That
  captured epoch is independent of later host time. Simulation elapsed minutes
  then advance the calendar durably; changing the computer clock cannot change
  presence, schedules, earned knowledge, or dream-night passage.
- Anomaly binding is indexed by space. Era influence and resident wound are
  independent columns. Era influence pulls; winner-plus-trace resolves
  competition. There is no combined accessor.
- The approved isolated exterior homes are **game/data/orison_v2/exterior/**
  for authoritative records, **game/assets/building/orison_v2/exterior/** for
  generated runtime assets, and **art/generated/orison_v2/exterior/** for
  staging. Promotion must be explicit and hash-gated. Neither production
  layout record nor any existing floor asset is an output target.

The campaign clock deliberately does not prescribe a fictional start date.
The owner's ruling is real-time creation: actual local date/time is captured at
new campaign creation, then the simulation becomes independent.
