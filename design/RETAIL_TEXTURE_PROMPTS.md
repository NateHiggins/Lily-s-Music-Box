# RETAIL & STREET TEXTURE PROMPTS (task #49)

Six surfaces the new spaces stand on that currently wear stand-in flat
colours. Drop each under the exact stem in `art/textures/ai_sources/`;
SLOTS entries follow once delivered. All are TILING swatches except
where marked. References on file: the Harukiya bar (Akira) and the
Half Baked corner store.

## 1. `red_steel_shelving.png` — 0.8 m  (bodega gondolas + wall shelves)

```
Seamless tileable texture of cheap corner-store steel shelving enamel,
bright signal red gone dull with age, brush-touched-up in a slightly
wrong red in patches, chipped at pressed edges and screw holes exposing
grey zinc metal beneath, fine scratches from decades of cans being slid,
a few small rust blooms at chips, light dust film. Wear evenly
distributed, no single dominant blemish. Orthographic top-down view,
flat lay, flat even diffuse lighting, no shadows, no highlights, no
specular, base color map only. Seamless, infinitely wrapping on all four
edges, no landmarks, no watermark, no logo, no text. Square 1:1, high
resolution, macro detail. Coverage: 0.8 metres across.
```
