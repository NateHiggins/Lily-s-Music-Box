# Fitted hand-laundry cloth and ironing furniture

Evidence class: **INERT**

Classification: **ADAPTATION**. The original Model Laundry trade source in
**art/data/shop_interiors.py** owns forty wrapped parcels, nine shirts on a
rail, an ironing table, its padded top and a sleeve board. Those 53 exact
source records, original material identities and source rectangles remain
in the editable native. New geometry replaces their outward box boundaries.
Shop actors, hours, counters, keys, stock and utility ownership stay with
their existing production owners.

**scripts/build_laundry_fittings.py** makes 370 individually closed positive
stocks: wrapped bundles with seams and closed string loops; shirts with
folded sleeves, cuffs, collars, plackets and buttons on timber hangers;
curved metal hooks; a tubular rail, suspension rods and ceiling plates;
worked table legs, aprons and top; padded forms and a sleeve-board tongue.
The native inspector checks each stock is manifold and connected, and each
of the 53 assembly graphs has one component at a 20-micrometre surface
contact tolerance. This is contact evidence, not structural capacity.

The parcel undersides bridge the source's 5-mm shelf gap. The table top
bridges the 10-mm gap below its pad. The sleeve-board tongue enters the
pad edge, and two rail suspension plates terminate on the authored 3.3-m
ceiling. Hangers extend above the original shirt envelope to wrap the rail.
These are declared fitting adaptations, not changes to the protected layout.

**laundry_fittings.blend** retains the source boxes and closed construction.
The export has 99 bounded material partitions and 83,928 triangles. Physical
boundaries use those same triangles. Exactly 636 original box triangles are
removed; every other source vertex attribute, material slot and triangle
index is retained. The shared fitter also continues to mount shop seating,
including streamed retirement and reconstruction.

New cloth uses the existing runtime catalogue's **linen** maps and its
locked 0.6-metre scale. Close-up inspection rejected the shipping **linen_b**
variant's large creases for these shirts. Paper, timber and chrome retain
their original shipping variants. Materials are cloned locally; the 60
catalogue definitions, 15 visual locks, texture files, global shaders and
lamp calibration are unchanged. Native materials use the same relative
images, metre charts and normal strength as the installed variants.

**scripts/inspect_laundry_fittings.py** verifies source/map hashes, native
stock volumes and connected joins and renders twelve front/rear views.
**OrisonV2LaundryFittingsTest** checks the imported partitions, UV derivatives,
literal retention of other source attributes, collision and 57 support
contacts, with paired production and clear shirt-detail views. Wrapper run
receipts and these views are inert; no ledger requirement is promoted.

The architecture inspection also resolved a misleading cropped view of the
C apartment beds. All 22 installed beds already use the native bedding
library, including eight completion-template beds. **OrisonV2BeddingTest**
now covers all 22, 88 physical contacts and the twelve shared role meshes,
with additional clear views in the C bedrooms.

Wash tubs, mangle, sad irons, pulley airers, ticket presentation and rear
workshop fittings remain open. Broad roof/weather, service routing, terrain,
apartment furniture and texture acceptance continues. Heating cutouts remain
parked. This batch does not declare the building complete.
