# Calibrated local roof-hardware finishes

Evidence class: **INERT**

Native close-ups exposed oversized crust on collector bronze and rough concrete
appearance on small galvanized fittings. Same-camera temporary studies replace
only the local maps, preserving the existing physical tile scales and metallic
response. Final native and production-lamp inspection is required for acceptance.

## Catalogue contract

| Local finish | Installation | Physical tile / relief | Construction owns |
|---|---|---|---|
| **bronze_sheet** | Sixteen original curved collectors | 0.6 m / 0.06 mm | Closed 4 mm sheets, attachment brackets and blind feed sockets |
| **galvanized_roof** | Original masts/guys, aerial metal stocks, tank metal stocks and beacon fittings | 0.9 m / 0.05 mm | Native joints, flanges, bolts, thin weather covers and supports |

Deterministic periodic maps contain albedo, roughness, height and physically
derived normal data. They contain no lettering, photographs, baked illumination,
bitmap joints, wood-exposing paint chips or inflated crust. **art/tools** holds
the three generators; **art/textures/procedural** holds their four source maps,
scale metadata and hashes. The shared catalogue/mapping owns each new key.
The runtime generator adds only optical policy; canonical runtime textures and
force-tracked mipmapped import settings ship together.

The existing **bronze**, **metal** and **lacquer_red** definitions and maps stay
unchanged. All fifteen visual locks retain their exact specifications. Local
MatLib duplicates use native metre charts; the shared triplanar cache remains
intact. Existing geometry/normal/pose equality must be demonstrated for all
three refitted hardware exports. A changed material suffix is not a moved stock.

## Verification and limits

Temporary comparison studies are under **tmp/city-beacons** and are bound to
their source native files. They establish the reason for local repair, not
runtime acceptance. Rebuild all consumed catalogue/reference bindings in order,
reopen the four actual native sources, and inspect the production lamp at close,
assembly and skyline scales. The production suites require all three exact maps,
finite metre UV derivatives/tangents, bounded culling cells and identical visible
and physical faces. Support/clearance tolerances stay unchanged.

Exact final candidate, receipts, reviewed images, comparison results and
publication status belong to **tmp/city-beacons**. Acceptance stays pending until
that report is complete. Broader masonry, rooms, weather closure, drainage and
material families remain open. No global light, shader, service owner, door/key
behavior, protected layout or Mina asset is redesigned by this surface repair.
