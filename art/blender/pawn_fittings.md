# Pawnbroker pledge furniture and counter

Evidence class: **INERT**. Classification: **ADAPTATION**.

Forty immutable source boxes become nine assemblies: a four-tier parcel rack,
upright violin case, shelf-mounted sewing machine, hanging coat, grille counter,
safe, covered balance, ledger and corded loupe. The native contains 308 closed
stocks, 33 material partitions and 44,672 triangles. Runtime retires exactly
480 source triangles and checks 23 floor or furniture contacts.

The rack retains the four deck heights and 24 original parcel owners. Six posts
reach the original oak floor; parcels lower 5mm onto their decks. A bracketed
upper shelf supports the static machine. The violin case stands in a floor
cradle. The hollow coat faces the operator aisle, narrowed 15% across its
shoulders to fit between the rack and party wall; its peg terminates in a post.

retaining its 1.18m worktop. Actual brass bars surround an open fixed wicket.

bay at X=20.90..21.68, Y=-54.72..-53.88. Feet meet the 0.01m floor. The fitted
door, dial and handles are passive and add no custody or opening mechanism.

The balance, ledger and loupe sit on separate worktop stations. The bell has
a closed 4mm glass shell and empty interior. Bell and loupe borrow unchanged
shipping glazing maps, then reuse the display's local clear dielectric. Other
finishes use existing catalogue maps and metre UVs; dark enamel and cloth use
local tint multipliers. No new material keys, images or lettering are generated.

Build **scripts/build_pawn_fittings.py**, then run **inspect_pawn_fittings.py**
in one Blender batch. The inspector loads the retained shop/passage shell and
accepted display/clock geometry; it checks joins, positive manifold stocks,
intersections, contact rays and metre charts before nine renders. Production
module **pawn_fittings** in **OrisonV2FabricationBatch.tscn** checks retirement,
map ownership, imported tangent bases, physical geometry, supports, glass
thickness, wicket opening and sampled standing clearances. Both passage load
paths install the same fitting; the residency test checks reconstruction.
