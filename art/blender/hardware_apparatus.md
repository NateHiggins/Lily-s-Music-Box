Evidence class: **INERT**

balance, an unloaded paint-shaker frame and a mixing paddle. Fabrication
contains **53** closed, connected stocks, **5** bounded material partitions
and **13,280** triangles. Exact-boundary replacement removes **48** outward
original triangles; original reference boxes and all other source arrays and
material objects retain their owners. Both passage loading paths install the

floor bearings carry a cast bed and joined central pedestal. A brass pivot,
beam, six hangers and two open pans are actual geometry. Their inner floors

between the original separate base and beam blocks.

parts; this work claims no weighing, agitation or live electrical capacity.

floor. A small lean seats its shaft on the shaker crown's actual east face at

leg. The paddle shifts **0.28 metres toward the shop rear** along the same
crown, exposing its blade beyond the paint-bench end rather than wedging it
between the bench leg and shaker. This is an explicit contact adaptation; the original hidden paddle
reference keeps its pose. Ten contacts cover both apparatus and paddle.
