# Household toasters and medicine cabinets

Evidence class: **INERT**

Ten source toasters use two native mechanism variants and one crumb prototype:
169 closed stocks, 23 partitions and 41,144 triangles. Twelve medicine cabinets
use two handed cases and ten household item prototypes: 189 closed stocks,
33 partitions and 48,260 triangles. The original accessory roster, support
anchors, seeded contents, interaction owners and audio remain authoritative.

Toaster stock includes formed vented shells, insulated heater supports, moving
carriers and levers, a fitted withdrawal pan, braided lead and seated loose plug.
The original carrier and lever have different strokes; their complete release
overshoot and busy wiggle are checked independently. The fully withdrawn pan
is the existing hand-held service pose. Eighteen source crumbs per household
retain their seeded dimensions, footprint, yaw and colour, with bottoms seated
on the actual pan or grease film. Fifty installed counter bearings and thirty
continuous external movement envelopes pass native inspection.

Cabinets have thin folded cases, supported glass shelves, rebated hinge stiles,
annular moving knuckles, fixed pins and the original 95-degree pivot. The 28
source household items retain their inventory identities and seeded footprint;
their native heel or guard feet sit on the actual shelf tops. The complete
source jitter/yaw range fits the case. Forty-eight wall bearings include the
existing 4B wall extension. Twelve continuous world leaf sweeps and 46 local
hinge pair checks pass. All lettering remains Label3D; no bitmap lettering is
introduced. Ordinary brown bottle material ownership remains source-owned.

The original MirrorGlass node, reflection layer, fallback handoff and shared
live renderer remain. The V2 collision body receives its original hinge's
global transform explicitly: rotating an ancestor alone left the animatable
physics body closed. No original prop authority script is changed.

Build, inspection and rendering scripts use the normal family naming convention
under **art/blender/scripts**. Native motion inspectors precede import. Review
all changed render states before importing; exact export accessor hashes allow
unchanged views to be reused when a single prototype changes. Capture candidate
rejections retain collider paths and source-local ray coordinates for diagnosis.
