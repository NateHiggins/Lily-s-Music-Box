## Construction and source

**art/data/city_beacons/source_plan.json** supplies the bounded fabrication and
fixture camera offsets. **scripts/build_city_beacons.py** checks current city
registration against all 335 saved closed masses and reads the saved fabricated
mast trees. Each housing uses four closed stocks: a fitted pedestal, lower
weather lip, red tapered body and closed upper weather crown. Exact unions retain
one closed connected material volume. Four-metre material partitions carry
native metre charts, explicit tangents and matching triangle collision.

The pedestal footprint uses 74 percent of the original mast radius. Its centre
and 64 circular edge stations must bear on the actual saved mast-head plane
within 100 micrometres. All seventeen installations require 1,105 native and
production bearing samples. The source red hue informs **beacon_lacquer**;
**galvanized_roof** supplies the metal fittings. The local finish record is
**city_hardware_finishes.md**. No lettering, photograph or wood-trim damage is
baked into the housing maps.

The native source links the retained city and mast references with portable
same-directory paths. Generator, plan, reference, catalogue contract and each
calibrated map are hash-bound. Bound text uses LF-normalized hashes and **-text**;
PNG, Blender and GLB bytes are hashed unchanged. Build masts/aerials, then tanks,
then beacons after a catalogue change. Generated portable meshes are never edited.

## Inspection and management report

The first production run records 417 misses with 3 mm ray brackets on narrow
mast-cap triangles. An actual-world diagnostic compares 3, 10, 30, 100, 200 and
500 mm reaches: every longer bracket hits all 1,105 target stations with the
same maximum 47.7 micrometre displacement. Final checks require independent
10 and 30 mm brackets, each meeting the original 100 micrometre bearing tolerance.
No target, collider exclusion or contact tolerance is loosened. The source
radius also uses a 10 nanometre numerical comparison because subtraction of
distant original rectangle endpoints introduces ordinary floating-point rounding.

Bidirectional stock edges initially report the original mast-cap edges touching
the pedestal underside. Only edges lying wholly in that exact source-owned
backing plane (within 0.2 micrometres) are recorded as bearing contacts. Edges
above/below it and all other hardware intersections remain failures. The checked
native sources pass 547,749 edges with zero penetrations and 84 recorded plane
contacts. **tmp/city-beacons** retains the initial diagnostics and corrected checks.
