# Source-fitted Harukiya stage curtains

Evidence class: **INERT**

Classification: **ADAPTATION**. The sixteen original drape boxes and original
pelmet remain authoritative source records. Their thick plaster material and
depth masked the stage sign and entered the platform. The native installation
derives its fit from those records, the retained wall, floor, concrete dado and
measured bounds of the complete existing neon hardware. No source wall, floor,
sign, piano, speaker, microphone or original marker moves.

**OrisonV2BarStageFit** measures the existing sign and preserves the three stage
signal markers. Run through the Godot lane and pass its ignored
**tmp/bar-stage/source-fit.json** to **scripts/prepare_bar_stage.py --probe**.
Preparation checks source identities, positions, orientation and numerical
serialization, then freezes exact source values and LF-normalized bindings in
**art/data/bar_stage/source_fit.json**. **scripts/build_bar_stage.py** refuses
changed bindings and supports an isolated output through **BAR_STAGE_OUT**.

The native has **118 positive closed construction stocks**: a two-millimetre
folded sheet, sewn header and lower hem, thirty-two sewn tabs and threaded rings,
cloth-covered timber fascia, rail, three wall plates, collars, bearing arms and
embedded anchors/screws. Upper cloth clears the full measured sign; the lower
cloth bends forward above the concrete dado and ends ten millimetres above the
original floor. The rail's sixteen-millimetre diameter is physically seated in
the collars; the plate backs start on the retained wall. This is geometric
fabrication, not a structural-capacity calculation or moving curtain mechanism.

Three shipping material partitions contain **36,340 triangles**. Cloth uses a
continuous unfolded arc-length chart with separate front/back layer offsets;
the sewn header gathers that chart. The constant lower pleats retain horizontal
fibre length within one percent. Iron and wood have physical metre charts and
checked imported tangent derivatives. Existing **fabric_warm**, **iron_blackened**
and **wood_dark** catalogue maps and optical settings remain; cloth receives a
local red tint. No new bitmap, lettering, material key or shared projection change
is introduced. The inherited woven finish remains subject to broader cloth review.

The stage wrapper uses the existing source-ownership adapter. It retires exactly
**204 triangles** from seventeen original boxes, preserving all **48** restroom
triangles and every unselected serialized vertex/normal/tangent/UV buffer.
The remaining source collider is rebuilt from its retained faces. Each native
partition has one collider made from its exact visible triangles. Precise import
and disabled generated LODs preserve the small joins.

**scripts/inspect_bar_stage.py** reopens the saved native, independently checks
all stocks and **78 actual wall/plate/rail/collar samples**, and tests every
cloth triangle against retained imported bar triangles after removing only the
seventeen replaced boundaries. Four original/delivery pairs use Blender-only
review illumination. The rear bearing pair is explicitly a metal-only cutaway;
cloth and wood are hidden there to expose the rail support.

**OrisonV2BarStageTest** checks installed ownership, charts, exact colliders,
**54** wall/plate samples and actual cloth corner/centroid clearance samples in
production physics. It verifies the original sign bounds and marker transforms,
eight-glyph text owner and motif response. Four ordinary standing views use
unchanged production practical lights and player lamp. Captures and wrapper
**suite_run** receipts are inspection evidence, not schema-2 **runtime_contract**
proof or completeness-ledger promotion.

Scoped logs, native inspection and captures are under **tmp/bar-stage**. The
inherited sign cabinet exceeds the rear wall/ceiling band; that original fit,
piano/microphone/PA fabrication, final cloth finish, independent shop services,
heating shell readiness and wider V2 acceptance remain open.
