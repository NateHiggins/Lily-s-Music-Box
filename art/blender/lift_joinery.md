# V2 fitted cab joinery

Evidence class: **INERT**

**art/blender/scripts/build_lift_joinery.py** generates the editable
**art/blender/lift_joinery.blend** and **game/assets/props/lift_joinery.glb**.
Three fitted backing boards replace bare gaps around eight raised oak panels.
The panel molding uses continuous mitered rings and recessed grooves, rather
than stacked boxes. Side backings butt against the rear backing. A flat front
stile keeps the cab controls clear of molding; actual bores accommodate their
two existing lower mounting stems. The handrail mounting course stays exposed.

Four rear enamel sections finish the wall around the live mirror, with an
actual opening matching its outer frame. Existing oak and enamel materials,
car collision, controls, handrail mounts, moving body and saves remain owners.
Only the original lower-panel visuals are hidden. V1 keeps its presentation.

OrisonV2LiftJoineryTest probes imported triangles at fifteen wall stations,
compares them to actual car collision walls, checks both mounting bores and
the handrail/mirror openings, and captures the finished cab. Passenger route,
live mirror and title/Continue suites cover production integration. Receipts
live under **tmp/lift-joinery**; no runtime ledger promotion is claimed.
