# Supported city rooftop masts and guys

Evidence class: **INERT**

Classification: **ADAPTATION**. This restores 25 masts and 75 guy spans already
authored in the immutable city layout. It does not create occupied buildings,
utility connections or controls. The other 140 aerial components and 98 tank
components remain separate work. Heating cutouts remain parked.

## Authority and fitted construction

**art/data/building_layout.json** supplies the original endpoints, radii and
building groups. **art/data/city_masts/source_plan.json** supplies the bounded
stock dimensions. **scripts/build_city_masts.py** reads the actual saved
**city_shells.blend** and seats plates on its faces. That city source and its
335 original closed masses are unchanged.

The historical registration's whole-blockout hash predates later service ports.
The generator rederives every consumed offset from the current blockout and
exterior regions, compares those offsets with the saved registration, and
checks all 335 actual solid bounds within 20 micrometres. The current fixture
binds the actual source bytes. The old registration receipt is preserved.

Each mast has a 160 mm square, 12 mm thick plate, four hexagonal bolt heads and
a fitted collar. Each guy terminates in a supported plate, short post and
ferrule. Original mast upper endpoints, guy upper endpoints and wire radii
remain. Nominal and fitted lower endpoints are both recorded. Guys that aimed
beyond a stepped roof now seat on an actual supported face. Full plate
footprints fit inside flat native faces beyond their bevel setbacks; guy
spans clear the retained city solids between the mast and anchor.

The editable native file retains **675 closed positive stocks** and **25 closed
fabricated trees**, each with one connected component. Boolean fabrication
joins the wire ends, collars, plates and bolts. Retained city faces own the
exact plate backing planes. Runtime triangles are clipped into four metre
cells, with actual matching triangle collision and metre charts. The installed
export contains **223 parts / 54,397 triangles**, recorded by the final fixture
and candidate run.

## Mapping and inspection

The local material duplicate reuses the existing catalogued **metal** albedo,
roughness and normal maps, 0.9 metre tile and 0.35 normal strength. Native maps
use relative paths; the shared MatLib projection and visual locks remain.
No new bitmap, global lighting or shader is introduced. The existing coarse
metal appearance remains a broader material-review finding.

Very thin Boolean-joint triangles need local UV origins and double-precision,
edge-aligned charts. A scoped Blender export extension derives standard
tangents from each chart's native guide and exported normal, then removes the
authoring guide. It reads the completed primitive because custom attributes
can precede the normal callback. No generated glTF is edited after export.
Strict derivative, handedness and metre-scale checks cover every imported
partition without a skipped face or relaxed tolerance.

Both mast and retained-city import sidecars disable position compression.
The original city geometry bytes and UID remain. Its compressed import moved
50 bearing planes by 100–404 micrometres; full precision restores their actual
native seats while preserving the 100 micrometre runtime contact tolerance.

Saved-source inspection reopens the native file and verifies closed volumes,
connected fabrication, source bindings, partition bounds and relative maps.
It renders assembly, base and anchor views with read-only city context.
The installed test checks 100 plate centres plus 400 corners against original
city collision, 75 clear guy spans and four paired production camera views.
The initial focused installed run passes **2,364 checks** with zero failures.
Final verification also compares every collision triangle and actual world
pose with its visible partition, retaining all 84 original city-shell checks.
Street visibility is limited by the nearer buildings. Render counters are
observations, not frame-rate proof. Normal walking and retained behavior are
checked separately by the candidate suites.
