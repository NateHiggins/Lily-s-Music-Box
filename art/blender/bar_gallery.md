preparation script with **--probe**. Numeric serialization differences are checked within
ten nanometres before replacing copied source records with their exact original
JSON values. **scripts/build_bar_gallery.py** refuses changed input bindings.

wood packers per frame and blackened embedded pin/head stocks. Every packer
starts on the retained wall and terminates on the actual frame backing; the
approximately 5.5-mm gap is filled rather than hidden by a moved wall.
