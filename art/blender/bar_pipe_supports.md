# Fitted Harukiya ceiling-pipe supports

Evidence class: **INERT**

Classification: **ADAPTATION**. Four original **retail_bar_pipe** records in
**art/data/building_layout.json** retain their identity, endpoints, radius and
finish. This assembly adds supports against the actual imported ten-sided
pipe faces. Original pipe utility function and endpoint connections remain
under source ownership; no network or structural capacity is inferred.

**scripts/build_bar_pipe_supports.py** makes twenty-four split collars with
separate upper and lower bands, bored ears, washers, brass bolt shanks, hex
heads and nuts. The nominal nut bore matches the closed static shaft;
submillimetre helical thread relief is unresolved. Three-millimetre iron
bands follow the actual stored pipe profiles, with a one-millimetre joint
gap and twenty-four-millimetre axial width. Twelve-millimetre stems connect
to retained ceiling faces through ninety-millimetre bearing plates and
four fixing heads. Four drops cross the high brick-lined well; the remaining
twenty meet the low retained soffit.

The first support of **retail_bar_pipe0** shifts from source X -10.4 to -9.5
because a straight drop at the initial station crosses the original WC
lintel. The other stations stay in their planned grid. The full plate
footprints and spans clear the original fabric and fitted light mounts.
The four pipes themselves remain unchanged.

The saved native keeps 576 individually closed stocks with positive volume,
48 local material partitions and 65,472 triangles. Editable metre charts use
the existing **iron_blackened** and **brass_dull** catalogue maps. The export
retains normals, UVs and tangents without duplicating bitmap images. Local
MatLib clones preserve its shared projection policy and optical calibration.
Exact visible triangles provide collision; import compression and generated
LODs are disabled to preserve small physical contacts and chart derivatives.
The source plan, generator, retained import, original layout, maps and export
are hash-bound in the construction report and test fixture.

**scripts/inspect_bar_pipe_supports.py** reads the saved native, checks all
closed stocks, 600 retained ceiling samples, 720 pipe-face samples and 120
clear spans, and renders four close views using the actual catalogue maps.
Original bar geometry and existing light mounts are read-only context.
**OrisonV2BarPipeSupportsTest** checks imported contacts, all four immutable
pipe records, material bindings, metre UV derivatives and collision equality.
Four paired production views require a clear ordinary standing capsule and
retained floor support. Hiding the new supports for a visual comparison does
not claim a second physics or gameplay state.

passed without widening the thirty-micron contact bound. The first installed

east-facing view before candidate verification. Captures and **suite_run**
