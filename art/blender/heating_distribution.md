## Discovery and corrections

The isolated trial passes 3,567 checks and 10,818 native airway samples in
**tmp/heat-distribution/native-trial-inspection5.log.receipt.json**. Its one
oversized mesh, absent supports, uncut structural route and closed retained
receiver junction prevent installation acceptance. Four frames were directly
inspected; the ground-west underfloor route and light-slot bridge require
service protection and access.

Original masonry construction volumes exposed buried routes that a visible
triangle survey missed. Sixth-storey west branches now use the centre of the
authored light slot. H-D exits the chase through two orthogonal legs and clears
the south wall. Ports must cross complete original wall thickness. Adjacent
holes require common multi-bore plates; overlapping independent flanges do not
provide a valid seat.

Support discovery excludes historic millwork from structural bearing ownership.
The post-port trial has 491 proposed underside/wall seats, with ten adaptive
moves and no unseated station. These are planning observations, not verified
hardware, expansion, anchor or maximum-span acceptance.

The 84-partition export trial has 199,004 triangles, 11,043 clear native lumen
samples and zero sampled structural centreline hits. It fails five metric UV
checks on small partitions and is not accepted. The retained single-mesh trial
is distinct. **partition-inspection1.log.receipt.json** retains this failure.
The provisional 68 common liners have not yet been fabricated or seated.
