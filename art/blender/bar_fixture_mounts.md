# Fitted Harukiya light mounts and canopy stays

Evidence class: **INERT**

Classification: **ADAPTATION**. The eighteen original **F01_BAR_LT_** markers
in **art/data/building_layout.json** retain their positions, kinds, lighting
settings, hours, interactions and acoustic owners. The five globe sconces
face inward from their original east and west stations. Their original body
has a rear face 24.5 mm behind its anchor; the fitted brackets meet that
actual face. No lamp energy, range or global environment adjustment is made.

**scripts/build_bar_fixture_mounts.py** ray-fits new stocks against the
unchanged imported bar fabric. Sixteen fixture connections and four canopy
stays use 161 individually closed stocks, exported as 46 local material
partitions with 13,900 triangles. Two table suspension rods already reach
the retained soffit and remain under their original owner. Iron bearing
plates are 90 by 90 by 4 mm, with worked edges and four brass fixing heads.
Rigid stems are 16 mm in diameter; flexible drops are 12 mm in diameter.
The canopy retains its original body and four stays connect its top to the
actual ceiling above it.

The well lamp meets the existing flex with a small inline connector across
its 60-mm gap. It has no ceiling-size plate on that cord. The two west sconces
retain their stations beside the original gallery and scoreboard; free
vertical offset brackets rise above those decorations and bear on the clear
wall below the ceiling. Neither pictures nor a scoreboard serve as bearings.
The overlapping original gallery pictures remain a separate layout defect.

The saved native preserves each closed construction stock and editable
metre charts. Its actual catalogue **iron_blackened**, **brass_dull** and
**rubber_aged** maps are used for native inspection. The shipping export
retains normals, metre UVs, tangents and material partitions without embedding
duplicate image copies; the existing MatLib binds all three shipping maps.
Local material clones retain the catalogue optical settings and leave its
shared projection policy intact. Every physical mesh uses its exact visible
triangles. The mount import disables lossy compression and generated LODs.

The retained bar import uses the same precision policy: its former compressed
vertices shifted an east bearing plane enough to fail the original 30-micron
contact bound. The source glTF/bin bytes, original import UID and all source
records remain unchanged. Precision restores the authored planes; the contact
tolerance is not widened. The pool fitter continues to remove only its seven
original box boundaries, retaining all other current imported attributes.

**scripts/inspect_bar_fixture_mounts.py** inspects the saved native, positive
closed volumes, 486 actual retained bearing samples (including two original
rods), and 110 clear span samples. It renders four close views against the
read-only original bar. Native views omit dynamic fixture actors; production
inspection separately includes those original bodies and their lighting.
**OrisonV2BarFixtureMountsTest** checks all eighteen actors, five inward
orientations and back-plate junctions, 484 bearing samples, 110 clear physical
spans, exact collision, material bindings and UV derivatives. Seven paired
production views require a clear ordinary standing capsule and retained
floor support. The stage view uses the clear lane west of its chairs; the
stair view uses the clear landing west of the original crates. The original
visual comparison temporarily hides mounts and restores former sconce yaw;
it does not claim a second physics or gameplay state.

Scoped inspection lives in **tmp/bar-fixture-supports**. Captures and
**suite_run** receipts do not promote the runtime ledger. Independent supply
capacity, electrical intake, ceiling-pipe supports/endpoints, overlapping
gallery layout, inherited broad surface response, roof falls/outlets and
whole-game geometry/material acceptance remain open. These fitted contacts
make no structural-capacity claim.
