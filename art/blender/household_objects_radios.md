Evidence class: **INERT**

retain 21 V1 source records and one existing completion copy. Seventeen passive
actors use sixteen variants; five radios share one variant. Exact source
surfaces, bounds and rear-wall fit are compared before variants are shared.

The passive family contains five pinboards, two toolboards, two crates, a reel
deck, a six-leaf rubber plant, six stacked books, a camera tripod, two softboxes
and two continuous open cable leads. Cards keep their original counts and
positions, with fitted pins and overlapping sheet depths. Tools hang on real
pegs and cradles. The basement toolboard gets 130mm wall standoffs; apartment

terminal bud, six source-sized curved blades at the source node heights,

gaps. Crate contents retain their passive role. No new inventory, light emitter
or operation is inferred. Local catalogue finishes replace coarse galvanized
and pebbled maps with existing nickel and moulded bakelite; no new bitmap or
catalogue key is introduced.

and moving native partitions after the original ready method, rebasing the
