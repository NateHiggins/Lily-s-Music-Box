# Pawnbroker cases and passive window stock

Evidence class: **INERT**

Eight original source boxes become two glazed wall cases and one supported
window stand. The native source has 161 closed, connected stocks, three joined
assemblies, sixteen material partitions and 28,296 triangles. Runtime retires
exactly 96 original triangles; original layout records and shipping geometry
remain immutable.

The cases have floor-seated corner posts, inset walnut panels, rails, a lined
display floor, four 4mm side panes and a 5mm top pane within a folded brass
rim. They remain deliberately empty. The original 1.45m upper datum remains.
The east case and its top shorten 0.15m at the rear end to clear the retained
safe by 40mm. A 3mm inset separates the lower joinery from the retained
wainscot; these are declared adaptations. Four feet per case reach the
original 0.01m oak floor.

Six feet, lower rails and upper framing carry the window stand and its
retained plywood backboard. The deck rises from 0.44m to 0.64m so the stock
appears above the existing 0.55m sill. The source contains no itemized window
inventory. A collapsed telescope, pocket watch with a laid chain, and paired
field glasses are therefore explicitly inferred passive display stock. Their
cradles and cushion seat on the deck. None owns purchasing, clock-time,
optical input, case facts or signal behavior; none carries lettering.

Existing catalogue walnut, aged brass, dark bakelite, upholstery, porcelain
and iron provide local finishes. Walnut grain follows the length of each
post, panel and rail. Original plywood/glass maps remain underlying materials.
The two cases, field-glass lenses and remaining original fixed shop glazing
receive a local instance of the existing architectural clear dielectric
shader at its existing 0.06 roughness. This removes the diffuse lamp glare
observed through the original storefront. The source materials, fixed glazing
mesh, pose and collision remain; shared glass and lighting settings do not
change. Native inspection uses the original mapped glass; production captures
verify the declared runtime optical override.

passage context including the previously fitted clocks. Nine native renders
