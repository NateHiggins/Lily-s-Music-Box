Evidence class: **INERT**

visibility owner, collision hull, lamp, sign and interaction. The local finish

rolled rims and paper bands, folded packets, slatted produce crates, cabbage

icebox, a wheeled handcart, practical shades and flush floor seals.

Stock bases fit the actual shelf, sill or case shelf. Counter goods sit behind
the original glass. The static icebox's glow backing sits inside its cavity.
Shade shells leave bulbs visible below the retained ceiling plates. These
adaptations preserve source-node transforms rather than moving authored anchors.

screwed conduit drops. The actual imported contact test keeps its original
one-millimetre tolerance. Existing counter words fit its lower display fascia

inherited middle-dot encoding.

58 installed assemblies. Every constituent has one
**Metres** chart, source tint, unit normals and a tangent guide. Export projects

explicit source-tint guide preserves the saved colours in the exported channel.
Godot imports without compression or generated LODs. The saved native retains
relative paths to the existing catalogue maps; the export carries named
material bindings and no embedded copies of those maps.

**build_bodega_fittings.py** installs the native, GLB, construction inventory,

**inspect_bodega_fittings.py** reopens the saved native independently, checks
closed volume and physical chart distances, then renders six ordinary-height

without saving temporary room context into the native.

The runtime validates the complete source/native binding before substituting
meshes. All 18 original aggregate stock nodes remain the owners of their new
surfaces. Depletion uses the existing registry and presentation callback. Fixed
collision hulls, keys, schedules, source light positions and energies, shop
service and save ownership remain with their existing systems. The passive
scale and icebox fittings add visual detail, not new gameplay appliances.
