# DREAM-SURFACE-S2F biological topology references

This sheet is deliberately about form and optical behavior, not palette.

| Proposed visual element | Direct reference feature | Geometry / lighting consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Plasmodial body | *Physarum* forms one multinucleate body whose thick tubes continuously merge into thin exploratory regions. | One remeshed volume; broad low fans and raised ridges share the same surface. No overlaid veins. |
| Feeding network | Flow channels develop inside a spreading droplet, interconnect, and vary continuously in diameter. | Branches split and rejoin; radius changes along every route; junctions are smooth saddles. |
| Advancing fronts | Migrating amoeboid fragments polarize into a broad anterior and contracted rear while intracellular flow feeds the front. | Several unequal, thin scalloped fans point in one dominant direction rather than radiating evenly. |
| Embedded flow | Shuttle streaming moves cytoplasm and dense vesicles through the vein network. | Shallow subsurface grooves/ridges follow the grown network; later material motion must remain inside those routes. |
| Transport complex | Membrane proteins have extracellular domains, transmembrane regions and intracellular openings; transport crosses a bilayer rather than occurring above it. | One continuous hourglass-like complex spans a thick membrane, with a narrow internal lumen and organic sealing collars. |
| Capture and release | Endocytosis/exocytosis deform membranes around cargo and separate extracellular capture from intracellular release. | Four irregular vesicular cargo forms occupy different depths; the membrane cutaway supplies direction through occlusion, not arrows. |
| Nucleus | Nuclei have an envelope and non-uniform interior density; in *Physarum*, nuclei can be advected or held in the cortical network. | One dominant irregular double-shell body is suspended centrally and intersects no floor. |
| Vacuoles and vesicles | Amoeboid cytoplasm contains differently sized deformable vacuoles and dense vesicles carried by streaming. | Soft unequal cavities attach to or nest beside transport branches; small vesicles sit on routes rather than loose in space. |
| Contractile cortex | Actomyosin cortex and fibrils drive periodic contraction and shuttle flow. | Tensioned curved fibers connect distant membrane anchor regions and pass around, not through, the nucleus. |
| Phase contrast / DIC | Unstained cells show restrained boundary halos, optical-path gradients and depth-separated edge response. | Neutral oblique key plus soft backlight; broad gradients and thin edge highlights, without emissive outlines. |
| Fluorescence | Fluorescence isolates selected structures against weaker context rather than making every surface glow. | Deferred until clay approval; later use selectively for moving material only. |
