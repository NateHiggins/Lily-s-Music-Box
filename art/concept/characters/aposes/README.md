# Character A-Pose Modeling Sources

This directory contains the 19-character, front-facing A-pose source set intended for image-to-3D reconstruction, retopology, rigging, and placeholder animation work. The earlier `tposes` directory is preserved as an identity/reference archive.

## Production contract

- Relaxed, symmetrical A-pose with arms approximately 35 degrees below horizontal.
- Upright front view, feet hip-width and parallel, full silhouette visible.
- Natural neutral hands: straight wrists, gently curved separated fingers, visible thumbs.
- Pure white background and neutral, anatomy-readable studio lighting.
- Fitted, modular clothing with visible shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, and ankles.
- No robes, coats, skirts, scarves, wide legs, hanging straps, loose hems, or tented fabric requiring cloth simulation.
- Wardrobes reflect what each resident might plausibly wear when answering an after-hours maintenance call in their apartment.

## Wardrobe direction

The cast shares a grounded nighttime palette but remains identifiable through personal color, construction, and small rigid story details: Evelyn's repaired academic knitwear, Teresa's personalized scrubs, Mina's precise technical layers, Lena's sewing-informed paneling, Juno's waveform seams, Malcolm's botanical quilting, Omar's workwear, Rhea's audio detailing, Peter's careful old-fashioned grid knit, Cam's courier geometry, Noel's hand motif, Nadia's signal accents, Cal's frequency panels, Iris's painterly color blocking, Sacha's card-case tailoring, Jonah's archival restraint, Mae's catalog-label construction, and the transient guests' travel-coded garments.

These images are modeling references, not final texture sheets. Treat garment seams and panels as design guidance, then consolidate them into low-cost geometry and baked normal/albedo detail appropriate to the game's rendering budget.
