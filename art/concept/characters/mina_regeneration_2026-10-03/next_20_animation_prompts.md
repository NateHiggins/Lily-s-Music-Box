# Mina Vale: animation prompts 21–40

Evidence class: **INERT**

Each description contains exactly **400 characters**, including spaces and punctuation. Durations and playback are separate metadata. These are commissioning prompts; they do not add runtime animation clips.

21. **Begin walking — 3 seconds (once)**

   Mina begins standing with her feet beneath her hips and both arms at rest. She looks toward her destination, shifts her weight onto the right foot and eases her torso forward. Lift the left heel, take one measured step and roll through the sole as the right leg follows. Introduce an opposing arm swing without raising the shoulders. End midway into her normal walking cycle, ready to blend smoothly.

22. **Stop walking — 3 seconds (once)**

   Mina begins midway through her ordinary walk. She spots her stopping point, shortens the next stride and places her leading heel beneath her body. Bend knees slightly to absorb the remaining momentum, then bring the trailing foot alongside without scraping the floor. Her arms complete their swing and settle beside her skirt. Raise the torso gently and finish balanced, looking ahead in a calm idle.

23. **Close a door — 5 seconds (once)**

   Mina stands beside an open door and reaches her right hand to its handle. She takes a step away from the hinge, draws the leaf toward the frame and lets her elbow bend as it approaches. Slow the final few inches so the door closes quietly. Keep her palm on the handle for a brief latch check, then release it. Lower her arm, return the stepping foot and finish facing the closed door with soft knees.

24. **Lock a door — 6 seconds (once)**

   Facing a closed door, Mina takes a key from her jacket pocket with her right hand. She leans slightly toward the lock, inserts the key and turns her wrist until the bolt seats. Pause under gentle pressure, then reverse enough to withdraw the key. Her left hand tests the handle once without opening the door. Return the key to its pocket, straighten and lower both hands into a settled standing pose.

25. **Knock and wait — 4 seconds (once)**

   Mina stops within easy reach of a closed door, with her feet stable and shoulders level. Raise her right hand to chest height, curl the fingers loosely and knock twice with the middle knuckles. Each tap comes from an elbow movement; avoid striking with the whole arm. Hold the hand near the surface for a moment, then lower it. Tilt her head slightly toward the door and finish listening for a reply.

26. **Hold a door for someone — 6 seconds (loop)**

   Mina stands outside the swing of an open door, her hand steady on its handle and her elbow softly bent. Keep the leaf fixed while she glances toward the person approaching. Shift a little weight onto the rear foot, leaving a clear path beside her, then return without moving the hand contact. Her left arm hangs loosely by her skirt. Bring her gaze back to its starting direction for a seamless loop.

27. **Wait for the lift — 6 seconds (loop)**

   Mina waits one step from the lift doors, feet close together and hands resting at her sides. She lifts her eyes toward the indicator, raises her chin slightly and pauses to check it. Let one heel relax without leaving the floor as she transfers her weight, then bring her gaze back to the doors. Add one quiet breath and a blink. Restore the opening head angle and weight balance for a seamless loop.

28. **Enter the lift — 6 seconds (once)**

   Mina begins facing open lift doors. She glances down at the sill and takes three measured steps into the cabin, lifting each shoe cleanly over the threshold. Once both feet are inside, turn clockwise through 180 degrees with separate steps, letting the eyes lead the shoulders. Her arms remain close to her sides, clear of the door frame. Finish upright facing the entrance, feet planted and relaxed.

29. **Exit the lift — 5 seconds (once)**

   Mina starts inside the lift, facing its open doors. Her eyes check the landing before she transfers weight forward and takes three measured steps out. Lift the front shoe over the sill, place it firmly on the landing and bring the trailing foot clear. Shorten the final stride to stop without swaying. She turns her head slightly toward her next destination, then settles with hands beside her skirt.

30. **Tired standing idle — 8 seconds (loop)**

   Mina stands with neatly placed feet and hands loose beside her skirt. As she exhales, her shoulders drop slightly and her head dips a fraction. Shift her weight slowly onto the left leg, soften the right knee and hold for two seconds. She blinks, draws a quiet breath and lifts her head, recovering her posture without snapping upright. Return the weight evenly over both feet to close a gentle loop.

31. **Adjust her spectacles — 3 seconds (once)**

   Mina begins looking straight ahead, feet still and chin level. Raise her hand toward the spectacles with the index finger extended and thumb relaxed. Touch the bridge lightly, nudge the frame upward and make a tiny sideways correction until both lenses sit level. Her eyes close briefly during contact, then refocus on the person ahead. Withdraw the hand along the same path and lower it to her side.

32. **Check the pencil behind her ear — 3 seconds (once)**

   Mina pauses with her eyes fixed on a thought ahead of her. Her right hand rises beside her cheek; the index and middle fingers touch the pencil tucked behind her ear. Feel along its exposed end, press it into place and stop before dislodging it. Her head tilts a few degrees away to make room for the hand. Release the pencil, lower the arm and return her head to level with a small, relieved breath.

33. **Straighten a repaired cuff — 4 seconds (once)**

   Mina brings her left wrist in front of her waist and looks down at the cuff. With her right thumb and forefinger, pinch its edge and give one delicate tug. Smooth the repaired seam with two short passes of her fingertips, keeping the fabric against the wrist. Her shoulders remain low and her feet stay still. Release the cuff, lower both hands and return her gaze ahead, her posture neatly restored.

34. **Correct the record — 5 seconds (once)**

   Mina faces a listener and takes a breath before interrupting. Raise her right hand to chest height with one index finger extended; keep the elbow close to her ribs. Make one short downward gesture to mark the exact detail, then hold the finger still while her mouth forms a brief correction. Her eyebrows lift without anger. Fold the finger, lower the hand and finish with steady, direct eye contact.

35. **Disagree carefully — 4 seconds (once)**

   Mina listens with her chin level and hands open at her sides. Her eyes narrow slightly as she considers the idea. Turn her head a few degrees left, then right, giving a slow, contained shake without moving the shoulders. Pause facing the listener; her lips press together before relaxing. Let one hand open a little at waist level as if making space for a correction, then lower it and settle evenly.

36. **Proofread a page — 6 seconds (loop)**

   Mina sits upright at her desk with feet flat and a page beneath her hands. Her left hand steadies the paper while her right index finger tracks one short line from left to right. Follow the fingertip with small eye movements and a slight forward lean. Pause at the end as if checking a word, then lift the finger and return it above the line's beginning. Restore her posture for a quiet reading loop.

37. **Turn a page — 4 seconds (once)**

   Seated at her desk, Mina steadies a paper stack with her left hand. Her right thumb and forefinger slide to the top page's far corner, pinch it and lift it into a shallow curve. Guide the sheet across to the left, letting her elbow travel clear of the desk edge. Lower the page carefully, release the corner and smooth it with a flat palm. Bring both hands back to rest beside the newly exposed page.

38. **Read a personal letter — 8 seconds (once)**

   Mina stands holding a folded letter at waist height with both hands. Raise it toward her chest, open the fold carefully and spread the sheet without creasing it again. Her eyes travel across lines while her chin dips slightly. Pause on one passage; the fingers tighten briefly, then soften. Lower the letter a few inches and look into the middle distance, finishing still with the page held securely.

39. **Carry a laundry basket — 4 seconds (loop)**

   Mina walks with both hands gripping opposite sides of a laundry basket held just below her waist. Take four measured steps, landing heel first and rolling through each sole. Keep the elbows bent and the basket close to her body, allowing only a natural rise as her weight changes legs. Her shoulders stay level and her eyes look ahead. Use an in-place cycle, closing on the same foot and basket pose.

40. **Fold a garment — 8 seconds (once)**

   Mina stands at a waist-high table with a shirt flat before her. Smooth the fabric outward using her palms, fold one sleeve inward and repeat with the other. Bring the side edges toward the center with careful, matched hand movements. Lift the lower hem, fold it up and press the resulting rectangle gently. Keep her feet planted and bend from the hips. Finish with both hands resting beside the fold.
