# Mina Vale: 20 critical animation prompts

Evidence class: **INERT**

Each movement description is exactly **400 characters**, including spaces and punctuation. Name, duration and playback are separate metadata. All durations are 2–10 seconds. These are production prompts for the new Mina model, not exported motion files.

Use Mina's own proportions and rest pose. Keep movements precise and human. The requested turn has a rightward direction; produce a mirrored left version later. Door actions use her right hand, with contact targets adapted to the actual leaf and lock. Facial actions require an eye/mouth/brow rig. Speech body gestures can loop, while mouth timing follows the eventual voice track.

## 1. Calm idle — 6 seconds

Clip: **mina_idle_calm**. Playback: **seamless loop**.

Mina stands upright with feet hip-width apart, knees soft and hands relaxed beside her skirt. Over six seconds, a quiet breath lifts her ribs; her weight shifts a little toward one foot, then returns evenly. She blinks once and checks the room with a tiny eye movement, keeping her head nearly still. Finish in the exact opening pose for a seamless loop. Keep her manner precise, tired and contained.

## 2. Ordinary walk — 4 seconds

Clip: **mina_walk**. Playback: **seamless loop**.

Mina walks ahead in a measured four-step cycle, chin level and shoulders held neatly. Each foot lands heel first, rolls through the sole and leaves from the toe; knees bend softly without bouncing. Her arms swing close to her sides, with opposing shoulder turns. Keep the skirt clear of her knees and the shoes level with the floor. Make an in-place loop with consistent stride and soft hip movement.

## 3. Hurried walk — 3 seconds

Clip: **mina_walk_hurried**. Playback: **seamless loop**.

Mina takes six quick, short walking steps while trying to stay composed. Her torso leans only slightly forward; shoulders stay controlled and elbows bend a little more than in her ordinary walk. Feet lift cleanly, roll heel to toe and never break into a run. Her eyes flick ahead as if checking the next doorway. End on the same foot phase as the opening. Use an in-place loop with no sideways drift.

## 4. Turn in place — 3 seconds

Clip: **mina_turn_around**. Playback: **one shot**.

From a neutral standing pose, Mina looks over her right shoulder, transfers weight to her left foot and turns clockwise through 180 degrees using three steps. Her hips follow the gaze, while the shoulders lag, then align. Keep both shoes on the floor except during each step; never twist a planted knee. Her arms remain low and clear of the skirt. Settle facing the opposite direction in a calm idle.

## 5. Climb stairs — 4 seconds

Clip: **mina_stairs_up**. Playback: **seamless loop**.

Mina climbs four ordinary stair treads with deliberate steps. Lift each knee just enough for the shoe to clear the next riser, place the whole sole on the higher tread and transfer weight before raising the trailing foot. Lean the torso slightly uphill without hunching. Arms make balancing swings; her gaze stays a few steps ahead. Keep pelvis height smooth and finish at a repeatable walking phase.

## 6. Descend stairs — 4 seconds

Clip: **mina_stairs_down**. Playback: **seamless loop**.

Mina descends four stair treads at a slow pace. Her eyes look down, then return ahead. Reach her leading foot to the lower tread, touch the forefoot first and lower weight through a bent supporting knee before bringing the trailing foot down. Keep the torso upright, shoulders quiet and arms slightly away from the body for balance. Avoid jumping or rigid legs. Finish at a repeatable stepping phase.

## 7. Unlock a door — 5 seconds

Clip: **mina_door_unlock**. Playback: **one shot**.

Standing beside a closed door, Mina takes a key from her jacket pocket with her right hand. She looks at the lock, guides the key into it, turns her wrist through a firm arc and pauses to feel the mechanism release. Her left hand rests by the handle without gripping it. She withdraws the key, returns it to the pocket and lowers both hands. Keep her feet planted and finish in a quiet standing pose.

## 8. Push a door open — 5 seconds

Clip: **mina_door_push_open**. Playback: **one shot**.

Mina stands arm's length from a closed door and reaches her right hand to its handle. She turns the handle, shifts weight onto the forward foot and pushes the leaf away with a controlled extension of the elbow. Take two steps through the opening while the hand follows the handle. Release it only after her body clears the threshold. Lower her arm and settle upright, without colliding with the leaf.

## 9. Pull a door open — 6 seconds

Clip: **mina_door_pull_open**. Playback: **one shot**.

Mina reaches for a closed door handle with her right hand, turns it and pulls the leaf toward herself. She takes one backward step, then a sideways step to clear its swing. Her elbow bends easily as the handle approaches; the torso turns gently without losing balance. Once the opening is clear, she releases the handle and steps past. Return her arms to rest and finish upright beyond the threshold.

## 10. Sit at her desk — 6 seconds

Clip: **mina_sit_down**. Playback: **one shot**.

Mina approaches a chair, turns until its seat is behind her and checks its position with a downward glance. She places one hand near the seat edge, uses the other to smooth her skirt and bends both knees to lower herself down. Keep the back long, feet flat and weight centered over the chair. Once seated, bring her hands onto the desk and make a posture adjustment. Finish upright and ready to work.

## 11. Rise from her desk — 5 seconds

Clip: **mina_stand_up**. Playback: **one shot**.

Mina begins seated upright at a desk, feet flat beneath her knees and hands resting near her papers. She draws one foot back, leans forward from the hips and presses down on the chair edge to stand. Extend the knees smoothly, letting the head and shoulders rise together without jerking. Once upright, smooth the front of her jacket with one hand, lower both arms and settle into a neutral idle pose.

## 12. Write a caption — 6 seconds

Clip: **mina_write_caption**. Playback: **seamless loop**.

Seated at her desk, Mina holds a pencil in her right hand while the left steadies a paper sheet. She leans forward and writes a short line with precise wrist and finger movements, her eyes tracking the pencil tip. Pause, lift the pencil just above the page, reread the line and add one mark. Keep the shoulders low and feet planted. Return to the starting writing position for a restrained work loop.

## 13. Listen attentively — 6 seconds

Clip: **mina_listen_attentive**. Playback: **seamless loop**.

Mina faces a speaker with her hands low and open, shoulders square but not rigid. She holds eye contact, tilts her head a few degrees and makes one slow, thoughtful nod. Her brows lift as she considers the words; a breath moves her upper chest. Avoid broad gestures or repeated nodding. Let the head return to level and the hands remain quiet, ending in the opening pose for a natural listening loop.

## 14. Speak with precision — 6 seconds

Clip: **mina_talk_measured**. Playback: **seamless loop**.

Mina speaks to a person facing her with a level head and steady eye contact. Her right hand rises to waist height, makes two open-palm gestures and lowers again; the left hand stays close to her body. Add subtle brow and mouth movement, with a pause between thoughts. Keep shoulders contained and feet planted. Finish with both hands at rest so the gesture can loop beneath separately timed dialogue.

## 15. Strained explanation — 7 seconds

Clip: **mina_strained**. Playback: **one shot**.

Mina starts to explain something, raising one hand as if choosing a precise word. Her fingers tighten, her brow draws inward and her mouth closes before the sentence can finish. She looks down for a moment, takes a breath and gently presses the thumb into the opposite palm. Her shoulders rise only a little. Let the hand drift low and end with her facing the listener, still trying to stay composed.

## 16. Recognition — 6 seconds

Clip: **mina_recognition**. Playback: **one shot**.

Mina listens with a guarded expression, then becomes still as a new understanding reaches her. Her eyes focus directly, the brow relaxes and her lips part slightly without speaking. A breath releases; the shoulders soften and her hands uncurl at her sides. Lift the chin a fraction, as if seeing the other person clearly for the first time. Finish in a quiet, open stance without a wide smile or nod.

## 17. Annotate the air — 5 seconds

Clip: **mina_annotate_air**. Playback: **one shot**.

Mina notices something beside the listener and turns her eyes toward it before moving her head. Her right hand rises to chest height, with index and middle fingers close together. Trace two neat strokes in the air as if setting a caption in place, then make one correcting stroke. She studies the invisible result, presses her lips together and lowers her hand. Keep the torso still and feet planted.

## 18. Anxious pacing — 10 seconds

Clip: **mina_pace_anxious**. Playback: **seamless loop**.

Mina takes three quick steps across a small clear space, shoulders tight and eyes lowered. She stops abruptly but quietly, touches one cuff as if correcting herself and turns through 180 degrees in small steps. Walk back to the starting position and repeat the turn. Keep the path narrow, arms close to the body and movement restless rather than frantic. End in the same pose and location for a loop.

## 19. Allow silence — 8 seconds

Clip: **mina_accept_silence**. Playback: **one shot**.

Mina faces the listener and starts to raise a hand to fill the pause. She catches the impulse, opens her fingers and lowers the hand slowly. Hold four seconds of near-stillness with gentle eye contact and only natural breathing. Her mouth remains closed; the shoulders loosen. After the pause, let a faint sense of relief reach her eyes. Finish quietly, without nodding, speaking or making a caption.

## 20. Quiet thanks — 6 seconds

Clip: **mina_quiet_thanks**. Playback: **one shot**.

Mina looks directly at the person before her and takes one steady breath. Her guarded posture loosens; one hand opens at waist height, palm angled gently upward. She gives one slow nod, lets a slight smile reach her eyes and lowers the hand again. Keep the expression brief and sincere, with no flourish, bow or exaggerated happiness. Finish standing comfortably, holding the connection for a moment.
