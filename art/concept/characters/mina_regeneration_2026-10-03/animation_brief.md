# Mina Vale animation commissioning list

Evidence class: **INERT**

72 proposed clips for the regenerated Mina. These are action descriptions for animation production; they are not newly exported motion files. Each clip should keep her precise, contained manner, with warmer motion after she learns to leave silence unannotated.

## Performance and delivery

Use the new model's own rest pose and bone axes. Deliver the character mesh without embedded motion and a separate personal motion library. Preserve exported proportions; do not transfer another model's joint spacing or scale tracks. Body movement stays restrained, with no generic dance, combat or cartoon exaggeration.

Loops should join smoothly; one-shots should settle into an appropriate idle and must retain one-shot playback. Locomotion must match navigation speed and floor contact. Provide left/right door hand variants and chair, desk, bed and lift contact markers during production. Hat, pencil, key, paper and laundry actions need attachment and contact events rather than permanently held props.

Eyes, blinks, brows and subtle mouth movements need a face rig. Keep speech mouth motion separate from looping body gestures and drive it from the eventual voice performance. Emotion should register at conversation distance. Preserve the existing dialogue role fragments **strained** and **recognition**.

## Commissioning order

First produce calm/listening idles, ordinary walk and turns, stairs, doors, sit/stand, writing, the two existing case roles and accepting silence. Next complete daily chores and conversation variants. Finish the intimate emotional transitions after the face rig is working, so vulnerability reads through eyes, breath and hands rather than large gestures.
