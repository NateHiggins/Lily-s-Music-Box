the movement inspiration: the fine distal club makes a local protrusion toward
a candidate cleft, dwells and samples, retracts to reconsider, then tries two
more sites before committing. The massive root follows late. At contact the
existing vascular bolus arrives at the sensory club as a brief
electrochemical flare; cilia and suckers stabilize sequentially, then the
existing surface transformer transfers secretion into the shared LivingField.
The resulting conversion is ordinary matter's biologically and dimensionally
wrong response to the creature's sincere communication attempt.
