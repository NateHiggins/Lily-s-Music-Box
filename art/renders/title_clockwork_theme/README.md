# Clockwork Waltz title screen

Production 1280 × 720 captures, 2026-08-15.

- `00_before.png` is commit `07607f4`: the placeholder `REALTY MAINTENANCE`
  menu over the sales plate, with `three_am_radiator.ogg` as its looping theme.
- `01_escapement_failure.png` and `02_original_session.png` record the rejected
  first redesign from commit `2638389`: two record-dependent faces and a quiet
  clockwork overlay. They are retained as decision history, not current art.
- `03_grand_mundane_original.png` is the final opening frame: the untouched
  Clockwork Waltz over one rain-soaked waking-world hero joining ORISON, STREET
  and the Vantry Arcade through a maintenance worker and ordinary infrastructure.
- `04_grand_mundane_return.png` proves the optional returned record changes only
  the record copy. The waking-world hero remains identical.

The images are deterministic silent renders. The dream world is deliberately
absent because it is the game's reveal. `TitleScreenAudioTest.tscn` separately
proves real playback and the finished-stream handoff.
