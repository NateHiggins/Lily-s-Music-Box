# Original Diner rear bench and shelves

Evidence class: **INERT**

The original **storm_shop_luncheonette_backbar**, its top and three
**storm_shop_luncheonette_bbshelf0/1/2** records become two passive
assemblies, **26** closed positive connected stocks, three mapped
partitions and **4,888** visible/physical triangles. Original plan
envelopes and upper datums remain: the serving sheet is **1.02m**, the
three shelf runs end at **1.36m / 1.76m / 2.16m** and their depth stays
the original **0.05m**. Hidden original reference poses remain.

Six chrome posts fill the original **0.04m** floor gap. Thin joined side
and end panels, upper/lower rails and a supported countertop replace the
solid body. Three narrow floor-bearing timber uprights join the original
shelf runs. Rails sit a millimetre inside the post face to remove visible
coplanar black patches. Each stock is closed and each assembly connected.

The editable source and export keep existing shipping chrome, countertop
and timber maps. Native metre charts and tangent derivatives reach the
composed partitions. A cell-owned, one-time room reflection gives the
fully metallic chrome the lit Diner to reflect. Its plan follows the
original floor; its height is three metres and capture distance eight
metres. The existing project reflection atlas supplies its resolution.
No shared material, lamp, new finish key or invented shelf load is added.
The supported API is described in the [Godot ReflectionProbe reference](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_reflectionprobe.html).

Native inspection proves nine actual floor contacts and both sides of
the three unchanged urn/pie-case seats at **1.02m**, with no unintended
overlap against **50** accepted native partitions and remaining original
context. Temporary retirement covers **27** exact original boundaries
(**324** triangles) plus nine exact old receiver owners (**836** triangles).
Only the five selected original records (**60** triangles) retire in game.
All other source arrays, material slots and physical/visible ownership
remain. Startup and reconstruction mount the same fitting.

All eight final native and six focused game frames were directly reviewed.
**OrisonV2DinerBackbarTest** passes **106** checks for exact source
retention, imported charts, physical faces, hollow inner panels, source
shelf dimensions, floor/display seats and the bounded reflection owner.
All six requested standing stations are clear. Initial occluded views,
black-chrome comparisons, coplanar-joint images and the unsupported
per-probe-resolution diagnostic remain separate evidence. Wider committed
verification remains required before production acceptance.

Godot normalized the earlier Till import identifier after its initial
native inspection. The Till builder now has a bounded
**--refresh-import-binding** mode: it refreshes only that import record
and its own source hash, refusing every other source drift. Both paired
construction fixtures are regenerated; the Till blend, mesh and runtime
data remain byte-identical. All nine rebound Till native frames were
reviewed and all eight supports and 43-context-partition checks pass.
The rear bench builder has the same bounded normalization mode.

Food/drink service, urns, griddle, fountain/pumps, mixer, pie-case glazing,
menu, fan, changing reflection content over time, continuous routes,
utilities/capacity, wider optical glare and human acceptance remain open.
V2 remains incomplete.
