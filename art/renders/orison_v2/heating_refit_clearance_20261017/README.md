# Restricted-cell heating refit review

Evidence class: **INERT**. Scoped technical evidence archive, not whole-V2 or
human acceptance. The four sealed apartment thresholds remain sealed.

**qualify_current.py** checks the original 108-observation control/save contract,
complete executed helper bindings, current native construction/export lineage,
the fresh V7 acquired geometry, full main-height coverage, sixteen inspected
captures and bounded native/contact diagnostics. The runtime-only control test
preceded the final native collar adjustment; its receipt and eight original
images remain unchanged. Fresh geometry captures are all under **context/**.

**qualify_broad_current.py** independently checks eight separate scopes. Native
changes require fresh surface and apartment-access contracts; the five unchanged
runtime-only contracts retain their original executed receipts and source hashes.

**context/context.glb.gz** preserves the exact acquired mesh bytes, compressed
without editing glTF. Decompress to a temporary directory beside a copy of
**context/context.json** to inspect or reproduce the world-contact diagnostic.
The scoped qualifier streams the decompressed bytes through SHA-256 and checks
the actual acquisition digest. The raw GLB is intentionally not duplicated here.
**context_coverage.json** contains four exact installed routes plus one explicitly
diagnostic acquisition route spanning the full changed main. It creates no actor.

**context_contacts.json** and **context_review.json** contain actual triangle
findings and bounded dispositions against the acquired corridor. The world
diagnostic excludes duplicate distribution meshes; **assembly_contacts.json**
separately checks new versus retained native heating. Sampled containment remains
incomplete containment evidence. **joint_review.json** checks the fitted repair
collar against the acquired floor datum and the main's open bore. The earlier
rejected face plates remain in **rejected_plates_review.json** as a counterexample.
**rejected_main_collar_review.json** preserves the later full-height finding:
the generic sleeve entered F04's floor by 15 mm. It is a rejected historical
diagnostic, not current acceptance evidence.

The captured radiator bodies still have flat cast profiles, blocky fittings and
rectangular rust pads. New three-column castings and service parts are being
prototyped separately and are not installed by this checkpoint. Technical
UV/PBR/mipmap compliance grants no photorealism, lighting, walking access through
sealed doors, acoustic, frame-time or whole-game acceptance.
