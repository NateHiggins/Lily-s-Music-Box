# DREAM-COLOR-C1 phenotype/material bible

## Governing rule

Color communicates material and process, never faction or creature type. The default ecology is unstained and derives its appearance from optical density, thickness, refractive boundaries, suspended matter and incident light.

## Process mapping

The C1 presenter consumes existing cellular state vectors and exposes bounded instance parameters only. It owns no decisions, persistence, targeting or simulation.

- High energy/oxygenation analogue: amber-vermilion density moving along existing routes.
- Information storage/flow: iron/Prussian density localized to information anatomy.
- Electrical signaling: short cold-white polarization passage.
- Growth/protein synthesis: muted moss/bottle-glass shift at advancing regions.
- Digestion: carmine/rust/dark amber localized to cargo and vacuoles.
- Mechanical strain: increased orientation-dependent interference, not a red flash.
- Senescence: route disorder → lost structural coherence → milky opacity → nicotine/oxidized residue.

Process color is local, animated through object-space routes, mixed where masks overlap, and decays. No whole-organism instantaneous recolor is permitted.

## Metameric bands

- Filament: warm incident lamp reveals amber cytoplasm, dense tissue and true mineral conduction.
- Daylight: neutral/cool incident lamp separates pearl membrane, pale olive suspension and iron-dense interiors.
- Electrical/dream: unstable incident light reveals only selective cold polarization/fluorescence masks.

No camera-wide grade, bloom dependency, screen-space chromatic aberration, unique per-organism material, per-frame texture or GPU readback is authorized.
