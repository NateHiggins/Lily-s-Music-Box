# K0-RADIO — eighteen homes, not one receiver copied eighteen times

This sheet is the bounded mechanism inspection proof. Production-live testing
proves placement in the real apartments; the small stage keeps family
silhouettes readable without the building's furniture and lighting hiding
centimetre-scale controls.

## Sources and inference

Documented families come from the Smithsonian Crosley Pup and Atwater Kent 44,
Science Museum Group Marconiphone V2 and crystal receiver, a November 1928
trade advertisement for a Radiola 18 table, and a 1928 Mullard cone speaker.
URLs and the exact documented facts live in `game/data/domestic_radios.json`.

The allocation to residents, condition, mixed speakers, home-built chassis,
collector arrangement and every placement are Orison-specific inference. No
frame claims to reproduce one museum object exactly.

## Quantitative and production proof

- Focused catalog/prop suite: PASS 80/80.
- Production-live: PASS 16/16 — 18 unique units, every receiver off at boot,
  bounded audio, reachable Area3D interaction, no blocking body, five previous
  baked radios preserved, local switching, and byte-stable RealityState.
- InteractionInventory: exit 0.
- Player apartment 4B production performance at 2560 × 1440, pinned 64/16
  light/shadow budget: 11.11 ms average, wall 11.03 ms, PASS against 16.6 ms.
  The prior corrected checkpoint was 10.61 ms; the 0.50 ms movement remains
  inside budget but is stated rather than hidden.
- A/A controls are byte-identical.

## Honest limitations

These are low-cost procedural production props, not final museum replicas.
Finish-specific textures, cloth cords, printed scales, wear and apartment
context can improve in a later material pass. The gallery is a silhouette
proof; it does not claim the neutral inspection lighting is the apartment
lighting. The live suite—not this stage—owns the placement claim.
