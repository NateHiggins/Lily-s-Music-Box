# Lessons from the first pilot

The November 2026 exercise produced ten minutes at 1080p24 using 27 shot budgets,
170 selected dialogue turns and six 100-second captures. These are observations,
not required defaults. The owner enjoyed the exercise, requested this skill and
authorized disposal of generated media. Rough animation was not thereby accepted
as production game quality.

| Observation | Next decision |
|---|---|
| Identically named actors had different rigs/facial capability | Inspect installed imports before choosing the presentation driver. |
| Competing skeleton/face writers could overwrite film poses | Use one ordered pose pipeline and one face writer. |
| Staged hair went white and forearms vanished | Check material maps and visible-part bindings in native close-up. |
| Seated cloth fanned out and knees penetrated it | Test the hardest pose early; camera concealment is only a POC workaround. |
| Enabled brush playing still looked empty-handed | Judge silhouette, grip and actual contact in the intended shot. |
| Tripod/release cable obstructed the photo | Block props/camera/actors together and inspect the shutter frame. |
| The later photograph was hidden by the bar structure | Insert the actual captured image and verify the final cut boundaries. |
| Short-line ASR and contractions gave ambiguous mismatches | Inspect the actual words before replacing an otherwise usable take. |
| A late take swap risked stale mouth/timing data | Bind envelopes, duration and subtitles to the final selected WAV hash. |
| Sparse speech left long holds | Give silence purposeful action; preview dramatic pacing with voices. |
| Packet-hash comparison rejected remuxed footage | Make a fresh cheap concat or compare decoded images when necessary. |
| PCM silence acquired a tiny AAC tail | Measure codec residue separately; retain exact PCM and avoid needless retries. |
| Many intermediates survived the exercise | Preserve small sources/lessons, then retire authorized media with a manifest. |

Natural acting, phoneme lip-sync, instrument contact, stronger night lighting and
a continuous artistic viewing remained separate work. Carry that distinction
into the next production rather than treating technical completion as polish.
