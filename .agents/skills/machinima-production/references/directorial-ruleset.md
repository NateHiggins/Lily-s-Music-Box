# Lily's directing grammar

Adapted from Lily's supplied cinematography package; see the
[integration record](direction-review.md#integration-record). These creative
defaults complement [Neo-Noir Neon 1929](neo-noir-neon-1929.md). They do not
replace its adopted palette, lens targets, atmospheric depth or measured edit
targets. Other projects retain their own style.

## Build one legible dramatic change

**Credible baseline → precise discrepancy → practical consequence → emotional
truth.** A person has a practical task. Something impossible complicates it.
The person's attempt to cope reveals character. Avoid a montage merely
advertising strangeness. Tenderness, mortality and ridiculous inconvenience can
coexist without making the character wink at the audience.

For each shot choose a principal job: orient, reveal, contrast, reaction,
relationship, transition or interactive affordance. The beat, not the cut, is
the unit of direction. It needs an observable change and time to interpret it.
Several beats may live in one frame.

For comedy, stage setup, expectation, discrepancy, practical response and an
afterbeat. For dread, establish normality and let a small deviation register
before escalating. A friendly creature need not become threatening in behavior
to seem ominous from a restricted viewpoint. Do not alter its established nature.
Let warmth and vulnerability survive the joke; not every speaker trades quips.

## Camera and geography

Start with a locked frame. Move when recognition, changing distance, a gaze,
physical action or an offscreen sound requires it. Slow dolly/pushes give real
parallax; a zoom changes framing without moving the viewpoint. Embodied POV may
have restrained drift, but automatic bob or random jitter does not add humanity.

General lens references are 28 mm for environmental presence, 35 mm for a
tableau/two-shot, 50 mm for conversation and 85 mm for private detail. They are
neither FOV angles nor replacements for this game's adopted 18–24 mm wides and
135–200 mm compressed views. Record gate, aspect and camera distance; use the
[conversion guide](godot-direction.md#optics-with-explicit-units).

- Stage foreground, readable subject, consequence and negative space. Keep exits,
  barriers and important object locations understandable across shots.
- Establish the prop before isolating the hand. A close-up cannot repair geography.
- Preserve the line of action, eyelines, handedness and motion direction. An axis
  crossing needs a deliberate transition or re-establishing view.
- Symmetry can be comic or uncanny; canted and high/low views need a dramatic
  reason. Do not tilt the normal player camera to imitate film coverage.
- Keep the essential action sharp and visible. DOF, bloom, foreground occlusion
  and darkness must not erase a face, contact, clue or interaction affordance.
- Avoid arbitrary B-roll, redundant talking-head coverage and decorative orbits.
  A shot of a place should establish, contrast, anticipate or carry a sound bridge.

## Time and cuts

Let thought, material action and the selected audio set the duration. A pause
can be an unfinished gesture, a delayed glance, a mechanical process or a choice.
It should not be dead runtime padding.

A ten-second comedy exercise might orient for two seconds, establish expectation
for two, reveal the discrepancy over three, then hold response and afterbeat.
This is an exercise, not a duration rule. The package's 10/15-second generative
clip conventions do not impose cuts or chunk lengths on a native film.

Cut on a perceptual change, gaze, completed action, sound bridge or intentional
withholding. Preserve action through the cut. Use J/L cuts and graphic/motion
matches when their relationship contributes meaning. Fast editing alone cannot
convey believable speed; physical motion, scale, sound and contrast must support it.
For music videos, retain the approved full master and phrase timing.

## Economical acting is specific acting

- Let gaze lead a motivated head turn, then shoulders. Listening can finish the
  scene: a breath, hesitation, swallowed reply or smile losing certainty.
- Give weight a support foot/hip or a real seat. Verify reach, palm orientation,
  grip and release, cloth clearance and prop possession in native views.
- A contact has approach, alignment, touch, material response and departure.
  Target coordinates alone do not prove that the whole hand or object fits.
- Offset blinks, breaths and idle phases between people; avoid synchronized or
  metronomic loops. Use one ordered pose writer and one face writer.
- Speech responds to observation and current action. Pauses belong to the
  character's thought, not simply to gaps between sound files. Preserve authorized
  words, accent/voice identity and legitimate knowledge.
- Use the chosen audio for mouths. RMS motion is a disclosed POC approximation,
  not measured phonemes; silence and listening should not become talking motion.
- Preserve an authored unseen POV subject instead of adding a revealing reverse
  angle for conventional coverage. Let intimacy be mutual recognition, not mugging.

Realism here means intention and material causality. Stylized, pixel, contour or
rubber-hose actors can satisfy it without becoming photorealistic. Preserve their
shape language and discrete animation where appropriate. Do not add film blur to
hard-edged pixel gameplay just because this guide includes film vocabulary.

## Light, effects and sound have causes

Motivate light from the scene's actual windows, lamps, signs, stage fixtures and
sky. Keep the period's physical objects even when the game's expressive lighting
is contemporary. Generic examples from other projects—fluorescents, VHS or neon
domestic rooms—do not establish new Queens technology or history.

Choose a dominant source and controlled counter-colour. Let used wood, fabric,
skin and metal remain distinct. Weather, wetness, smoke and reflections retain
their existing owners. Render noise, streaking and missing surfaces are defects,
not intentional grain. Inspect the encoded proof as well as the native frame.

For a surreal event, isolate the impossible behavior and let its surrounding
material world behave normally. Show its cause, visible change and consequence;
particles do not substitute for a mechanism. Do not invent a new anomaly in a
canon scene merely because a calibration example contains one.

Stage room tone, contact/mechanism, speech/breath, offscreen cue and music with
explicit source, distance and timing. This is a planning order, not a rule to mix
ambience louder than dialogue. Quiet can be the afterbeat. An offscreen sound may
lead the next view. Do not add a laugh track or musical sting by default. A song
performance naturally keeps the approved song central; protected silent endings
remain silent.

## Contained exceptions and continuity

An explicitly requested parody may use rough television/VHS treatment, a crash
zoom, freeze frame or cheap transition. Tag its `stylistic_mode`, bound its start
and end, and restore the normal treatment. The effect supplies a narrator or
medium; it does not authorize anachronistic props in ordinary 1929 scenes.

Lock cast identity/voice/pronouns, wardrobe, fixed set layout, carried props,
date/weather, source lighting, screen direction and legitimate knowledge.
Record the event that changes any lock. Encode reusable facts in actual resources
and owner snapshots rather than repeating a generation prompt for every shot.
For replay and player control, use [interactive presentation](interactive-presentation.md).
