# Direction review and calibration

Apply the checks relevant to the requested deliverable. A shot-list request
produces a shot list; a skill update produces guidance. A finished film needs
finished-film review. Do not generate a calibration scene or broad infrastructure
unless it resolves a real uncertainty in the authorized work.

## Keep one usable production record

Extend the current edit/brief with creative intent, timed beats, continuity locks,
applicable interaction contract, real implementation owners and verification.
These can be sections of one artifact; six separate documents are unnecessary.
The [neutral manifest](../assets/shot-manifest.example.json) demonstrates the fields
without declaring a game scene or an installed parser.

| Review pass | Evidence and decision |
|---|---|
| Authority | Confirm current canon, actual scene/state owners, authorized lines, period, legitimate knowledge and protected beats. Proposals stay proposals. |
| Direction | Each shot has a job and subject; action, consequence and response have time to register; cuts, movement and geography have reasons. |
| Physical/optical | Inspect support, hands, contacts, mechanisms, possession, cloth, camera clearance and the intended focus/detail in native frames. |
| Performance/audio | Listen and view as capabilities allow: thought, gaze, breath, silence, vocal delivery, mouth timing, contact Foley and source distance. |
| Interaction | Where applicable, verify entry/exit, skip/cancel/replay/reload, ownership restoration and no duplicate or unearned state changes. |
| Delivery | Verify selected inputs, relevant variants, measured costs, final decode, seams, subtitles and ending; review continuous finished playback when possible. |

An attractive picture cannot excuse a softlock, duplicate transaction, unreadable
essential clue, broken mechanism or hidden failed contact. A wrapper's exit zero
is not a gameplay contract, and a numerical contact is not a full-hand visual
review. Keep original failures and source-bound evidence.

For editorial discussion, optionally score camera motivation, physical truth,
subtle acting, timing/silence, visual clarity, sound staging, emotional truth,
continuity, agency and recovery from 0 (broken) to 3 (excellent). Mark inapplicable
categories N/A; explain the weak items rather than chasing a total. The package's
24/30 suggestion is not an acceptance gate or Lily's approval.

Report implemented, technically verified, sampled visually, continuously
viewed/listened, actually played and artistically accepted as distinct states.
Don't claim a complete audiovisual watch from a contact sheet and full decode.

## Small studies when a proof is useful

- **Blank receipt, 12 seconds:** an unnamed adult receives one blank sheet from
  a mechanical printer, studies it, carefully places it in a tray and waits.
  The reaction is the joke. Use the manifest to test contact, quiet, continuity
  and recovery without adding dialogue, music, new canon or an essential clue.
- **Compassion in an institutional room:** a friend braces on a table, pauses
  and offers a restrained reassurance. Breath, posture and the listener carry
  intimacy. Preserve an unseen POV subject when that is the authored view.
- **A living drawing:** a contour figure shifts weight and studies an imaginary
  wristwatch. Support, hand intention and the held look make absence readable;
  photoreal shading is irrelevant.
- **Environmental vignette:** one small anomaly occurs within ordinary work
  while player control remains available. Treat the anomaly as an exercise;
  introducing it or granting a clue in gameplay needs the real scene authority.
- **Explicit parody:** a contained cheap-TV demonstration may use a crash zoom,
  tape seam and failed flourish. Restore the scene's normal treatment afterward.

The supplied prologue/time-connection and Tardi examples are lessons in physical
cause and restricted viewpoint, not free calibration material. Their exact
approved action/timing are protected by [the interactive guide](interactive-presentation.md#preserve-the-actual-prologue).
